- Self-serve with replies for one location: about $30 to $65 a month; a second group at $99 to $125.

- $19 is below every self-serve tool that clearly includes replies. $29/$59 matches Semrush Local. $39/$69 sits just above BrightLocal Grow and well under the $99 group.
