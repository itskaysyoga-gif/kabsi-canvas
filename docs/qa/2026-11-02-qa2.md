## What passed

- **Layout**: 270 visits per browser (54 pages and states, 5 widths) in Chromium and in WebKit: no sideways scroll, no page error from Kabsi's code, no stretched image; heights in WebKit within 10 percent of Chromium. The phone bar, Nora and the Founding 25 line cover nothing; the cookie bar on phones leaves the main button visible. Contact sheet: `contact-sheet-390.jpg`.
- **Accessibility**: axe (WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 A and AA) 0 violations on every public page and free check state at 390 and 1440 in both browsers, and on all 106 signed-in screens. Keyboard only: skip link first; header, Businesses menu and the phone menu open with Enter, close with Escape and return focus; the free check box works with arrows and Enter; every focused element shows a ring (L6 aside); an approval and its Undo work by keyboard (L7 aside).
- **Words**: no em dash, en dash, exclamation mark, Arabic or banned name (K-02) on any public page, in the 46 email previews (HTML and text), the 4 Auth templates or the WhatsApp templates. "spot" appears only inside a customer's quoted example review on a guide. Arabic exists only in the product's silent Arabic answers (say.ts), never shown publicly.
- **Links and tags**: all 41 internal links answer 200; the 404 answers 404; every page one H1, a canonical (noindex pages aside), a description; titles unique per URL. sitemap.xml, llms.txt and llms-full.txt on kabsi.co are byte-equal to the repo; robots.txt keeps /app, /start, /login, /staff, /scan/ out. validator.schema.org: 0 errors on Home, /faq, /pricing, /founding, /partners, /security, /how-it-works, /google-review-link, /scan, /for, /for/auto, /for/hair-salon, /for/barbershop, a guide and /guides.
- **Security**: HSTS (2 years, preload), X-Frame-Options DENY, nosniff, referrer and permissions policies on kabsi.co; `npm run check:anon` ok; the anon role has no grant on any public table (information_schema read-back: none) and 12 sensitive tables answer 401 to the publishable key.
- **Journeys (QA accounts, live)**: sign-in with an email code (minted code exchanged at Supabase Auth); /start for the new QA owner through "Is this your business?", "Who looks after its Google profile?", Manager access with the authorisation tick, invite pending (390 and 1440); approve a reply then Undo, approve and let it go, approve a post then Cancel (390, all pass), approve and Undo by keyboard (1440). Owner, manager and staff on all 16 signed-in screens at 390 and 1440: manager and staff see "This page is not part of your role" on owner screens; /staff refuses all three. Disconnect: the dialog ("Keep Kabsi", "Disconnect") opened and closed with Escape on the QA owner's fictional business; demo businesses have no Disconnect by design. Plan: the Plan page read only (no payment). The emails of these journeys (login code, confirm sign-up, welcome, Manager steps, access works, setup waiting, review ready, free check report, team invite, receipt, plan cancelled, disconnected, deletion) rendered at 390 and 1440: one yellow button each, no sideways scroll, footer and why-you-got-this present.
- **Lighthouse live** (phone, throttled, median of 3, run 41411573410): accessibility, best practices and SEO 100 on every page but /login (SEO 66, noindex on purpose).
