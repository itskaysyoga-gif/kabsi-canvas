# Kabsi final founder decision

Version 3, 24 November 2026. This replaces every earlier version. Nothing has been built, sent, bought or changed.

## Decision

Kabsi is a proactive Google management service for local businesses. It never contacts a business's customers. One plan at 29 US dollars a month. Run a paid pilot with 3 to 5 real businesses before any advertising.

My verdict: this is a real but thin product. It is worth the pilot because almost all of it is already built and the pilot costs close to nothing. It is not yet worth an advertising budget, and at this price paid ads probably never will be the way it grows.

## Founder override of 24 November 2026

Removed from scope: sending email, SMS or WhatsApp to customers; a separate email account, Amazon SES, Gmail sending; notice forwarding and parsing; customer contact storage, imports, appointment-sheet and voice extraction; suppression lists; any request delivery engine. No provider question about customer review emails will be sent. This may be reconsidered only if a simple, compliant, valuable method appears.

## Plain answers

**What Kabsi is.** A service that looks after a local business's presence on Google so the owner does not have to.

**What it does by itself, with no tap from the owner.**
- Hears of every new review within minutes and writes a reply in the owner's voice.
- Alerts the owner at once when a low rating or a delicate review arrives.
- Checks hours, phone, address, website, category and status on Google every day.
- Checks every day that the website, booking link and menu link load.
- Spots public holidays ahead and prepares the holiday hours.
- Prepares up to 2 posts a month from the business's own confirmed facts and the calendar.
- Reads Google's own performance figures and three nearby competitors' totals.
- Verifies that everything it published is live, and records it.
- Sends a short weekly receipt and a full monthly report.

**What needs the owner's tap.** Every reply, every correction to the listing, every post and photo, every hours change. Automatic publishing of safe replies stays off until Google confirms the consent wording and the safety tests, shadow mode and pilot have passed.

**What the owner does once.** Adds Kabsi as a manager on Google. Chooses email or WhatsApp. Optionally places the review link or counter card.

**Included in the plan.** Reply to every review. Low-rating alerts. Listing protection with one-tap corrections. Website and link checks. Google status alerts. Holiday hours. Up to 2 prepared posts a month. Photos from the owner's phone. A first-week profile check. Review link, QR code and NFC card support with tap counts, a ready message to send from the owner's own phone, and short guides for switching on review requests inside the systems the business already uses. Review patterns. Competitor totals. Google's performance figures. Weekly receipt and monthly report.

**Outside the plan.** Any message to customers. Ads, websites, ranking promises, bookings, customer records. The visibility map and AI answer checks stay switched off.

**Price.** 29 US dollars a month per location. 14 days free with a card. Cancel any time. Private founding offer: 19 for the first 12 months, first 25 businesses.

**Why 29 and not 19 or 39.**

| | 19 | 29 | 39 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Left after costs, per business per month | about 15.40 | about 24.80 | about 34.30 |
| Most Kabsi can afford to spend winning a customer | about 64 | about 103 | about 143 |
| Businesses needed for 5,000 a month | 264 | 173 | 129 |

Without automatic requests, Kabsi no longer competes with the 29 to 39 tools that send texts. Its nearest comparisons are reply tools at 10 to 44 and listing care at about 40. At 39 it would be the dearest option for what it now does. At 19 a customer is worth so little that nothing can be spent to win one. 29 is the highest price the remaining product can defend.

**What it costs us.** About 4.20 a month per business at normal use (3.50 to 9.20), all estimates. Fixed costs about 139 a month. Break-even is 6 businesses at 29, or 10 at the founding price.

**Why a business would pay monthly.** Reviews keep arriving and each needs an answer. Google changes listings without asking. Links break. Holidays come round. A profile with no recent post looks closed. None of it can be done once, and most owners do none of it.

**Why they would keep paying.** Only if they notice the work. The weekly receipt and monthly report exist for that. The honest risk is a quiet business: with 3 reviews a month and no incidents, the owner sees little and leaves. The pilot measures exactly this.

**Biggest threats.** Google's own AI reply suggestions inside Business Profile. Reply tools at 10 US dollars. The owner's "I will do it myself".

**What makes Kabsi different.** It does the whole job, including publishing and checking, and it comes to the owner on email or WhatsApp with the work prepared. It also protects the listing and links, which the cheap reply tools do not.

**What must be validated.** That owners understand it in one sentence. That they connect Google. That they approve work most weeks. That they pay a first, second and third month.

**Should we proceed.** Yes, to the pilot only. If fewer than 2 of the pilot businesses are still paying at day 90, stop selling the subscription.
