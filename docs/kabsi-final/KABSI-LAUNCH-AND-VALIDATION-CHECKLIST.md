# Kabsi launch and validation checklist

Version 3.1, 24 November 2026. Replaces every earlier version. The question: will a business understand Kabsi, connect Google, receive real work, and pay for three months.

## 1. Launch tests (GATE-2)

On Yawmiyati and two more authorized listings. Each line needs evidence. A fail blocks the pilot.

| # | Test | Pass when |

## 2. Blockers before the pilot

The backend restored and watched from outside (FIX-0). Paid AI key with a hard cap. Creem live with a written agreement on the account. Kapso Pro. The three build sessions merged and section 1 passed. Each needs Rashid's yes where it costs money or changes production.

## 3. The pilot

- **Who:** 3 to 5 real businesses that are not the founder's own, English-speaking, each with an active Google Business Profile that receives at least 4 reviews a month. Found through people Rashid knows. No cold outreach.
- **Terms:** the founding price, 19 US dollars a month, charged from day 1 after the trial. A paid pilot is the point: a free pilot proves nothing about willingness to pay.
- **Agreement:** one page: what Kabsi does, that nothing is published without their yes, that they can leave at any time, and that Kabsi may ask them three questions a month.
- **Length:** 90 days.
- **Kabsi's cost:** under 50 US dollars a month on top of fixed costs.

**What is measured**

| Measure | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Understood in one sentence | The owner can say what Kabsi does without help |
| Time from signup to first approved work | Under 24 hours |
| Share of drafted work approved within 48 hours | 70 percent |
| Weeks with at least one approval | 3 of every 4 |
| Reviews with a verified reply | 95 percent, median under 24 hours |
| Founder support time per business | Under 20 minutes a month |
| Paid month 1, 2 and 3 | See gates |
| What they say they would miss | Asked at day 30 and day 90 |
