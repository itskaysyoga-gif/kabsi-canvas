# Kabsi website and conversion blueprint

Version 3.1, 24 November 2026. Replaces every earlier version. Copy rules: no em dashes, no en dashes, no exclamation marks, no Arabic, plain words, design tokens only.

The Lovable prototype has not been reviewed: this session cannot open it. It will be used as the visual reference once access or screenshots are provided. Until then the "Kabsi landing page" canvas is the reference.

## 1. Positioning

- **Brand line:** Your business on Google. Taken care of.
- **One sentence:** Your Google business profile, managed for you. Approve work and make changes straight from WhatsApp or email.
- **For:** owner-run local businesses in English-speaking countries, the United States and United Kingdom first.
- **One action on every page:** the free check.
- **Never said:** AI agent, automation, API, dashboard, optimize, ranking, guaranteed, "we contact your customers".

## 2. The three benefits

1. **Every review answered.** A reply written in your voice for every review, and an alert the moment a bad one arrives.
2. **Your listing protected.** Hours, phone, website and booking link checked every day. One tap to fix what changed.
3. **Proof of the work.** A short summary every week and a full report every month.

One supporting line under them: "Also included: holiday hours, up to 2 posts a month, photos from your phone, and tools to get more reviews."

## 4. Pricing page

- One card: "29 US dollars a month per location."
- Terms beside every signup button and at checkout: "14 days free. Then 29 US dollars a month until you cancel. A card is needed to start. Cancel before day 14 and you pay nothing."
- Included: a reply to every review; alerts for bad reviews; your listing checked every day; website and booking link checked every day; holiday hours; up to 2 posts a month and photos from your phone; review link, QR code and counter card tools; a weekly summary and a monthly report.
- Not included, said once: Kabsi does not message your customers, run ads, build websites or promise rankings.
- The founding offer appears only on its private invitation page and at that checkout: "19 US dollars a month for your first 12 months. From month 13 the price is 29 US dollars a month. We email you 14 days before the change."
- "Google Business Profile is free from Google. Kabsi is an independent paid service."

## 5. Other pages

- **How it works:** connect Google; choose how you hear from Kabsi; your first day; a normal week; your monthly report.
- **Industry pages:** one template. Each changes only the headline noun, the example review and reply, and one sourced fact. Salons and barbers, auto care, cleaners, restaurants and cafes first.
- **Free check result:** rating and reviews beside 3 nearby businesses; listing basics; whether your links work now; the reply Kabsi would write for your latest review; what Kabsi would do each month; "Start 14 days free". No score, no fear wording.
- **Get reviews (signed in):** the link, QR, card, ready message, placement links and the short guides.
- Removed from navigation: Partners, AI answers, the visibility map.

## 6. FAQ

- **What does Kabsi do?** It answers your reviews, keeps your Google listing right, keeps your profile fresh, and shows you what it did.
- **Do I have to do anything?** Tap yes. Kabsi prepares the reply, the fix or the post, and you approve it on WhatsApp or email.
- **Can I ask Kabsi to change something?** Yes. Message it in plain words or send a voice note: your hours, a photo, a post, a reply. Kabsi shows you exactly what will change before anything goes to Google.
- **Does Kabsi contact my customers?** No, never. Kabsi gives you a review link, a QR code and a counter card to use yourself.
- **Does Kabsi need my Google password?** No. You add Kabsi as a manager and can remove it at any time.
- **What if a bad review comes in?** Kabsi tells you at once and writes a calm reply for you to approve or change.
- **Will this raise my ranking?** Kabsi does not promise rankings. It does the work that keeps a listing healthy.
- **Which countries?** Any English-speaking business with a Google Business Profile.
- **How do I cancel?** One tap in Settings, or tell Kabsi on WhatsApp.
- **Is Kabsi part of Google?** No. Kabsi is independent.

## 7. Conversion rules

- One primary action per page, repeated top, middle and bottom.
- Phone first at 390 px.
- No capability appears on the production site until its launch-test line has passed. Automatic replies are not mentioned until they are live.
- Events: `landing_viewed`, `free_scan_started`, `free_scan_completed`, `signup_started`, `signup_completed`, `google_access_granted`, `first_yes`, plus new `subscription_started`.

## 8. Growth without an ad budget

At 29 US dollars a customer is worth about 310, so about 103 is the most that can be spent to win one. The ad forecast is about 250. Paid ads are therefore a small probe, not the plan.

1. **The pilot** (launch checklist).
2. **Free tools that already exist:** the review link and QR generator and the free check, each ending in "Start 14 days free".
3. **Guides:** the 27 published guides, plus one a week chosen from real search queries.
4. **Referrals:** a free month for each referred business that pays (existing mechanism).
5. **Ad probe:** 400 US dollars, only after the pilot's day 30 gate and only with Rashid's yes.
