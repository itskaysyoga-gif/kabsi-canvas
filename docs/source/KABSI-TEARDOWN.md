# Competitor teardown (master plan 30.7 step 1), lane R chat, 25 Oct 2026

Read on 25 Oct: the homepages of Localo, Semrush Local, BrightLocal, Podium, Local Falcon and Birdeye. For clarity, trust and conversion only. No copying.

## What each says in 5 seconds
- Localo: "Local SEO tools" (names the category, not the outcome). 14 days free, no card. 13 tool cards, no prices on the homepage.
- Semrush Local: "Drive more foot traffic". 4 to 5 competing calls to action, a very heavy menu, jargon (GBP, listings).
- BrightLocal: "Shine bright, locally" (a brand line that says nothing). Strong review-site scores, 3 wordings for the button, prices hidden, no product picture in the hero.
- Podium: "AI Employee for local businesses". Demo only, no prices, numbers with no method behind them.
- Local Falcon: "AI Visibility and Local Rank Tracker". The map grid is the product. Many acronyms, prices hidden, the free credits appear only at the bottom.
- Birdeye: "AI coworkers for multi-location brands". Demo and enterprise pricing, logos only above the fold, a free AI visibility check hidden in the header.

## The pattern
Nobody shows a price on the homepage. Nobody shows the product working in the hero. Most say the category instead of the result. Most have more than one main action. Proof is logos and stars, never the owner's own data. All are built for marketers and agencies, not the owner at the counter.

## What Kabsi does better (decisions for Web 2)
1. The hero shows the result in 5 seconds: the owner's own map, and the WhatsApp message that delivers it.
2. The free scan is the main action, above the fold, one yellow button. No demo call needed.
3. The price is on the homepage: "Free for 14 days. No card. Cancel in WhatsApp." and from $19 a month.
4. Plain words only. No acronyms, no "AI coworkers", no "local SEO" in the hero.
5. Honest proof: every example labelled Example, no invented counts, every number sourced and dated.
6. A map anyone can read: a colour-blind-safe scale, the rank printed in every pin, the legend in words.
7. One action per screen, everywhere: site, app, email, WhatsApp.
8. Phone first, in Meta's in-app browser, under 2.5 s to the hero.
