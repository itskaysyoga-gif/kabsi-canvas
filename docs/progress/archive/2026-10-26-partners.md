### Founding 25
- `founding_applications` (RLS on, no browser grant). Written only by `programs` (`POST /functions/v1/programs/founding`): Turnstile, 5 an hour per visitor, 300 a day in all, then the service-role-only `founding_apply`. One open application per WhatsApp number. Staff hear of each one on WhatsApp.
- Staff page, Founding 25 panel: accept, waitlist, decline (`staff_founding_decide`, advisory lock, 25 hard cap, the 10/5/5/5 mix, any trade 30 days after the first application); link an accepted place to its business (`staff_founding_link`).
- The founding flag: `private.founding_days(org)` = 90 for an accepted, linked place. `private.start_trial` uses it (90 days from working Google access, no Creem); a trial already running is set to 90 days from its start. `private.entitlements` drops the trial caps (100 drafts, 10 backlog drafts) for it and says `founding`; `private.trial_take` drops the 3 profile fixes.
- Real places left: `GET /functions/v1/programs/founding` (`founding_places()`, cached 5 minutes).
- Messages: `private.founding_tick()` daily (cron `kabsi_programs_daily`, 06:23 UTC) queues `founding_message` on days 60 to 64, 85 to 88, 90 to 92, once each (`day*_sent_at`); none once the business pays. WhatsApp through the outbox (billing_notice template first outside 24 hours) and email (billing category). Day 90: "Choose a plan" on the Plan page through Creem; nothing charged by itself. The trial's day-7 and day-11 emails are skipped for founding businesses.
- Founding price: `foundingCheck` marks the subscription `founding` when a Founding 25 business pays (any mode).
- Day-45 kill gate on the staff page: program day, gate date, businesses that approved work in the last 7 days (audit log, action approval), weeks with an approval per business.

### Kabsi Partners
- `partners`, `partner_referrals`, `partner_commissions`, `partner_payouts` (RLS on, no browser grant).
- Apply: `POST /functions/v1/programs/partners` (Turnstile, rate limits, terms version 2026-10-26; a declined applicant may apply again after 90 days). Founding Partner places: `GET /functions/v1/programs/partners`.
- Approve: `staff_partner_decide` gives the handle (no brand or Google words) and the code `P-HANDLE`; the first 20 are Founding Partners. Payout details: `staff_partner_payout_details` stores them in Vault, the row keeps the method and the last 4.
- Attribution: trigger on `locations.via_code` (Growth 1 saves it at sign-up), plus a daily sweep for the owner's WhatsApp partner code within 60 days before sign-up. Last before sign-up wins; one referral per business, never changed. When both a via code and a WhatsApp code exist, the later touch wins. The 30-day trial is decided when the trial starts (`private.partner_trial_days`, an active referral that is not a self referral by then). Self referral (the partner's email or WhatsApp is an owner's or a team member's, or the business shares a Google place with a business the partner is a member of) is marked and earns nothing; checked again at trial start and at every payment.
- Ledger: trigger `billing_events_partner_ledger` when the job sets `processed_at`. `subscription.paid`: 25 percent of the amount before tax (`transaction.amount` or `last_transaction.amount`; else order `sub_total` less discount; else the product price), held 30 days. `refund.created`, `dispute.created`: an unpaid line is voided in full; a partial refund or one after payout adds a reversal line that comes off the next payout; later refunds take back only what is left of the line (never below zero). The line is locked while it is reversed. Live mode is read from Creem's `mode` (`live`, `prod` or `production`).
- Payout run `private.partner_payout_run` on the 5th (one run at a time; it locks each partner's cleared lines and pays exactly those; it refuses a future date) (cron `kabsi_partner_payout_run`, 06:17 UTC): lines past their hold clear; approved partners with 25 USD or more cleared in live mode get one payout with a JSON statement (date, trade, area, base, commission; never the business name); below that it rolls over; staff hear of it on WhatsApp. Staff approve the month's list and mark each payout sent with its reference (needs payout details). Staff can run it by hand.
- Partner terms (/partners/terms, updated 26 Oct): revenue collected excluding tax, refunds and chargebacks, while the subscription stays paid; 25 USD minimum; a statement; competing referrals (last before sign-up, the earlier partner earns nothing); self referral including WhatsApp and businesses the partner owns or runs, checked at every payment.

## Independent review (fresh agent, diff and rules only)
Eight findings, all fixed in this PR:
1. Blocking: the Google place self-referral check depended on a column nothing set. Now it also matches the places of any business the partner is a member of (by email or WhatsApp), and team members, not only owners.
2. Payout run race with a refund: lines are locked and the payout counts exactly the locked lines; the refund path locks the line too.
3. Partial refund then full chargeback took back too much: reversals now take only what is left, commission included (test added).
4. `staff_payout_run` with a future date cleared held lines early: refused (test added).
5. A valid via code always beat a later WhatsApp code: the later touch now wins.
6. A failing ledger trigger was silent: `applyEvent` now throws on the final update so the job retries.
7. Founding 25 payments used up first-30 places: excluded from the count.
8. The 30-day partner trial was given at sign-up before the self check could see the owners: now decided when the trial starts.
Also: amounts are read with a safe integer cast (`private.cents`), so an odd value never breaks the update.
