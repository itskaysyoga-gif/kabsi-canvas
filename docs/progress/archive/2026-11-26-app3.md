## Decisions to confirm

- An undated draft belongs to the month it was made (same rule as the Posts page's `monthOf`), so Yawmiyati's 25 Oct "A Google post is ready for you" expired with October.
- A question asked early for next month counts its 14 days from the first day of that month, so December's question stays until 15 Dec.
- Cancelling a plan paid before Creem is an email to hello@kabsi.co (no Creem subscription to cancel); the card says it does not renew by itself.
- The CSP report-uri is removed rather than moved: the reports were third-party noise and the error quota is shared by the whole Sentry organisation.
- Two tests changed with the behaviour, listed for lane R: `tests/coach.test.ts` (the sentence is gone; now checks no identity attribute is offered) and `tests/security-headers.test.ts` (report-uri must be absent); the e2e Early answers test now checks the section is gone.
