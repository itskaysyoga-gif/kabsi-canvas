## Found by the property test in CI (first CI run, seed -1466452477)

The owner approves a reply, the draft gets a new version inside the 10 seconds (the review goes back to a draft), then the owner presses Yes on WhatsApp for the new words. `approve_publication` handed back the first, stale publication (the old words) as "approved"; it then failed at the claim ("This reply changed after it was approved"), so the new words were never sent although the owner was told they were. Nothing wrong reached Google, but a yes was lost. Fixed in `supabase/migrations/20261027151630_sec1_approval_superseded.sql` (applied, version 20261027151630, read back): a publication still waiting on an item that is back to a draft is cancelled (`superseded`) and the new yes makes its own. Reproduced with the CI seed before the fix, passing after it; three more seeds of 600 runs pass; `sec1_roles_approvals.sql` holds the exact sequence; full suite Files=62, Tests=3496, PASS.
