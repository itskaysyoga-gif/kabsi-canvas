## What was built

1. kabsi.co/partners/join: the one sign-in panel (WhatsApp, Google, email code), then one short form (name, email, six kinds, country, how they share, the UAE permit line or "I will only share privately", the terms with version 2026-11-10.2). `public.partner_join` makes the account at once, status approved, code P- and 6 card letters. 2. kabsi.co/partner: link, QR, numbers from the one ledger (`public.partner_home`: visits, trials, paying, earned, pending, next payout; test mode apart), referred businesses as trade and area only, payout method asked once 50 US dollars has cleared (or a payout is on the way), stored in Vault, shown as the last 4, the rules. Visits come from a beacon on `?via=P-...` (once per visit, rate-limited per visitor and code a day).
3. Payout method change: `partner_payout_request` puts the detail in Vault at once and waits; with a confirmed WhatsApp number the partner taps "Yes, it's me" (button `pc:<id>`, the seam in agent.ts, checked against the phone asked and still the account's); with the window closed the partner is asked to message Kabsi first; without WhatsApp a 6-digit code goes to the account's sign-in email (5 tries, 10 minutes).
4. Review at the money: a first payout of a never-reviewed partner waits for one staff tap ("payouts" from the staff phone, button "Approve P-XXXXXX"); the month's approval on the staff page skips it; the tap refuses the partner's own phone, a paused or left partner and a payout with no method; the first 20 reviewed are Founding Partners. Staff notice on join. The monthly alert names the first payouts.
5. Attribution: proved in `supabase/tests/partners2.sql` with Creem test-mode events (visit, sign-up, 30 day trial, test payment, one 975 line held 30 days, refund voids it); self referral by account, email, phone and Google place earns nothing; last link before sign-up wins (including an owner link against a later WhatsApp partner code) and never changes; lines only when card payments are open (`billing_open` on, or a `billing_test` organisation in test mode).
6. Owners: "my link" on WhatsApp returns the owner's referral link R-XXXXXX (look 2, list marker) or, for an owner who is a partner, the partner link; one free month per referred business that pays (live payments only, recorded in `owner_referral_rewards`); one source per business, with a lock per organisation.
7. Creem test mode: the setup action was run (request 85996, HTTP 200): essentials_month, essentials_year, pro_month and pro_year renamed true ("launch price" names from `CATALOGUE`); the six others unchanged; nothing created; nothing missing.
8. R-155: `rowDescription` shortens a long service at a word boundary so the day and time stay. Test with a 60 character service; two older look tests updated to the new rule (they asserted the old drop-the-time behaviour).
9. Facts 01 and 08 rebuilt with `node scripts/build-kb.mjs`; Nora's reference answer en12 says join; the WhatsApp "partner" reply says join.
