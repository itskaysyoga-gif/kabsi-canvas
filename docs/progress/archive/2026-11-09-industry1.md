## The method

`scripts/lib/page-dup.mjs` (used by `scripts/industry-dup.mjs`, `tests/industry1-dup.test.ts` and the public e2e suite): the HTML of each page's `<main>`, scripts and styles removed, split at every tag, then into sentences at ". ", "? " and "! "; lower case; each sentence once per page; a chunk with no letter or digit (a lone "/" or bracket) is not a sentence. A pair's share is its identical sentences over the sentences of the shorter page (limit 35 percent).

Own text: SEO 1 called the 104 sentences on every page the template. R-144 asks for each page to be at least 70 percent its own text; the test reads that strictly: any sentence found on half the industry pages or more (11 of 21) counts as template, so own text is the rest. The strictest reading, sentences on no other page at all, is reported beside it (63.8 to 67.6 percent): it cannot reach 70 percent while every page links six related businesses and three guides by name, because those names and titles are on other pages by design. Lane R to confirm the reading (decision to confirm below).

This session's script reads 114 template sentences before, where SEO 1 read 104: it counts the free check box and the numbers of the numbered markers the same way on every page; both numbers describe the same pages, and both "before" columns here come from this script on a build of main, so before and after compare like with like.

Run it: `npm run build`, `PORT=4173 node .output/server/index.mjs &`, then `node scripts/industry-dup.mjs http://localhost:4173` (add `--table` for every pair). It exits 1 when a page is under 70 percent or a pair above 35.

- The rest (hero, one H1, the watch Example, the jobs, the guides, the related businesses, the closing free check) is as before. Design 9 and the tokens only.

Three pages' guide picks changed so close trades share fewer (florist, lash and brow studio, yoga and pilates studio). Every new line has its row in the "Industry 1" section of its claim sheet (645 rows); COPY.md section 72 lists the template and every page's new lines; facts 09 has the new order.
