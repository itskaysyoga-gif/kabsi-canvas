## Home LCP fix (31 Oct 2026)

Found in the after-merge check: on live Home at 390 px under Lighthouse's phone throttling (150 ms, 1.6 Mbps, 4x CPU), LCP was 5.4 to 5.6 s in 3 of 5 runs (1.2 s in the other 2). A MutationObserver showed the hero section removed at about 5.3 s: Home sat behind `React.lazy` and `Suspense`, and when the shell updated before the lazy chunk arrived, React threw the server HTML away and drew it again, so for a moment the footer's brand line was the largest thing on screen. The headline's entrance fade (from opacity 0) also kept it out of LCP, so the cookie bar became the LCP once the wipe was gone.

What changed: `src/routes/index.tsx` imports `D6Home` directly (the router already splits each route into its own chunk: in the build the Home text is in a 28 KB route chunk and the shared entry grew 0.6 KB, from 392,510 to 393,125 bytes); the hero h1 paints with no entrance animation (the lead line, the free check box and the rest keep theirs). `tests/performance.test.ts`: the check that asked for the lazy import now asks for the route's own chunk, no lazy boundary and no `autoCodeSplitting: false` (listed for lane R as a changed test; same intent, plus the regression).

Checks: typecheck pass; lint:changed (no lintable files changed); `npm test` 80 files, 691 pass; build pass; check:tokens ok; against a local production build: `home-demo.spec.ts` 8 passed, `public.spec.ts --grep "pages /$"` 2 passed with the Home baseline unchanged (no baseline updated), `smoke.spec.ts --project=m390` 13 passed. LCP on the local build (no compression) at 390 px throttled, three runs: 2.10, 2.08, 2.08 s, the h1, nothing removed (before: 7.0 s, the footer line, the hero removed every run).
