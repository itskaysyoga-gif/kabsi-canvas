## What was built

- One router for every kind of person (29.7): `phone_roles` (RLS: a person reads only their own rows, staff read all, no browser writes), roles unknown, prospect, owner, manager, staff, partner and staff_admin, each with its own tools (`roles.ts`) and Nora voice (`say.ts`). A phone with two roles is asked "Your business or your partner tools?" (or "Your business or Kabsi staff tools?") once; every reply to a bound phone is headed with the role and business it acts for. Business roles count only while the membership exists and the opt-in is active, so removing a member stops their phone at once (trigger on `location_members`).
- 9.1: the webhook checks Kapso's signature (secret from Supabase Vault, or `KAPSO_WEBHOOK_SECRET`), stores each message once by WhatsApp id, answers Kapso at once and does the work in the background (`EdgeRuntime.waitUntil`); a message whose first try did not finish is tried once more by the tick. Buttons resolve by the id inside them with no AI call. Free text goes to the intent router: one Haiku call that picks a tool from the role's own list (`intent.ts`, the text fenced as untrusted); "On it" goes first when it takes longer than 2.5 seconds.
- 9.2: binding by the one-time code from Connect WhatsApp ("KABSI 123456") and by the staff code from the staff page ("STAFF ABCD2345", 15 minutes, once); one conversation state per phone (active role and business, open question, language, last review, window, STOP, hand-off, scan and partner codes, the Click-to-WhatsApp referral, first and last touch); several businesses ("Which business?" as buttons up to 3, a numbered list above).
- 9.3: stats, this month, review link, QR, Approve and Undo, typed "yes" ("Which one?" with the business on each button when two wait), exact reply shown back for a Yes, "make it shorter" through the existing reply pipeline, photos (image and document) to Photos on Pro, video kept, "Ask a customer" contacts in E.164, talk to a person (Kapso inbox; free text then waits a day for the person), STOP and START in English, Spanish, French and Arabic, abuse (one notice, then silence). Hours and typed posts point to the app for now (see Found, not done). Billing answers "Billing is coming soon, reply 'talk to a person' for help" (session 10 fills it).
- 9.4 and 9.5: the one sender (`outbox.ts`, `policy.ts`, `whatsapp_outbox`): replies at once inside the window and not counted; business-started messages at most 6 a day, batched within 15 minutes, several waiting become one digest (WhatsApp charges service conversations after 1,000 a month per number), quiet hours 21:00 to 08:00 local except urgent ones, quality Medium or worse means digests only, outside the window only the utility template (the full message follows the reply), live only to `whatsapp_live_phones` and staff phones until `whatsapp_owners_on`. The existing review, post and Protection alerts now go through it (identity-field changes urgent).
- Templates (`templates.ts`): 9 utility templates, named parameters with examples, a "Show me" quick reply, no links. Submitted through a staff action.
- Staff actions inside the function (`kapso-admin.ts`), run with `x-cron-secret` only, so the Kapso key never leaves Supabase secrets: `setup` (registers the webhook, stores the secret in Vault, checks automations), `templates`, `health` (hourly by pg_cron; a drop alerts by email to hello@kabsi.co), `automations`. Answers hold ids, names and statuses only.
- 18.2: session 19's `google_needs_action` task (evidence.action suspended, verify, ownership or no_voice) sends the owner and managers a message at once, quiet hours or not, with plain steps and Google's own help page (opened 24 and 25 Oct: answers 4569145, 7107242, 3038177, 4566671), no promise and no appeal; the staff phone at the same time; "cleared" when Google clears it. A trigger on `tasks` wakes the function; the tick sweeps anything missed.
- 18.3: photo path tests on real files (JPEG as image; HEIC, PNG, WebP as documents; unreadable answered "Please send it as a photo.").
- 24.3, 24.8, 28.1: `scanHandoffText` and `scanHandoffHref` in `src/lib/site.ts` ("Hi Kabsi, my scan code is KAB-..."), Nora picks up the code, the greeting when it is missing, "Send the full map to my WhatsApp" answered (map image stub until V1).
