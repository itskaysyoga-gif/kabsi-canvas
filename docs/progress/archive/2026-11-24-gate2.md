## Independent review (a fresh agent given only the diff and the rules)
Confirmed and fixed: (1) the founding notice was stamped before it was sent, so a failed send or a business with no contact could still be switched to 29 with no notice: it is now stamped after the sends (the outbox and email keys keep a retry to one message), and no contact means no stamp, no switch and a staff alert; (2) a late notice was followed by the switch within days: `foundingChangeDate` moves the change one renewal later when the notice is under 13 days before the end, and the notice names that date; (3) owners already on the old 90-day Founding 25 trial would have got the trial caps: `private.founding_days` keeps 90 for places linked before GATE-2; (6) the trial screen showed only 29: it now also shows FOUNDING_LINE for invited owners.
Not changed: (4) the switch is a job, not the webhook; it writes only its own step times and asks Creem for the change, and the paid state still comes from the webhook. (5) A renewal clamped to an earlier day of the month can leave a 13th payment at 19 (an undercharge, never a double charge). (7) The first-day list replaces any other open question on that phone for 48 hours (LATER).
