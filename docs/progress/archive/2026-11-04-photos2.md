Why: on 4 Nov Rashid added a phone screenshot (1103 by 2400, a rating form) to Yawmiyati from kabsi.co. The check passed it, it went live on Google (publication 9138b98e, verified 06:10 UTC, media name ending `.../media/CIABIhDya...`), and there was no way to take it back. ## Behaviour

1. Remove from Google (a photo Kabsi put on Google)
   - On the photo's card in "Sent to Google", once it is live: "Remove from Google", then the confirm step ("Remove this photo from your Google profile? You have 10 seconds to undo." with "Remove from Google" and "Keep it"), then the Approved line with Undo.
   - New publication kind `photo_removal`, approved like a photo (`approval_kind` = photo): the owner, or a manager the owner lets approve photos. Dashboard only (`not_on_whatsapp`, `not_on_this_channel`). Staff are refused by the content function. The 10 second undo, one claim, the stop switch (gbp), the audit (the content function's audited approval), the plan check, all as for the post removal.
   - The payload's `media_ref` is read in `approve_publication` from the photo's latest verified `photo` publication of the same business, and must be `accounts/*/locations/<this business's google_location_id>/media/*` (else `photo_not_on_google`). The publish job checks the name again (NotSent), and `removeMedia` refuses any other shape before Google is called. Kabsi never deletes a photo it did not upload.
   - One `DELETE v4/{name}`. A 404 is "already gone" and counts as removed (verified), not an error. Then Kabsi reads the item by name (404 is gone); if that read fails another way, the first page of the photo list decides; more pages and not found is "pending".
   - States on the card: Removing ("Kabsi asked Google to remove it and checks until it is gone."), Removed from Google ("Google no longer shows this photo."), Did not go through (the failed line with "photo removal"; the photo stays posted with its button).
   - The WhatsApp outcome lines (`publication_outcomes_due`, `v4_whatsapp_due`) leave a photo removal out, like a post removal.
   - Retention: a removed photo's files go 30 days after removal; the new small copy is deleted with the other files.
2. Screenshot note (`_shared/photos/screenshot.ts`, no AI): no camera data (EXIF Make or Model in JPEG APP1, HEIC Exif, PNG eXIf, WebP EXIF) and a phone screen's shape (long side 1.95 to 2.4 times the short), or a PNG at a listed phone screen size. The note: "This looks like a screenshot. Google asks for photos of your business itself: the outside, inside, your team, your work." It sets the `screenshot` flag, so `suitable` is false: Approve all leaves it out, "Approve anyway" still works.
3. Kind picker: radio choices with one line of help each (COPY.md 43), nothing chosen unless the owner chose before; "Choose what the photo shows first." if Approve is pressed without one.
4. Photo card: object-contain on the surface colour, a broken link shows the plain frame; the track shows Approved, Sent, Live under 22rem (container query) with full names for screen readers (shared `TaskTrack`, so posts and reviews get the same fix); one card per photo being checked: "Checking your photo. About a minute."; tapping a photo opens it full size in a sheet.
5. Speed: the engine writes `<location>/<id>_s.jpg` (480 px, JPEG 75) and records `thumb_path`; the list signs the small copy (1 hour), the full file is signed when opened (5 minutes). Photos checked before 4 Nov have no small copy (LATER row).
