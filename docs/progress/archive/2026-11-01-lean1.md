What the 631 KB on Home is (a Lighthouse run from the build session, same page): the main bundle 127 KB, the Meta Pixel (`fbevents.js` 115 KB and its config 93 KB), PostHog (`array.js` 106 KB, dead-clicks 9 KB, web-vitals 7 KB), then first-party chunks: zod 25 KB, the twelve industry texts 23 KB, tailwind-merge 15 KB, the guide texts 10 KB, Speed Insights 5 KB. Framework floor: `react-dom` 202 KB raw, the router 51 KB, seroval 24 KB inside a 123 KB (compressed) main bundle.

## Already in place (checked, nothing to do)

- Hashed files under `/assets/` carry `cache-control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` and are served with brotli on Vercel (`curl -I https://kabsi.co/assets/...`).

- Images: WebP at 800 and 1600 px with width and height on `Img`; the Home hero photo is preloaded from 768 px.

## Decisions to confirm (lane R)
