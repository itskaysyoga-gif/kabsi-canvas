## 2. What changed

| Part | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| a. Switch | `whatsapp/agent.ts` (`switchCmd`, `switchBusiness`, the role pick), `whatsapp/roles.ts` | The role question asked by "switch" is marked; "My business" then shows the numbered list (or buttons for 2 or 3) at once, with no business active until one is picked. One business: "Done. You are now working on your business." as before |
| b. Old questions | `whatsapp/agent.ts` (`run`, the role pick, `pickedBusiness`) | A role or business question is closed by any message that is not its answer (a new request, a word, a photo); the held message is dropped and the new one handled. A role pick or a business pick now closes only its own kind of question, so an old "My business" or business button never closes a waiting proposal |
| d. Timeout | `whatsapp/agent.ts` (`PICK_OPEN_MS`), `whatsapp/roles.ts` | Both questions carry the time they were asked and close after 10 minutes; one saved before this change (no time) is closed at the next message |
| c. Typed Save | `whatsapp/agent.ts` (`bookSeam`, `PROPOSAL_WORDS`) | While a proposal waits: Save, save it, save this (also typed as a reply that quotes the proposal) works like the Save button; Change, change it like the Change button. Yes and ok already saved (RB-E). Forwarded words never save. Anywhere else the words keep their meaning |
| e. Names | `request-book/extract.ts` (`nameCase`), `whatsapp/book.ts` (`createInput`) | A name word written all in lower case gets a capital first letter (also after a hyphen or apostrophe); a word with a capital already, and a script with no capitals, is left as typed. Shown in the proposal and saved |
| e. Times | `request-book/replies/words.ts` (`windowLabel`, `asWritten`), `request-book/extract.ts`, `request-book/replies/index.ts`, `whatsapp/book.ts` | A day and part of the day the date reader understood, with no hour: "Thursday afternoon, no time set" (Today, Tomorrow, the weekday within 6 days, else the date), shown and saved in that form. Words it did not understand: "When: not clear, you wrote ..." and "Which day and time did you mean? Send it again, for example Thursday afternoon. Or tap Save to keep the words as written, with no time set."; saved as `"..." (as written)` so no list shows them as read |
