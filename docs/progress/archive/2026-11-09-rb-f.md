## The ease test for a salon worker (R-141: run in RB-K's first session, with these pass marks)

One page, for the person who keeps the salon's bookings today. About 15 minutes. Use fictional clients only. The tester says one sentence at the start and nothing after: "Use Kabsi on WhatsApp to keep track of these requests the way you would at work." Time each task from the moment the card is read to the last reply.

| Task (read from a card) | What they do | Pass mark |
|---|---|---|
| 1. A client asks for highlights on Thursday afternoon | Type it, tap Save | Under 30 seconds to "Appointment N saved" |
| 2. Which appointments still need a confirmation? | Type the question | Answer read under 10 seconds, and faster than their current way (time that too: their book, chat or calendar) |
| 3. The client confirmed Thursday at 3 | Type it, tap the time | Under 10 seconds to the updated line |
| 4. The client came in | Type it (answer the one question if asked) | Under 10 seconds |
| 5. Find Sarah's appointment | Type her name | Read under 10 seconds, faster than their current way |
| 6. It was a trim, not highlights | Type the correction | Under 10 seconds, and they read the before and after correctly |

Overall passes only when: no help is given after the first sentence; every task meets its mark; and asked "Would you use this instead of what you do now?", they say yes without the tester prompting. A miss is written down with the exact words they typed and is fixed before day 1 of the scorecard.
