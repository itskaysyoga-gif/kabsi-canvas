## Independent review (a fresh agent, given only the diff and the rules)

Confirmed and fixed here, each with a test (`book-f.test.ts`, the "review:" cases):
1. High: a name matched part of a customer's name ("im done" could change Ibrahim's job, "Sam came in" Samira's). Names now match whole words only (`nameFits`), common words are never names, and "Sarah's mum is here" is not about Sarah.
2. High: an older Yes, No or time button for the same request could answer a newer question. Each question now carries a nonce in its buttons, and a tap after 30 minutes is refused.
3. High: "not now, thanks" and "not sure, maybe later" were caught as corrections. Without a number, a correction now applies only when the words are on a request touched in the last 30 minutes; otherwise the message goes on as before.
4. Medium: a second typed "undo" could void the same request instead of walking back the next change, and could reverse another member's change. `request_recent` now skips Undo activities and undone changes, and returns the latest activity only when it is the person's own.
5. Medium: "3 confirmed Thursday 3" on a job set the time before the confirmation was refused. The kind and status are checked before any write.
6. Medium: "what's new", "open" and "open today" became lists. Only "what's", "anything" or "show me" with "open", and the everyday status words, are list questions now.
7. Medium: a combined reply with a question lost the Undo of its applied parts, and a partly failed Undo reported only the last row. Undo now sits next to Yes and No, and every row undone, plus what was not, is listed.
8. Medium: tapped buttons had no time limit, and a tapped time applied to a request changed since. Both are refused now (`rb_time` holds the version).
9. Medium: dropping readings already past could apply a bare hour with no tap. One reading left after the hour was in doubt is now a one-tap guess; "Monday 6pm" said on a Monday evening (only the day in doubt) applies; more than 3 readings keep the nearest day.
10. Low: "Sarah confirmed she will pay" was caught. A confirmation with no day or hour, or with no open appointment by that name, now goes on as before.
11. Low: "Ali called" read as staff contacted Ali. "called", "messaged" and "replied" were removed from the everyday words ("contacted" and "called back" stay).

Not fixed, in `docs/LATER.md`: "who asked about" filters after the database's 15 rows, so with many requests it can miss older enquiries. A leftover request book question after the book is switched off still gives a forwarded Yes the forwarded line (harmless: a forwarded Yes never approves anything). Selecting the owner by the location name was kept as the task asked.

## Messages per request (item 7)

`whatsapp_message_pricing` fills from the first delivered message after this deploy. After the script, the read (select only, no phone printed):

    select o.location_id, count(*) as delivered, count(*) filter (where p.billable) as billable, string_agg(distinct p.category, ',') as categories
      from public.whatsapp_outbox o join public.whatsapp_message_pricing p on p.provider_id = o.provider_id
     where o.location_id::text like 'e5e00000-%' and o.created_at > now() - interval '1 day'
     group by o.location_id;

Divide by the requests made in each test business. The design is 4 or 5 Kabsi messages per request. Inside the 24-hour window that the owner's own message opens, Meta's service replies are expected as category service and not billable.
