## What was built

- Link watch: `_shared/link-watch/` (`link-watch.ts` pure, `job.ts` wiring). Website, booking and menu links read fresh from the listing (`listingLinks` in the Google layer, mock returns none), checked through the existing safe fetch with a 10 second limit. Three failures in a row (now, 30 minutes, 2 hours) make one task (kind `profile_gap`, dedupe key `link_watch:<kind>`, written by `public.link_watch_event`); a recovery closes it and is logged. State is counts and times only (`public.link_watch`), never an address. Job `link_watch`, offered daily by pg_cron `kabsi_link_watch` (05:17 UTC).
- Weekly summary: `receiptText` is 3 to 5 fact lines (week, first 3 kinds of work, found changes, link taps) then waiting and one next step. The weekly build asks Google for nothing now (no activity, no search terms, no AI or competitor history). The weekly email drops Google figures, AI, competitor and "Checked N times". Taps by place come from `link_taps` (cards and placement links).
- Monthly report: `_shared/monthly-report/` (`compose.ts`, `feedback.ts`, `competitors.ts`, `profile-health.ts`, `api.ts`, `build.ts`, `sample.ts`), email `email/monthly-body.ts` and `copy.monthlyReport`, table `public.monthly_reports`, job `monthly_report` offered hourly by `kabsi_monthly_report` (the 1st from 08:00 local, catch-up to the 3rd). Bands: Work done, Results, What is next, plus recurring feedback (3 or more reviews of the last 90 days) and nearby totals. Everything from Google sits under `data.google` and `clear_old_monthly_numbers` (daily 03:11 UTC) removes it after 30 days. Nearby totals are read live (up to 3 picks, one read a month) and are in the email only; they are not stored.
- Placement links: `cards.placement`, `create_placement_link`, `link_taps`; panel `src/components/review-link/placement-links.tsx` on `/app/cards`; each is an ordinary review link on go.kabsi.co with `?s=m`, so the Worker, tap function and kv-sync are unchanged.
- Signed-in pages: `/app/report` shows the monthly reports above the weekly ones (`components/report/monthly-report.tsx`, the words come from the stored lines).
- For GATE-2 (`monthly-report/api.ts`): `latestMonthlySummary`, `googleFiguresSummary`, `competitorTotals` (monthly once a month, request once a week, no provider call when too soon), `recurringFeedback`, `profileHealthSummary`.
