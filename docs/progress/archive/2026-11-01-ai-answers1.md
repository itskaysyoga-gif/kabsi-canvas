## What it does
- When a run's answer names the business (strictly: "Rose Lane Barbers" is not Rose Lane Salon), and Kabsi has facts to compare, one structured Haiku call lists the facts the answer states. It runs only with room in the AI Visibility monthly cap (`provider_spend_room` first, `provider_spend_add` after, estimate 0.01 USD). Code keeps a claim only when its sentence is in the answer, its values are in the sentence, it sits under this business's name, it is plain and it is not about a closed branch.
- Compared with Business knowledge the owner confirmed (verified) and the hours on record. A missing fact or anything unsure is never a difference.
- A difference is kept on `ai_visibility_runs.accuracy` (deleted with the run, 30 days after the business leaves), opens one `ai_answer` task per fact (updated, never piled up), and is one line in the Results Report. An answer from the same assistant that gives a non-hours fact right closes its item.
- The free check: the one ChatGPT answer against the Google profile read live through the Places guard; `free_checks.ai.accuracy` keeps only `{fact, sentence, line}`, and the line carries ChatGPT's own value.
