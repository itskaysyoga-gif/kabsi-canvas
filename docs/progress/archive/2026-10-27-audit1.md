# Audit 1 (P0.7-06, session 20c): the page-by-page audit, 27 Oct 2026

Walked on kabsi.co (production, 27 Oct 2026, 14:30 to 16:00 UTC) with Playwright Chromium at 390 and 1440 px: every public page, then every signed-in screen as the QA demo owner, an owner in onboarding, a manager and a staff member. Each page was checked for: status, one h1, no sideways scroll, no serious or critical axe issue, and the words a reader sees (banned names, dashes, exclamation marks, Arabic script, listing, visibility, free scan, spot, grid, pin, coming soon, Published, app, raw values). No paid AI call, no Google write, no message to anyone. One database write: `tests/e2e/seed/qa-login-code.sql` through execute_sql, four times (qa-demo, qa-wa-manager, qa-wa-staff and delivered+kabsi-qa@resend.dev), each a one-time code that lasts 30 minutes.

## Result in numbers

- Public: 47 addresses at two widths (94 page views). Status, one heading, sideways scroll and axe: all pass. Word findings: 6 pages had a real hit, all fixed or listed below.
- Signed in: 105 page views (4 roles, 17 addresses, 2 widths, plus expired session, loading and error states). Axe and sideways scroll: no serious or critical issue. app.spec.ts: 28 passed with `KNOWN_AXE` empty (AXE_REPORT=1 printed nothing). The harness `scripts/app-shots.mjs` (made-up data) drew 79 route states at 390 and 1440 px: "No problems".
- Found and fixed in this PR (copy and layout): the 10 findings below, in order of how much they matter. Found and sent to docs/LATER.md: 6 rows. Pictures: this folder, `01` to `07` (before left, after right, 390 px).
