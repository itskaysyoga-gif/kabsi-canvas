## 2. The publications: each cause

Read from `publications` (all rows touched since 1 Nov, plus every row in an open state) and `jobs` (kind publish):

1. Cause 1, a counting bug (all 8 and all 5). `whatsapp_staff_summary` and `whatsapp_staff_numbers` counted `state in ('approved','publishing') and created_at < now() - 30 minutes`. An approved publication whose `publish_after` is still ahead is waiting by design (the owner scheduled it, or the demo pack did), and a demo business never calls Google (R-17). The staff Operations view already used the right test (`publish_after < now() - 15 minutes`). Fixed in the database: `private.publication_stuck_counts()` is that test, and both functions use it.
2. Cause 2, two demo fixtures in verifying (`de3...2021`, `de3...1016`, 4 Nov, `publish_after` null). The recovery step moves a stuck verifying row back to checking only when `publish_after` is set, so these seeded rows sit in verifying forever and the staff System health page listed them as stuck. Fixed: that list skips checking and verifying rows of demo businesses.
3. Cause 3, not a stuck row but part of the 8 and 5: the two demo photo publications (`...1204`, `...2204`) came due on 9 Nov and were rejected with "photo file not available". The demo pack seeded photo rows with no stored file. Nothing reached Google (mock mode). Added to LATER (the demo seed, not the pipeline).

Real businesses: 0 publications in approved, publishing, checking or verifying; 26 verified, 22 cancelled, none failed. Nothing to retry, nothing to expire, so no owner was told anything.

Retry: the five approved demo rows are not due. Three come due at 19:16 UTC on 10 Nov and the 5 minute safety net (`offer_due`) queues their publish job by itself; they publish in mock mode. Two (17 and 19 Nov) wait. Retrying earlier would have published a post before the date the owner chose.

Also seen, harmless: 6 pending `publish` jobs (next run 14 to 24 Nov) belong to cancelled publications (the owner pressed Undo). When they run, `claim_publication` answers not_approved and the job ends. Not changed.

## 3. The held WhatsApp messages: cause and rule

- Right now: 0 rows held (`whatsapp_outbox` by state: sent 131, mock_sent 46, dropped 3).
- Cause (a real bug, `supabase/functions/whatsapp/index.ts`): when the staff phone was outside the 24 hour window, the alert or digest went as a template and the full text was held (state held, due in 30 days) for the reply that opens the window. Meta then reported a delivery status for the template's provider id, and the status handler set `state = 'sent'` on rows in state sent or held. `whatsapp_outbox_release` (which runs when the person writes) finds only rows in state held, so the full text was never released and never sent. 18 staff alerts and digests from 27 Oct to 8 Nov carry that mark (state sent, `sent_at` null, due date 30 days after creation): ids 143, 144, 158, 159, 160 to 163, 192 to 194, 207 to 210, 212, 218 to 220, 228. Their templates were delivered; their full text never was.
- The 4 held on 7 Nov: rows 218 to 220 and one more were in state held when the count was read; they are among the 18 (the status flipped them later). The exact four cannot be told apart now.
- Fix: `_shared/whatsapp/outbox-status.ts`. A status now touches only rows already sent, and only a failure changes them; a held row is never touched. Tests: `outbox-status.test.ts` (failed before, passed after).
- Rule, closed: a held message waits 30 days for the person to write, then it is closed (`dropped`, error `held_expired`). Before, a claimed held row with the window still closed was held again for 30 more days, so it never ended. `outbox-held.test.ts` (the 31 day case failed before, passed after).
- The 18 old rows are history and stay as they are: resending 2 week old alerts would only confuse. Nothing was deleted.
