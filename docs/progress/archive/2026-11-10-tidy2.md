## Part 3, Kapso deliveries (R-147 item 5)

Read only. No Kapso template, webhook or setting was changed and no message was sent. The Kapso connector was not used (R-49): the code reads Kapso through its API with the existing `KAPSO_API_KEY` and `KAPSO_PHONE_NUMBER_ID` Supabase secrets, from inside the `health` function.

- `supabase/functions/_shared/whatsapp/delivery-check.ts` (new): reduces each delivery row to its time, event, state word, the HTTP answer Kabsi gave and the webhook id (no payload, phone number or text is kept), counts failed deliveries in a window, counts per 5 minutes, and builds the one alert and the one email. Failed means Kapso's own word (failed, error, exhausted and the like), or an answer of 400 or more from Kabsi's function, or an error on a row that is waiting. Waiting and unknown rows are reported and never alerted. A log that cannot be read is itself alerted, with no error text.
- `kapso-admin.ts`: `readWebhookDeliveries` (GET `/platform/v1/webhook_deliveries`, 100 a page, cursor paging, at most 30 pages) and `listNumberWebhookIds` (GET only).
- `health/index.ts`: the daily call `{"only":"kapso"}` (cron `kabsi_whatsapp_check`, 05:17 UTC, unchanged) now also runs the delivery check for the last 24 hours. Failed deliveries raise the urgent ops event kind `whatsapp_check` with its own key `whatsapp_deliveries:<day>` (staff phone on WhatsApp, R-64) and one email to hello@kabsi.co with the key `whatsapp_delivery_check:<day>` (9.9), once a day. `{"only":"kapso_deliveries","from":"<ISO time>","to":"<ISO time>"}` reads any window up to 30 days back and answers counts per 5 minutes with no alert. No migration: the existing cron job is used.
- Tests: `delivery-check.test.ts` (10 Deno tests: row reduction keeps no number or text, what counts as failed, window and webhook filtering, 5 minute parts, no alert when nothing failed, one alert and one email with counts only, an unreadable log is alerted, one failing route does not hide the other, the reader only sends GET and pages by cursor).
