## PR 1: the feature (PR 337)

### Behaviour

- A photo sent with words, by an owner or by a manager allowed to approve posts:
  - The photo goes through the photo engine as before (`acceptPhoto`, then the `process_photo` job).
  - The photo is held (`photos.post_hold`), so the review growth tick never offers it as a plain photo.
  - The post's text:
    - With AI on: Kabsi's draft (`writePost`, only when the Anthropic key is set and inside `ai_budget_take`; 150 to 300 characters, in English, from the words and the verified facts).
    - With AI off, or when the draft fails `checkTypedPost` or `checkPost` (with `kabsiDraft`): the owner's words, checked by both.
  - A failure gives the existing postProblem line, then "Or add the photo to your Google profile on its own, or skip it.", with "Add as a photo" and Skip.
- The draft:
  - `whatsapp_photo_post_prepare` creates a `gbp_posts` draft (source owner, Update, `photo_id`).
  - The token comes from `whatsapp_action_create(user, 'local_post', id)`, the same as a typed post.
  - The answer shows the exact text with Approve (`a:`), Edit on kabsi.co (`e:`) and Skip (`qs:`).
- Approve:
  - It is `whatsapp_approve`, then `approve_publication`, with the 10 second undo.
  - The payload already carries `photo_path`, and `api/jobs.ts` sends it as the post's media.
  - Then the existing outcome lines (Sent to Google, Live on Google) of `task-close.ts`.
  - Approve pressed while the photo is still being checked (`post_photo_missing`): "Kabsi is still checking the photo. Press Approve again in a minute.". The token is not used, so Approve works again.
- Staff: the draft goes to the owners and managers who may approve, through the outbox (`post_ready` template outside the window). Staff get "Thank you. Kabsi made a post with your photo and words ..." and never an Approve button.
- Plan posts: `private.photo_posts_left` reads `entitlements().limits`.
  - Essentials: 2 a month. Pro "weekly": counted as up to 5 a month.
  - Sent posts this month and photo-post drafts waiting from WhatsApp count.
  - None left: "[Business]: your plan has no posts left this month ..." with "Add as a photo" and Skip.
- Several businesses: "Which business?" first. The photo id and the words wait in the open question and are handled after the pick.
- "Add as a photo" (`hp:`) puts the photo on the plain photo way (Yes, Edit, Skip from the tick). Skip (`hs:` or `qs:`) skips the photo and any post waiting with it.
- A photo with no words: unchanged. Voice notes: untouched.

### Independent review (a fresh agent with the diff, the rules and CLAUDE.md only)

It found no high-severity issue. No path lets a post or photo reach Google without an approver's yes, and staff and strangers get no Approve token. Its findings and what was done (commit 2da8647):

1. Medium, confirmed: a held photo still showed as a plain draft in the app's Photos list. Publish or Approve all would have sent it to Google a second time, as a plain photo.
   - Fixed: `approve_publication` refuses a held photo as a plain photo (`photo_with_post`, from any screen).
   - Fixed: `loadPhotos` and `photos_approve_all` leave held photos out.
2. Low: a held photo was left with no buttons after a refusal from the database or a failed token. Fixed: those refusals skip the held photo and any post made from it.
3. Low: staff could choose "Add as a photo". Fixed: only an owner or manager can; staff get Skip, and lines that tell them to send the photo again without words.
4. Low: the plan count.
   - Fixed: the business row is locked while counting.
   - Fixed: the comment on demo businesses.
   - Left (LATER row): Pro "weekly" counted as 5 a month, posts counted by when they were made, and no re-check at approval.
5. Low: staff were told "the owner is asked" when nobody had WhatsApp on. Fixed: a separate line, "It waits for the owner's yes on kabsi.co."
6. Low: Skip and "Add as a photo" skipped any draft using the photo. Fixed: they act only on the post made from it (`photos.wa_post_id`).
7. Low: the pgTAP plan case had a null escape.
   - Fixed: it now asserts a limit applies, then 0 left, then `posts_used_up`.
   - Fixed: it asserts the plan table's Essentials 2 a month and Pro weekly.
   - Added: a case that a held photo is refused as a plain photo.
8. Plausible: a failed hold was ignored. Fixed: a failed hold stops the turn with "Please send it as a photo."
