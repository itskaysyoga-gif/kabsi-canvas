# OUTBOUND-3: Outbound reopens for the pilot, the US first (25 Nov 2026)

Pull request: https://github.com/itskaysyoga-gif/kabsi-canvas/pull/599

## What changed
- `OPEN_COUNTRIES` and the database check allow only US and GB.
  - US: any open business with an email on its own website's domain, in the first tier plus auto repair and cleaners. No company check. The time zone comes from the register's `time_zone`, or from the state when the state lies in one zone.
  - GB: unchanged.
- CAN-SPAM:
  - Every US footer (Email 1, the follow-up and the reply to a yes) carries "Kabsi, <postal address>" from `OUTBOUND_POSTAL_ADDRESS`.
  - With no valid address set, nothing sends at all and no US draft is made.
  - The From and the subject are honest; the opt-out works by mailto and by reply and is kept as a hash.
- Copy: COPY 65, OUTBOUND-3 block.
  - Email 1 now has two lines on what Kabsi does, and never a price.
  - The follow-up's founding line is 19 US dollars a month for the first 12 months (written "$19" for US recipients).
  - US wording for US recipients.
  - The yes reply uses `TRIAL_LINE`.
- Once a week:
  - Prepare runs every 2 hours on Sundays and fills the coming Monday to Friday up to each day's cap.
  - The staff page groups the week by day. Approve all takes up to 100.
- Deliverability:
  - outbound-send refuses (`dns_not_ready`) until SPF, DKIM at zmail (2048 bits) and DMARC read correctly over DNS over HTTPS. The reading is recorded and shown on the staff page.
  - mail-tester actions on outbound-send, test mode only.
  - Opening sending needs `dns_ok` and a score of 10 of 10, recorded before the opening statement, even for lane R.
- Kill gate:
  - `outbound_check_kill_gate()` runs before every send.
  - For the 14 days after the 300th Email 1, no new Email 1 goes.
  - Then fewer than 3 yes replies ends Outbound, with a stop and a WhatsApp alert.
- Jobs: `kabsi_outbound_prepare` (15 */2 * * 0), `kabsi_outbound_send` (*/10), `kabsi_outbound_inbox` (5-55/10). `sending_open` false, `test_mode` true.
