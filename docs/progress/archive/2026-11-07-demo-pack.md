## Not seeded, with the reason

- WhatsApp connection (`whatsapp_optins`): a row with a phone would let the WhatsApp sender try a real send to that number, and no demo guard exists on that path. So /start shows stage 3 (Connect WhatsApp) for the QA demo owner on both businesses.
- Shield rows Website, Service area, Special hours, Description, Open status, Attributes and Verification read "Not read yet": the page reads Protection's facts, and a fact for a field the mock Google listing does not carry would make Protection raise a false change on Home.
- Payments, Creem subscriptions and receipts: they would count in the revenue figures (the ops digest sums payments with no demo filter). The Plan page shows the plan and "No payments yet".
- Team members beyond the two owners: a new auth user on an address other than `@test.local` raises a staff alert (ops_on_user). The Team page shows the two owners and pending invitations on example.com.
- Coach "Not checked yet" banner, "Example" labels and the Google figures stay: they are the mock Google mode (R-17), not missing data.

## Independent review (fresh agent, diff and rules only)

No breach of the rules (no delete, demo rows only, invented names, no dash or exclamation mark, nothing that triggers a paid or external call). Confirmed findings and what was done:

- Fixed in `20261108034703_demo_pack_fixes.sql`: weekly report periods ran past the report's Monday (now the seven days before it, like the real report); the salon's late-opening post and special day disagreed (both now 09:00 to 20:00, "until 8 pm"); the salon offer ended on 19 Nov while its text said end of the month (now 30 Nov); the competitor snapshots gave both businesses a rating of 4.3 (now 3.7 and 4.7, as their series).
- Fixed in the first migration before merge: it raised on a database without the demo workspace, which stopped the Database and Load jobs on the first push. It now skips with a notice. The version applied to production ran in full (the workspace was there), so the repo text differs from the applied text only by that guard.
- Known, not changed: the assert fingerprints whole rows of live tables of real businesses (sync times, tasks), so a cron tick between its two reads could abort the migration with a false alarm; it rolls back and is safe to run again. The six AI answer rows are stored as `source live` because Insights reads only live runs, so the staff run counts include them (cost 0). The Pro yearly plan has no payment. Posts and special days that name a date (17 and 19 Nov) are consistent only because the pack was applied on 7 Nov. Posted reviews that Google has not read back have no `existing_reply`, which is what the app calls "Sent to Google".
