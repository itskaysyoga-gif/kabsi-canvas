## Built

1. **Model and detection** (`voice/transcribe.ts`): `scribe_v3`, language left out, `VOICE_SCRIBE_MODEL` to name another without a deploy, the reported duration read back, a 200 with no body is a failure.
2. **The quote line** (`voice/guard.ts` `heardLine`, `whatsapp/agent.ts` `withHeard`): `> I heard: <words>`, cleaned of dashes, exclamation marks and WhatsApp formatting, cut at a whole word to 300 characters, on the first reply only (plain, button and list messages alike). A message with buttons is never cut (look.ts), so the transcript is shortened to what the body can still hold, and with no room the quote goes first as its own message: an Approve button is never lost to the quote. `lookProblems` ignores the quote line.
3. **Unclear, empty, failed**: one line, "I could not hear that clearly. Please send it again or type it." for an empty, failed, unreadable or unclear (no letter or digit) transcript and for a file that cannot be downloaded; nothing is handled.
4. **Never a yes** (`router.ts`, `agent.ts`): the transcript of a voice note is never a yes, no, number, owner or staff code, START or a staff command (pause, resume, payouts, reset, status, waiting, today). A spoken yes, approve or confirm with something waiting answers "Not counted as your yes / A voice note cannot say yes. / Tap Yes, or type it yourself." STOP still works. Every approval still needs a button tap or a typed Yes.
5. **Limits** (`voice/guard.ts`, `voice/service.ts`): 3 minutes (read from the Ogg length, or refused when the length cannot be read and the file is over 1.5 MB) and 10 MB per note (as before); 30 notes a day a business (every phone of the business together; the first business on a multi business phone), 5 a day for a phone Kabsi does not know, and 100 a day for all strangers together so they can never use what owners need. Over a limit: "That is a lot of voice notes for one day. Please type this one and I will take it from there." The old 20 a phone and 400 a number ceilings are replaced. Unknown phones are now transcribed (they were refused before) and answered by Nora.
6. **Audio**: held in memory only for the call, never written; only the text is used.
7. **Cost and health**: migration `20261111084249_voice2_usage_and_health.sql`: `voice_usage` (Beirut day: notes, seconds, failures), `voice_health` (failures in a row), `voice_record(seconds, outcome)` (service role only), `private.voice_yesterday()`, and two keys on `whatsapp_staff_summary()`. The morning digest and "today" say "Voice notes yesterday: 13 minutes in 31 notes." Staff alerts (urgent, to the staff phone): "Voice notes passed 60 minutes today" (once a day) and "Voice notes are failing" (at the 3rd failure in a row, at most one an hour). One failure alerts nobody; an answered note, including a silent one, ends the streak.
8. **The eval op** (`POST /whatsapp/staff/voice-eval`) now only measures. Its old `{"apply": true}` that switched `voice_notes` off on a failing table would have contradicted the decision, so it is gone.
9. **Words**: facts 01 (the voice notes bullet), 11 (privacy table row) and 13 (new FAQ entry "Can I send a voice note instead of typing?"); `knowledge/kabsi-facts.md` and `public/llms-full.txt` rebuilt (Nora's source); `/privacy` (processor line says "A speech to text provider", no company; retention; section 14) and `/terms` (section 16); `docs/COPY.md`; `docs/SECRETS.md` (`VOICE_SCRIBE_MODEL`); `knowledge/internal/2026-11-11-voice-2.md` (team only: the provider's name, the table). Public facts do not name any language's score (R-61).
