## 5. Independent review

A fresh agent, given only the diff and the rules. All six findings confirmed and fixed here, each with table rows or a test:
1. Medium: "for" and "about" after any first word read as a person, so "Discount for students this week", "Promo for Eid", "Approved for friday", "Done for today" became drafts, and a later yes would have saved the draft instead of approving a post. Both words were removed from the person words; "Sarah for highlights" is still read through the pack's service.
2. Medium: "Add our new menu", "add Christmas offer", "Put down closed friday", "Note down that we close early" read as bookings. After the verb there must now be a pack service, or a name (after "book" or "new appointment"), or a name and what they want.
3. Medium: the past readings filter dropped today's readings for a visit logged after it happened ("Maya came in monday 6" on a Monday evening). The filter was then removed altogether, because CI's extraction evaluation (`scripts/request-eval`) counts those readings as ambiguous cases that must be flagged; extraction is as on main.
4. Low to medium: bill, invoice, receipt, subscription, refund and upgrade counted as Kabsi on their own, so "Maya wants a 3 month subscription" went to the router; and "Maya saw our Instagram post and wants a trial" was kept out as one of Kabsi's jobs. Those words count as Kabsi now only after "my", "our" or "Kabsi"; Kabsi's job words keep a message out unless a named person wants or did something.
5. Low: "Staff called in sick", "Bank called", "DHL came", "Supplier called about the delivery" read as customers. Words for staff, suppliers, couriers and the like are never a name.
6. Low: the changed `book-f.test.ts` check accepted any request result; it now asserts `request_proposed`.

Noted by the reviewer, kept as R-149 says: at a business with the book on, a support question that names neither Kabsi, the plan, billing, the trial nor one of Kabsi's jobs ("Is my data safe?") gets the fixed line, not Nora (decision to confirm for lane R). With several businesses and none chosen, help shows no request book block. "X booked Y" while another proposal waits makes its own proposal; the earlier draft stays until it expires.
