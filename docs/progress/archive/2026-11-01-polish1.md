# Polish 1: follow-ups of Lean 1 and App 2 (1 Nov 2026)

Lane R's decisions of 1 Nov 2026 on the two reports. Branch `claude/polish1`.

## What changed

1. Analytics and the Meta Pixel start at the visitor's first scroll, tap or key press, or 5 seconds after load, whichever comes first (`src/lib/after-paint.ts`, `quiet` option). On a touch they start in the next task, after the touch has been handled, instead of at an idle moment up to 2 seconds later, so a visitor who acts fast and leaves still sends the PostHog event and the Pixel PageView. Consent is untouched: `meta-pixel.ts` and `telemetry.ts` still ask `region.ts` and the stored choice before anything loads. `tests/performance.test.ts` pins the new rule for scroll, pointer, touch and key, before and after load, and cancel. Facts updated (file 11).
2. zod is out of `src/routes/_authenticated/start.tsx`: `readStartSearch` in `src/lib/search-params.ts`. `tests/search-params.test.ts` proves it equals the zod schema over about 2,700 combinations of odd values and every single-key value. Home first load, local build, gzip: 193.5 KB, and no zod chunk. tailwind-merge stays: it is the 13.6 KB `utils` chunk behind `cn`, and no smaller safe path exists inside this task (a lighter config or plain clsx changes which class wins in 100+ places). LATER row rewritten with the options.
3. The floating Nora button is gone from signed-in screens (`assistant-widget.tsx`: the launcher is drawn for the public site only). "Ask Nora" is the first item under "Need a hand?" in "Your account and help" (`app-layout.tsx`); it sends the same `kabsi:open-nora` event the setup chip uses. Public pages are as before. COPY.md 36 line updated.
4. Not changed, as decided: the Settings tab "General", the full review list under On Google, "See all" opening Reports; the old bell choices stay in the database until a cleanup task.
5. Gates: `LH_JS_KB` in ci.yml 260 to 213 (203 KB measured by the same gate on this build, plus 10). In lighthouse-live.yml 260 to 247 (kabsi.co read 237 KB on the gate before this change, plus 10; the pull request build reads lower because the proxy compresses differently and the Sentry loader is not there). LCP gate stays 3.0 s; previews for `claude/*` stay off.
