## Independent review (fresh agent, diff and rules only)
Twelve findings, the confirmed ones fixed in 3c7511c:
1. Editing or re-adding an AI question re-ran paid checks at once. Now unchanged text is a no-op, a question added back keeps its schedule, and each business gets at most 20 saves a day.
2. An edited question showed the old answer. An edit now retires the old row and adds a new one.
3. "On Google" counted replies Kabsi had not seen on Google. Now it needs a verified publication, or a reply Kabsi read back from Google, and never counts while the publications have not loaded. A posted reply not seen yet reads "Sent to Google".
4. /privacy did not name the waiting list. It does now.
5. A pgTAP check passed for the wrong reason. The id is now read before switching persona.
6. Missing cases were added: a stranger, and editing through another business.
7. Two saves at once could pass the limit together. The count is now locked per business.
8. The founding tick carried over when the choice changed. A new choice now clears the tick.
9. The WhatsApp box's words were not stored. They now have their own column and check.
10. /a said a reply was published before Kabsi had seen it. Its line now reads "Sent to Google. Kabsi tells you when it shows there."
11. Staff AI spend stopped at 1000 rows. It now reads page by page.
12. Terms said "app". It now says "the Kabsi website".

Checked and fine: approvals and the 10 second Undo keep the same server calls, nothing fires without a click, the waiting list's RLS, grants, Turnstile, rate limit and retention, both migrations are additive with definer functions that check the caller, the resume link has no open redirect, and the RLS reads.
