## What was built

- **Part A, Google.** `src/lib/wa-signin.ts` reads `GET /auth/v1/settings` once per page load; the sign-in panel hides Continue with Google (and its line) only on a clear `external.google: false`. The daily `health` call (`{"only":"kapso"}`, 05:17 UTC) also runs `signin-provider-check.ts`: off or unreadable raises one staff alert on WhatsApp (`signin_provider_off`, urgent) and one email to hello@kabsi.co a day. By hand: `{"only":"signin"}`. `signup.ts` is unchanged.
- **Part B, Continue with WhatsApp.** Browser: a 32-byte secret kept in the tab, only its SHA-256 sent to `POST /auth-whatsapp/start` with Turnstile and a signed geo token (`GET /api/geo?sign=1`, Vercel's city and country, 5 minutes, HMAC with a key both sides derive from the route key they already share). Server: Turnstile, 10 starts per visitor an hour, then a 6-character card-alphabet code, stored as a salted hash with the secret's hash, the browser and system read from the user agent, the city and country (no IP). WhatsApp: one seam in `agent.ts` after STOP and before every other route reads only `^Kabsi sign in CODE$`, `si:y:<id>` and `so:<id>`; forwarded messages are ignored; 5 sign-in messages per phone per 15 minutes. Tap: the phone's connected user, or a new user `wa-<uuid>@wa.kabsi.co` (never mailed: the email sender, `notification_recipients` and auth-code skip it); the opt-in is stored with the consent text beside the button, so onboarding skips Connect WhatsApp. The browser's next check with its secret spends the sign-in once and gets the session. Device and city are cleared on the tap or at 10 minutes; rows deleted after a day.
- **Session minting.** Supabase docs searched (admin.generateLink, verifyOtp with token_hash). Chosen: `auth.admin.generateLink({ type: "magiclink" })` (sends no email) then `verifyOtp({ token_hash, type: "magiclink" })` on the server, with the browser's user agent passed on so the session names the browser. Why: Supabase's own one-time token and session, made only after the tap and for the browser holding the secret; nothing stored; no phone provider, SMS, template or self-signed JWT. A WhatsApp-only user needs an email for it, hence the placeholder address.
- **Part C, the notice.** `private.signin_notice_scan()` every minute (cron `kabsi_signin_notices`) turns new `auth.sessions` rows (after the owner's WhatsApp opt-in, within 10 minutes) into notices; a refresh keeps its session, so it sends nothing. Inside the window: WhatsApp, urgent, "New sign-in on Chrome, Windows", button Sign me out everywhere (deletes every session of the user, audited). Outside: no approved Utility template covers a sign-in (all name a business), so email instead; no template submitted; LATER row. Notices older than 30 minutes are skipped.
- **Onboarding.** "Where should receipts go?" (`account_contacts`, `set_contact_email`), only for wa.kabsi.co accounts, Skip remembered in the browser; checkout refuses without it.
- **Order.** Phone: WhatsApp, Google, email. Desktop: Google, WhatsApp, email. In-app: WhatsApp, the email form, Google below.

## Phone check for Rashid (after the merge)

1. On the phone, open kabsi.co/login in Safari or Chrome: the first button is Continue with WhatsApp, then Google (if on), then email.
2. Tap Continue with WhatsApp, pass the check, tap Open WhatsApp: WhatsApp opens with "Kabsi sign in" and 6 letters and digits, to Kabsi's number. Send it.
3. Kabsi answers "Sign in on Safari, iPhone, near <your city>?" with Yes, it's me and "Only tap if you started this on kabsi.co just now." Kabsi sent you no code.
4. Tap Yes, it's me: "Signed in". Back in the browser, the page goes on to your dashboard (or setup) by itself.
5. Within a minute, WhatsApp shows "New sign-in on Safari, iPhone" with Sign me out everywhere. Tap it: "Signed out everywhere", and the browser's next page asks you to log in.
6. On a computer, kabsi.co/login: Google first, WhatsApp second; Continue with WhatsApp shows a QR code; scan it with the phone and repeat 3 and 4; the computer signs in.
7. Forward the sign-in message to Kabsi from another chat, or send an old code: no sign-in happens (forwarded: no answer; old code: "This sign-in has ended").
