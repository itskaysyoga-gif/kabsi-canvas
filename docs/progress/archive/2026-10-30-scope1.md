## Part 2 (branches claude/scope1-measure, PR 280, and claude/scope1-staff)

Which timestamp each measure uses (all per business; demo and test accounts left out):

- Sign-up: `locations.created_at`. Google access confirmed: `locations.access_granted_at`.
- A = `locations.created_at` to the first of: the first unanswered review Kabsi read (`reviews.created_at`, strictly after sign-up, no existing reply, content not purged), the first change found on the profile (`profile_changes.detected_at`), the first free check finished after sign-up (`free_checks.done_at`, linked to the business). A free check finished before sign-up is stored apart in the new `locations.free_check_before_signup_at` (a trigger on `free_checks`) and is never the end of A; the pgTAP case has a business whose only result is a check before sign-up and its A is null, not zero or negative.
- B = `access_granted_at` to the first of: `publications.verified_at` (new column, stamped once by a trigger when the state becomes verified: a reply, post or corrected detail seen live on Google, including an auto-fix), or `tasks.resolved_at` of a profile change, profile gap or Google action task an owner or manager closed as done with no publication (confirmed by the owner where Google cannot show it).
- C = `access_granted_at` to the first `publications.verified_at` of an action the owner approved (every channel but `auto_fix`; `approved_by` is NOT NULL on every publication, so the channel is what tells an approval from the one switch the owner set for auto-fix).
- A finding (a review, a profile change, a check result) never counts as B or C.
- The first-task chooser (`pickFirstTask`, `first_task_candidates`) already follows 30.19: an unanswered review with a safe draft, else a post from facts the owner confirmed, else an hours fix only for a real difference, never a sensitive review, nothing changed to show activity; no task is created for a measure (nothing here creates one). Nothing to fix.
- Slow start alert: `public.slow_start_due()` (service role) claims a business once (`locations.slow_start_alert_at`) when access was confirmed more than 48 and less than 14 days ago, B is empty, and real work waits (an open reply, post, profile change, photos, hours or fact task, or a change sent to Google and not yet seen there). The onboarding tick sends `kabsi_staff_alert_v3` (through `ref("staff_alert")`) to the staff phones, naming the business and the step. A business with no real work waits: nothing is sent and nothing is counted as a failure. Deno test in `onboarding.test.ts`.
- Evidence log: `public.staff_evidence` (RLS, staff read; `staff_evidence_add` and `staff_evidence_delete` check `is_staff()`; the assistant function inserts with the service role when a hand-off has the reason `not_supported`), 24 months in `private.retention_sources_apply()`, CSV export in the panel (`src/lib/evidence.ts`, formula cells neutralised), privacy page and facts.
- Validation: `public.staff_validation()` and `public.staff_activation()` (staff only), panel `Validation figures` on /staff. MRR from the latest `subscription.paid` event of each active live subscription (and extra), yearly divided by 12; no data if an active live subscription has no paid event or a non-USD one. Cash by month from distinct payment ids, refunds and chargebacks from their own events; a month with a payment with no payment id, another currency or a refund with no business is `no_data`. Margin per account before tax and fees (the events carry neither).
