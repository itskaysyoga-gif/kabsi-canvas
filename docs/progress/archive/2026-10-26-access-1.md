# 26 Oct 2026, Access 1 (lane R)

Found: Rashid removed and re-added the Kabsi Clients group on Yawmiyati (10:53 and 10:54 UTC). The access job (every 5 minutes, live) listed the invitation and left it pending each run: Sentry KABSI-EDGE-G, 17 events, reason "name matches but address or city does not".

Cause: Google's `Invitation.targetLocation.address` is one formatted string (documented, and the fixture `accounts.invitations.json`). `invitations.ts` read `addressLines` and `locality` off it, so no live invitation could ever pass the address check. Earlier connections went through staff by hand (D267).

Changed:
- `invitations.ts`: a string address is split into comma parts, country and postcode parts dropped, on both sides; it matches when every part of the shorter address appears in the longer one and its street has two words or more. Country-only, city-only or different-town addresses never match. The object shape gains a 2+ word rule over street lines and city only.
- `api/cron.ts`: an accepted invitation that matches no business by place ID raises a Sentry error for staff (removal by hand; automatic removal is in docs/LATER.md).
- `google/index.ts`: a business is used by one invitation per run, so one consent never opens two listings. Skipped entries carry the invitation's address (`place`) for staff.
- Migration `20261026122647_access_alert_wording.sql`: the staff alert for lost access names the Kabsi Clients group, not hello@kabsi.co.
- A name-only rule for businesses connected before was written and dropped after the independent review (it could accept every branch of a same-named chain); a second review closed the country-level match.

Tests: `invitations.test.ts` passed; with `google/contract.test.ts` and `google/mocks.test.ts` 86 passed; `deno check` of `google/index.ts` and `invitations.ts` clean. The full `scripts/deno-check.sh` could not run here (deno.land is blocked in this container); CI runs it.
