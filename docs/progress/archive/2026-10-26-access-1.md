Later parts alone (area, postcode, country) never count. The object shape gains the same 2+ word rule over street lines and city only.
- `google/index.ts`: a business is used by one invitation per run, so one consent never opens two listings. Skipped entries carry the invitation's address (`place`) for staff.
- Migration `20261026120000_access_alert_wording.sql`: the staff alert for lost access names the Kabsi Clients group, not hello@kabsi.co.
- A name-only rule for businesses connected before was written and dropped after the independent review (it could accept every branch of a same-named chain).
