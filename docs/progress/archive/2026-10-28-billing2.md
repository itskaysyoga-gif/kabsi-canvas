## What was built
1. Extra-location fallback (10.2.3). Creem refuses to add an item to a subscription (`subscription_item_add_unsupported`, QA 1), so each extra location is a second checkout for the Extra location product with `customer.id`, on the owner's same Creem customer, in the plan's period. Table `billing_extras` (one row per extra subscription; RLS on, service role only). `billing_extras_sync(org)` writes the running units on the owner's plan row; never a second plan row. A failed payment of an extra counts 7 days, like the plan. Cancelling the plan (Kabsi, the portal, Creem's own end, a pause) schedules the extras' cancel (`ended_with_plan`); Keep my plan resumes them. Remove a location ends the newest extra at its period end; `private.entitlements` then turns off watch, draft and publish for the newest business past the plan's count (`location_on_plan` false). Nothing on Google changes. The Plan page and WhatsApp "add a location" return the Creem link; the location joins when the payment goes through.
2. Partner ledger: an extra's `subscription.paid` makes its own 25 percent line from its transaction amount (existing trigger, organisation from the checkout metadata or the extra row). `private.creem_base_cents` and `ledgerOf` fall back to the product price times its units.
3. Creem's mode: the API reference names `EnvironmentMode` test, prod, sandbox. `sameMode` reads prod, live and production as live; test, sandbox and local as test; an event with no mode belongs to its endpoint (tests for each in `tests/creem.test.ts`).
4. Founding price at day 90: checked, no code change. Nothing charges by itself; the owner chooses a plan through the normal checkout; `foundingCheck` marks the plan row `founding` (any mode); extras never take a founding place. The messages, read from `supabase/functions/_shared/programs.ts` `foundingLine`:
   - Day 60: "[Business]: you are 60 days into your three free months of Kabsi Pro as one of the Founding 25." / "They end on [date]. Nothing is charged by itself: on that day you choose a plan or stop." / "What should Kabsi do better for you? Reply here and a person at Kabsi reads it."
