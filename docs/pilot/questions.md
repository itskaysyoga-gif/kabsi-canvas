# Day 30, 60 and 90

Three short conversations, by call or WhatsApp, with the owner. At most three questions a month, as the pilot agreement says. Write down the owner's own words; do not lead them. Record the answers in the pilot sheet with the date.

## Day 30
1. In one sentence, what does Kabsi do for you?
2. What did Kabsi do this month that you would have done yourself, or not done at all?
3. What was confusing, slow or annoying?

Also record (from Kabsi, not from the owner): connected yes or no, weeks with at least one approval (of 4), share of drafts approved within 48 hours, reviews with a reply that is live on Google and the median time to it, Rashid's support minutes this month, paid month 1 yes or no.

## Day 60
1. Is there anything Kabsi sends you that you now ignore?
2. Has anything gone to your Google profile that you were not happy with?
3. What one thing should Kabsi do that it does not?

Also record: paid month 2 without being asked, weeks with an approval, support minutes.

## Day 90
1. If Kabsi stopped tomorrow, what would you miss?
2. Will you keep paying for it after the pilot? Why, or why not?
3. Who else do you know who should have it, if anyone?

Also record: still paying at day 90, the same measures as day 30.

## What the gates read (launch checklist section 4)
- Day 30: at least 3 businesses connected, approved work in 3 of 4 weeks, and paid. 2 means revise; fewer than 2 means stop.
- Day 60: at least 3 paid a second month unprompted.
- Day 90: at least 3 still paying. Fewer than 2: stop selling the subscription.
- Any warning or restriction from Google, Meta or Kapso stops everything until it is resolved.
