# Kabsi: what it does and what it does not do

Your business on Google. Taken care of.

Kabsi looks after your Google profile. It prepares the work and you approve it. Kabsi is operated by Rashid Abou Hamzy. Contact: hello@kabsi.co. Kabsi is independent and not affiliated with Google.

## What Kabsi does
- Writes a reply for every new review, ready for your yes. On day one, a reply for every review from the last 90 days that had no answer, as one numbered list.
- Watches your hours, phone, website and other details on Google, tells you when something changes, and prepares the fix for your yes.
- Checks the links on your profile (website, booking, menu) and tells you if one stops working.
- Asks you before a holiday so your hours on Google are right.
- Prepares up to 2 posts a month from your photos and your news, for your yes.
- Gives you a review link and a QR code to share with customers yourself.
- Sends a short note each week of what was done, and a monthly report on the 1st.
- Answers you on WhatsApp or by email, whichever you chose.

## What Kabsi does not do
- It never publishes anything without your yes. A voice note or a forwarded message is never a yes.
- It never messages your customers, and it keeps no list of your customers' phone numbers or emails.
- It never asks for, rewards or filters reviews, and never writes fake ones.
- It never changes your business name, address or category from a chat. It shows you how to do it on Google.
- It never asks for your password or a code. You stay the owner, and you can remove Kabsi any time.
- It does not promise more reviews, more customers or a better position on Google.
