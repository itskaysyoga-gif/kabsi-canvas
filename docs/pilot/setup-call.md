# The 15-minute setup call

Rashid and the owner, on a call or a video call. The owner uses their own phone for every step on Google. Rashid never asks for a password or a code, and never takes control of the owner's screen or account.

Before the call: the owner has the one page (`what-kabsi-does.md`). Rashid has the business name and checked it is on Google Maps.

## Minute 0 to 2: what we are doing
- "In 15 minutes Kabsi will be connected and looking after your Google profile. Three steps: find your business, add Kabsi as a manager on Google, then choose WhatsApp or email."
- "Nothing goes live without your yes. You can remove Kabsi any time."

## Minute 2 to 4: step 1, find your business
- The owner opens kabsi.co on their phone, taps "Check my business free" or signs in, and finds the business.
- They confirm it is theirs and that they can add people to its Google profile. If someone else looks after Google, stop here and plan a call with that person.

## Minute 4 to 10: step 2, add Kabsi as a manager on Google
- Kabsi shows the steps and Kabsi's ID to copy. The owner opens their Google profile, then Business Profile settings, People and access, Add, pastes the ID, chooses Manager, then Add (on some screens, Invite).
- "Manager lets Kabsi prepare changes. You stay the owner."
- Wait on the screen. Kabsi checks for the invitation every few minutes and shows "Access works." Do not move on until it does. If it does not come within 10 minutes, note it (checklist line 1) and check whether the owner is signed in to the right Google account.

## Minute 10 to 12: step 3, WhatsApp or email
- WhatsApp: the owner taps WhatsApp, then "Open WhatsApp", and sends the message that is written for them. Kabsi answers in the chat.
- Email: the owner taps Email. Approvals and reports come by email.

## Minute 12 to 14: the trial with a card
- The owner taps "Start 14 days free". Creem's page shows the exact price before they pay: 19 US dollars a month for a founding business. They enter the card on Creem's page, not with Rashid.
- "Nothing is charged for 14 days. Cancel before day 14 and you pay nothing. From month 13 the price is 29, and we email you 14 days before."

## Minute 14 to 15: what happens next
- "Kabsi is now writing replies for every review from the last 90 days that had no answer. They arrive as one numbered list. Send a number to read one, then tap Approve. Nothing goes to Google without your tap."
- "Each Monday you get a short note of what was done. On the 1st of each month, a monthly report."
- "If something looks wrong, reply in the chat or write to hello@kabsi.co."
- Book the day 30 call now.

## After the call (Rashid, the same day)
- Staff, Programs, Founding 25: check the business is linked ("Link for the founding price").
- Check the first-day list arrived and note the time from signup to the first approval (target: under 24 hours).
