Task Site 3a (master plan 30.50 item 10, as changed by 30.51). Text only: the screenshots were looked at in the session and are not committed (CLAUDE.md, the Clean 4 guard). Each finding names the screenshot it comes from, so a later session can take the same one again with the same script.

## How it was captured

- Playwright (the pre-installed Chromium) against production, https://kabsi.co, on 10 Nov 2026, before any change of this task was deployed.
- Widths 320, 375, 390, 430 (phone emulation, touch), 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 px. Full page, reduced motion, every page scrolled to the end first so the lazy parts load.
- Routes: every address in https://kabsi.co/sitemap.xml that the task covers in full (Home, Pricing, How it works, /scan), and for the other pages: About, Security, FAQ, Partners, Partner terms, Setup call, Privacy, Terms, Guides, /for, four finished and two template industry pages, the review link tool, /google-profile-check, /manager-steps, /login, a 404 and two guides. That is 28 routes at 8 widths, 224 full-page screenshots. The other 18 industry pages and 25 guides use the same two templates as the ones captured; they are not listed one by one.
- Menus: the phone menu open (320, 390), the header's Businesses link (1024, 1440). Forms: an empty search sent (390), typing into the search (390). Errors: the free check when its bot check cannot run (390), the 404.
- A real free check result: two saved checks from the database, `/scan/XJPRAUXS` (6 Nov) and `/scan/CFE4RMDN` (5 Nov, the one the Scan 3 live test reuses), at all 8 widths. No new check was run: the bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) cannot load from the session's network (`brunhild.challenges.cloudflare.com` unreachable through the proxy), so a new check stops at "Please complete the check and try again". Read only; no data provider was called.
- Every page was also measured in the browser: the page's width against the window (sideways scroll), elements that cross the window's edge, links and buttons smaller than 24 px, and the yellow buttons on the page.

Measured on every route at every width: no sideways scroll and no element past the window's edge (0 of 224).

## Labels

- **Confirmed defect**: something is wrong or broken on the page.
- **Confirmed inconsistency**: the page works but breaks a rule of DESIGN.md, COPY.md or the master plan, or differs from the same thing elsewhere.
- **Recommendation**: it works and follows the rules; a change would make it better.
