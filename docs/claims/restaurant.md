## Changed from Design 8 to the facts (lane R decision 1)

- Hero message "3 changes ready for Google ... the lamb shank at 19.50", the FAQ "Yes, after your yes" (menu) and the
  job "Send the new menu ... Kabsi prepares the menu photo and the changes": Kabsi does not edit menu items (facts 04).
  Rows 2, 3 and 25.
- The job "A booking link that works" and the guide's "the link changes after your yes": the booking button is
  [COMING]. Replaced by "Dish photos, checked" (row 8) and row 24.
- "Special days, asked in time" named Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve and a private event as days Kabsi asks about; Kabsi
  asks before public holidays only (facts "Holiday hours"). Row 6 names bank holidays; row 16 keeps the others as
  Google's special hours.
- "Each gets a reply that mentions the dish or the table they wrote about": facts 7.1 allow it only when the owner gave
  the facts. Rows 7 and 20.
- "Most people look at a restaurant's menu before they book": a share with no source; left out.
- The free check example rows "Christmas hours", "Menu photos From last year", "Reviews with no reply", "Booking link":
  not in Google's public data (K-82, lane R decision 10). Row 11.
