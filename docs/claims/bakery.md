The pick is the sum of "bakery" and "bakery near me", and both are how a customer types. "cake shop" is a separate trade search (5,400
and 49,500) and is on the page. The other terms are product searches with no volume in the table.

## Left out

- The UK rule on allergen labelling for food packed on the premises for direct sale (often called Natasha's Law). Search results said
  it applies to bakeries, but the Food Standards Agency pages could not be read in full today (the fetched pages gave no definition, date
  or label wording), so no sentence uses it. Web 2 reads the FSA page before any allergen line goes on a page.
- Allergen rules in the UAE (Dubai Municipality and others): not read today.
- A second Google category beyond "Cafe". "Cake shop" and "Pastry shop" appear in Kabsi's own category suggestions but no Google page
  or live profile read today showed them.
- A second photo: the bakery has one (`bakery-window`). `bakery-counter` shows a brand sign and is not used (DESIGN-PROPOSAL, Design 5
  round 2).
