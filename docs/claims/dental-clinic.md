# Claim sheet: dental clinic

Page `/for/dental-clinic`, file `src/content/industries/dental-clinic.ts`. Checked 5 Nov 2026 (Industries 2), against branch `claude/industries2-c`.
Source IDs (G, H, K, P) are in `README.md`. F means the facts file `knowledge/kabsi-facts.md`, cited by section; "F 13 hard
reviews" is the FAQ answer "What happens with hard reviews?" (`knowledge/facts/13-faq.md`). Example business: Parkside Dental,
Dubai (invented, labelled Example), with Smile Clinic and Northgate Dental Care as invented rivals.

Medium risk note: every review of a dental business is at least medium risk and gets a careful draft with the issue shown above
it; a reply never says the reviewer was a patient and never mentions a visit, an appointment or a treatment (F 13 hard reviews;
F FAQ "Does Kabsi treat clinics ... differently?"). Only a high risk review (illness, injury, an allergy or another safety
problem, a legal threat, a complaint that names a team member, and the rest of that list) gets no draft.
