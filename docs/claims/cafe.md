milk latte.

Reason for lane R: "coffee" is too broad for the UAE pick. The word also means beans, instant coffee, brands and recipes, so most of
its 201,000 searches need not be for a place to sit. That is a reading of the word, not a measurement; DataForSEO does not split it. "cafe"
(49,500) and "coffee shop" (18,100) always mean a place. The UK pick "cafe" already works and also has the largest number. The page keeps
"cafe" as the free check term in both markets (it was "cafe" in the template). "coffee" is not on the page.
