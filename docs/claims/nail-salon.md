## Search terms (D1)

On the page: nail salon, nail salon near me, beauty salon, manicure, gel nails, acrylic extensions, pedicure, lash extensions, nail art.

| Market | Pick (volume) | Candidates | Proposal |
| ------ | ------------- | ---------- | -------- |
| UAE | nails (14,800) | nail salon 6,600; beauty salon 14,800; nails 14,800; manicure 3,600 | Use "nail salon" |
| UK | nails (201,000) | nail salon 74,000; beauty salon 27,100; nails 201,000; manicure 18,100 | Use "nail salon" |

Reason for lane R: "nails" is the biggest number but the word also means the metal fastener and a person's own nails, so many of those
searches are not for a salon. That is a reading of the word, not a measurement; DataForSEO does not split the volume. "nail salon"
and "beauty salon" always mean a business. The page keeps "nail salon" as the free check term in both markets.

- Dubai Health Authority or UK local council licensing for beauty treatments. Not sourced today; if lane R wants it, Web 2 reads the
  regulator pages first.
