## Search terms (D1)

On the page: car repair, garage, mechanic, car repair near me, mot test, car service, brake repair, car ac repair, tyre shop, garage open
Saturday.

| Market | Pick (volume) | Candidates | Proposal |
| ------ | ------------- | ---------- | -------- |
| UAE | garage (9,900) | car repair 3,600; garage 9,900; auto repair 1,000; mechanic 2,900 | Use "car repair" |
| UK | garage (135,000) | car repair 18,100; garage 135,000; auto repair 5,400; mechanic 40,500 | Use "car repair"; add "mechanic" |

Reason for lane R: "garage" is the biggest number in both markets but the word also means a parking garage, a garage door, a garage
conversion and a home garage, so much of the volume is not for a repair shop. That is a reading of the word, not a measurement;
DataForSEO does not split the volume. "car repair" and "mechanic" always mean the work. In the UK "mechanic" (40,500) is more than twice
"car repair" (18,100), so the check can use it too. The page keeps "car repair" as the free check term in both markets (it was "car
repair" in the template) and lists "garage" and "mechanic" as searches. "mot test" is a UK term with no row in the table; "car ac
repair" suits the UAE and has no row either.
