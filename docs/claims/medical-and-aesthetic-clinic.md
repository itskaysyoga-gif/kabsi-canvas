## Search terms (D1)

On the page: aesthetic clinic, aesthetic clinic near me, skin clinic, dermatologist near me, laser hair removal, hydrafacial,
acne treatment, general practitioner.

| Market | Pick (volume) | Candidates | Proposal |

| UAE | clinic (12,100) | aesthetic clinic 1,600; medical clinic no data; skin clinic 590; clinic 12,100 | Use "aesthetic clinic" |
| UK | clinic (14,800) | aesthetic clinic 2,900; medical clinic no data; skin clinic 5,400; clinic 14,800 | Use "skin clinic" |

Reason for lane R: "clinic" alone covers every kind of clinic (dental, eye, vet, walk-in), so the volume says little about customers of
this page. That is a reading of the word, not a measurement. "aesthetic clinic" is what the page is about in the UAE. In the UK
"skin clinic" has almost twice the volume of "aesthetic clinic" (5,400 against 2,900). Volumes are small in both markets (590 to
5,400), which also means a search for the clinic's own name or "near me" matters more than a generic term. The page keeps "aesthetic
clinic" as the free check term; Web 2 can use "skin clinic" for the UK check. "medical clinic" has no data in the table.
