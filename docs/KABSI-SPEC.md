## 1. Product in one paragraph

Kabsi is a Google Business Profile assistant for local businesses anywhere. The owner adds hello@kabsi.co as a Manager on their Google profile. From then on, every new review arrives by email with a reply already drafted in the reviewer's language; the owner taps Post, Edit or Skip. Kabsi also drafts Google posts, checks and posts photos, sets special hours, watches the listing for unwanted edits (Listing Shield), and sends a weekly email report. Nothing is ever written to Google without the owner's explicit approval. An optional NFC + QR card (go.kabsi.co/{CODE}) opens the business's Google review page in one tap, for every customer, with no filtering. Until Google approves the API application, real businesses are served by concierge mode (D267): a person does the Google steps by hand and the owner still approves every word.

Presented as five modules (D282): Replies, Profile Care, Listing Shield, Review Link and Card, Monday Report. Plans: Free, Pro, Partner.

Sold by: self-serve signup (with a free trial, after billing ships) · partners (commission by default, D274) · the Lebanon field team (cash, Whish, OMT, USDT; D291).

Prices (USD, all countries), target from D281: Free ($0: review link, QR code, printable table card, share message, monthly Google snapshot email later) · Pro $19 a month or $190 a year (two months free; all five modules, a team of 3 once built, Nora support) · extra locations $15 a month or $150 a year · Lebanon bundle $120 a year (Pro for 12 months, an NFC card and in-person setup; sold by the field team; cash, Whish, OMT or USDT; this is the existing pro_12m plan) · 14-day trial without a card (30 days through partner links and inserts). Extra cards (Lebanon only) $10 each or $40 for five. Monthly plans cancel any time; yearly plans are refundable within 14 days; when a plan ends the review link and card keep working and only the service stops. The 6-month plan ($75) is retired (nobody holds it). Until billing ships, the live system still sells Pro 6 months $75 and 12 months $120 (paid in USDT, staff-confirmed) and the knowledge base and Terms describe that; do not describe the new prices publicly before they exist. Partner economics (D274): commission is the default: 30% of each client payment for 12 months (40% for the first 10 "founding" partners), clients from a partner link get a 30-day trial. Agencies with 5 or more active clients may resell: $8 per active location per month, billed monthly in USDT (TRC20 TMbdkH9hY14RGgz9N99DCXu3LXGDZBDqMe or Binance Pay ID User-2ad9b), founding $6 locked 12 months, first location free for its first 30 days (D239). Nothing is billed to any partner until Google access is live on a real business and the location is not a concierge or mock one.

- Promises of reviews, ratings, "5-star", rankings, "boost", "Local SEO results", "dominate", "guaranteed top 3", "100% Google compliant", "zero hallucinations".
- Saying or implying that replies, posts, keywords or photos raise a ranking (D257).
- Calling taps or scans "reviews": they are opens of the review page.
- Em dashes in any copy or AI output (D244).
- Invented numbers ("95% time saved", "3x reviews") or testimonials.
- "Locks your profile", "blocks competitor edits", "automatic replies".
- Anything implying Google endorses or partners with Kabsi. Footer: "Kabsi is independent and not affiliated with Google."
- Asking only happy customers to review. Cards and copy say "Leave us a Google review", never "5 stars".
- Advertising a feature before it works end to end (videos, the partners page and ads included: no Shield, commission or trial claim before it ships).
- Naming a company, licence or registration Kabsi does not have (D290).
- JSON-LD never includes AggregateRating or Review markup about Kabsi.

## 7. Cloudflare Worker: go.kabsi.co

```
GET /{CODE}            CODE matches ^[2-9A-HJKMNP-Z]{6}$ (case-insensitive, upper-cased)
GET /{CODE}?s=q        QR scans carry s=q; NFC tags carry no query, so source=nfc
GET /                  302 to https://kabsi.co
```

KV s:{CODE} holds {d: destination, b: location_id, a: active}; on a miss, GET the tap function and cache the answer. Active: 302 to destination, then ctx.waitUntil(fetch(tap, {code, source, country: request.cf.country, device, is_bot})). Unassigned or unknown: 302 to {APP_ORIGIN}/activate/{CODE}. Disabled: 302 to {APP_ORIGIN}/?sticker=disabled. Bot flag: known bot user agents, or request.cf.botManagement when available. Bots are still redirected, just flagged. Rate limit: Cloudflare rule on go.kabsi.co/*, 30 requests per 10 s per IP, block for 60 s. The Worker also skips logging above 5 taps per IP per code per minute (KV counter). No IP is stored anywhere. Sentry via @sentry/cloudflare.

Planned (D271, Q01): kv-sync writes each card's value to KV with no expiry (write-through) instead of deleting the key, and every card is backfilled once, so a tap never waits on or fails with the database; taps are deduplicated over 10 minutes per code without storing IPs; GET /_check performs a synthetic tap that UptimeRobot calls every 5 minutes (Q16). The Worker is deployed on its own (D231), not through the Cloudflare MCP.

## 11. Security checklist

- RLS on every table; advisors run after every migration.
- Service role key only in Edge Function secrets.
- Action tokens: 32 random bytes, only SHA-256 stored, single use, 7-day expiry, GET never performs an action.
- Pub/Sub: verify the Google OIDC token (issuer, audience, email of the push service account).
- tap: shared secret header; the Worker is the only caller.
- Public functions (places-search, lead): per-IP rate limit via a Postgres counter + honeypot on forms; sensitive or expensive routes fail closed on a limiter outage (D266).
- Owner can revoke Kabsi at any time by removing the Kabsi Clients group from their profile; gbp-sync detects lost access, pauses the location and emails the owner.
- Data deletion on request: delete the location, cascades; Storage paths removed by the same function.
- Every SECURITY DEFINER function pins search_path; service-only ones are revoked from public, anon and authenticated.
- Review text never becomes instructions to a model (D257).
- Every server-side conversation or session lookup checks that the requesting user owns the record, not just that the id was guessable (D265).
- Every Google-write publish path claims its target row atomically (a publishing state) before calling Google; a write left in an ambiguous state (timeout, network, 5xx) is never auto-retried, only a definite rejection is auto-reverted (D266). Extend the claim to gbp_posts, photos and hours_publish before Gate A.
- Manager invitations are accepted only when they match a consented business in access_pending and the role is Manager; anything else stays pending and alerts #kabsi-alerts (D270, D293). hello@kabsi.co uses Google Advanced Protection with two hardware keys.
- Public tools and Nora tools sit behind Turnstile, cache, and a daily Places cap (D285); billing webhooks verify signatures and are idempotent.
- Sentry and jobs_log scrub review text, reviewer names, emails, tokens and payloads before they leave the server (D266).
- Secrets never appear in code, commits, logs, PR text or screenshots.
