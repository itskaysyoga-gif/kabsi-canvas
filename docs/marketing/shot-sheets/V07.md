## Product screen: S05

Setup (once, by Hussein's Claude, after Hussein's "apply"): add one new mock review to Juniper Hair Studio (a 4-star review is fine). Within about 5 minutes Kabsi drafts it and emails the demo login. This is the same step as the open P0.1-06 After-merge email check, so do both together.

Before recording, so "You're all caught up" appears at the end: in the app, approve or skip the other drafts of Juniper Hair Studio off camera (Chloe Adams, Ben Carter, Camille Durand, Jordan Ellis), or record V01's second take on Chloe Adams first.

Flow on the phone:

1. Open the email in the phone's own Mail app, not the Gmail app, so no Google screen appears. Crop or blur the "To" line: the demo login address never shows on screen.
2. Tap "Review reply". The reply page opens with "Demo data" beside the logo.
3. Pause on the draft, tap "Approve reply".
4. Pause on "Posted. Your reply is on Google."
5. Tap "Open Kabsi" (or open the app), Reviews: "You're all caught up."

One new review gives one take. A second take needs a second mock review (another "apply").
