## Rules for this video

- Leah is a presenter, never a customer. No "I use Kabsi", no "my café", no "my customers".
- The tag "AI presenter" stays on screen for the first 3 s. The ad text says "Presenter is AI-generated." Turn on Meta's AI label if it is offered.
- Paid versions carry no "Illustrative scene" tag because there is no scene actor, only Leah.

## Hooks (on-screen text, first 2 seconds; the voice says the same thing first)

- A: If you run a local business, your Google profile works for you every day.
- B: What Kabsi does, in 15 seconds
- C: Reviews. Profile changes. One short report.

Hook C and the full script need the profile change and report features, so C is for the full version only.

## Claims check

- Source script changed in one place: "reviews pile up and details change while you're busy" became "Reviews come in, and details can change, while you're busy". The rules forbid generalisations and wording that asserts something about the viewer's own business, and the first form does.
- "Kabsi drafts replies to your Google reviews. You approve." is allowed. "Tells you when something on your profile changes" is only in the full version, after P0.2-04.
- "Fourteen days free, no card" and the free call are only in the full version, after P0.7-07 and P0.1-V3.
- Leah never says she is a customer, an owner or the founder. The AI tag and the ad line are in every paid cut.
- No "automatically", "real-time", ranking or speed claims. "Google profile" is plain descriptive wording.
