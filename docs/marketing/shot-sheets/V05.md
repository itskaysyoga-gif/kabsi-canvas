## Voice

```
Got a one-star review?
Here's how to reply.
One: thank them, even if it stings.
Two: name the actual problem, so it's clear you read it.
Three: offer to put it right, and point them to the contact details on your profile.
Four: sign with your name.
Never argue in public, never share their details.
Kabsi prepares replies like this for your new reviews.
You approve.
```

Line 5 differs from the VIDEO card ("give a way to reach you directly"): Kabsi's drafts never carry a phone number, email or link (K-113), and the draft on screen points to the profile, so the voice matches the screen.
