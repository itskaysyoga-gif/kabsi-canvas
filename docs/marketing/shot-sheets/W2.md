## Technical rules (website, LCP under 2.5 s on a phone)

- Autoplay muted, play only when scrolled into view, loop.
- Each loop is MP4 and WebM, under 1.5 MB each, 720 px wide.
- Stop for visitors who prefer reduced motion; show the poster frame instead.
- No captions file (silent). Provide an `aria-label` or a text line next to each loop saying what it shows.
- The section's own button carries the call to action; nothing is burned into the loop.
