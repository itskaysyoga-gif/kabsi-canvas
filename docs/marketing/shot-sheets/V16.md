## Before you ask: the consent checklist

Do every item, in this order, before anything is filmed. Record the answers in the customer's file, not in this repository.

1. The business has used Kabsi for long enough to have something true to say. Never ask in the first week.
2. Ask the question "Is anyone at Kabsi (team, friends, partners, related businesses) connected to your business?" If yes, the video says so on screen ("Connected to Kabsi: ...") and may not be used as a customer story in ads. Do not guess the answer.
3. A signed release covers social media, the website and ads, for a stated period. The customer can withdraw it, and Kabsi takes the video down within a stated time. Rashid or a lawyer approves the wording before the first use; this sheet gives the checklist, not the legal text.
4. No incentive. No free month, discount, gift or favour for the video. The referral reward (one free month, I3) is never offered for it.
5. The customer never mentions or is asked about leaving a Google review for Kabsi or anyone. Reviews and this video are separate things.
6. The customer shows only their own profile, with permission. No reviewer's name or review text appears unless that reviewer and the business have both agreed in writing. The default is to show no review text.
7. If the customer speaks Arabic or another language: English captions are written, then read and approved by the customer in writing before publishing. Never publish a translation the customer has not seen.
8. The customer sees the final cut and approves it before it goes out. Any later change needs a new approval.

## The email Rashid sends (three questions, from the VIDEO plan)

```
Subject: Three questions, if you have a minute

Hi <first name>,

If you would like to, film yourself on your phone answering these three questions. Hold the phone upright in good light. Anything you say is your own words, and you can film it again.

1. What was Google like for you before?
2. What does Kabsi do for you now?
3. What would you tell another owner?

Send me the video when you are ready. Nothing goes out until you have seen the final cut and signed the release. There is nothing you need to do or give in return.

Rashid
```

(Written to the Kabsi house rules: no dashes, no exclamation marks. Rashid may rewrite it in his own voice.)

## How the customer films

Phone upright (9:16), window light, steady, one take per question, 10 to 20 s each. Their own words, their own accent, their own language. No script is supplied. No product claims are suggested to them.

## How Rashid edits (Higgsfield AI Video Editor)

1. Trim each answer to its strongest sentences. Cut only the beginning and the end of a take, or a pause. Never reorder words so that the meaning changes, and never make them say something they did not.
2. Captions in the brand caption style, then the customer approves the caption text.
3. Name and business on screen at the start ("Name, business name, city"), with the "Connected to Kabsi" line if item 2 of the checklist was yes.
4. End card for the time: early access, Profile Check or start free (brand kit).
5. Cuts: 30 to 45 s (organic and website); one 15 s cut (the single strongest answer, one take, unedited).
6. Music low and licensed, or none. No AI generation, voice cloning, face changes or lip sync on the customer.

## What the customer says that cannot run

A customer may say things the claims table does not allow, such as "I rank higher now", "it replies automatically" or a number of new reviews. The customer's statement is treated as Kabsi's claim in an ad. If a line breaks the table, either cut that line (when the rest still reads as they said it), run the video as organic only, or ask them to re-film. Never edit the claim away by changing words. Results must be real: a number or outcome is used only if Kabsi's own data shows it.

## Hooks

The customer's own first line, unedited, as on-screen text. No written hook is supplied.

## Claims check

- No fake customers and no AI testimonials: the whole video is the real person, unaltered. The FTC rule on testimonials (in force since October 2024) is the reason items 2 to 4 and the "what cannot run" rules exist.
- No incentive, no review gating, no mention of asking customers for reviews.
- No Google screen is shown. Their profile appears only if they choose, with their permission.
- Every number or result stated is checked against Kabsi's own data before the video runs as an ad. "Trusted by X businesses" is never added.
