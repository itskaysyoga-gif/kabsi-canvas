# Feature truth: what each video shows and whether it is live

Checked 4 Oct 2026 against `main` (7dcb18f), `knowledge/kabsi-facts.md` and the plan. Google Gate A is pending, so every Google call runs in mock mode, and real owners are in early access: a person on Kabsi's team posts what they approve, within one working day (`src/lib/concierge-copy.ts`).

How to read it (VIDEO "Feature truth check"; plan R-10):

- **Live**: works today for a new customer, as the video shows it. Can be recorded now.
- **Early access**: works today, but not quite as the video shows it (for example the team posts the reply by hand). Can be recorded now; the video carries the "Early access" tag and stays organic.
- **Not live**: the screen is not true yet. Record only after the task named.
- No video becomes a paid ad until P0.7-07 records "Paid ads may start". Then re-check this table: every feature in an ad must be **Live**.

Update this file whenever a task in the "Becomes live with" column merges and its After-merge checks pass.

## Claims table (VIDEO "Rules every video must follow")

| Say                                                                                                  | Never say                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Kabsi drafts replies to your Google reviews. You approve."                                          | "Automatically replies", "posts for you automatically"                                  |
| "Nothing is published without your approval."                                                        | "Real-time", "24/7 monitoring", "Google Protection" (until Gate A and P0.2-04 are live) |
| "Kabsi checks your Google profile and tells you when something changes." (only once P0.2-04 is live) | "Rank higher", "get to #1 on Google", "guaranteed more reviews", "boost your rating"    |
| "One short report every week."                                                                       | Any score or grade for a profile                                                        |
| "$19 a month. 14 days free, no card." (only after P0.7-07)                                           | "Official Google partner", "approved by Google", "Google-certified"                     |
| "Google says more reviews and good ratings can help local ranking" (with the source on screen)       | Any statistic not from Kabsi's own data; "trusted by X businesses"                      |

Always allowed: "Your reviews and listing. Taken care of." and "Kabsi looks after your business on Google: a reply ready for every new review, your hours and details kept right, and fresh posts. You just approve." (K-110).

Also never: superlatives ("easiest", "best"), speed claims ("in seconds", "one tap") unless measured, time-saved figures, generalisations about owners, or wording that asserts something about the viewer's own business ("your hours just changed").
