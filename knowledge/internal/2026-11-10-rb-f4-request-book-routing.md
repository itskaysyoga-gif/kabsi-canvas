# RB-F4: request book messages on WhatsApp (10 Nov 2026, lane R R-149)

A team note, not a public fact: the request book is on only for Kabsi's own test businesses, so it is not in `knowledge/facts/`.

- At a business with the request book on, the order is: STOP and the commands that need no model, then the request book (its commands, then a message about a person and what they want or did), then the off-topic line, then everything else.
- The pack's words win over Kabsi's own product words: at a gym, "trial" is the gym's trial. A question is about Kabsi itself only when it names Kabsi, the plan, billing, the owner's own trial or one of Kabsi's jobs (reviews, replies, Google, the listing, posts, photos, hours, stats).
- "X booked Y" with no open enquiry for X is a new visit, appointment or job (by pack), proposed with Save and Change; with one, it converts as RB-F built.
- "help" at such a business adds a short request book block; "reset test" is shown only to Kabsi staff.
- When public: a fact line in `knowledge/facts/03-assistant-rules.md` with the request book's own section.
