A team note, not a public fact. Nothing here reaches Nora, the website, `knowledge/kabsi-facts.md` or the llms files.

## What changed

- Since 10 Nov 2026 (R-151, master plan 30.51) Founding 25 (three months of Kabsi Pro free, by application) is private. It left every public surface: the line above the header, the Home, Pricing and industry page cards, the footer and About links, the facts, the llms files, the FAQ, the sitemap and the social image. Nothing replaces the line above the header.
- Rashid offers it only by message and email, with a private link: https://kabsi.co/founding/<slug>. The slug is the app_settings value founding_invite_slug (24 characters). Never write a real slug in a file, a commit, a log, a pull request or a screenshot.
- Staff read or rotate the slug with the function staff_founding_invite(boolean) on the staff Founding panel. The old link stops at once on rotation.
- The page at that link is noindex nofollow with no canonical. /founding with no slug, or a wrong slug, is the real 404. The place count and the application both need the slug.
- Harvey's follow-up email will carry the invite while places remain. That email is built by Outbound 2, not by Founding 1.
- Internal names do not change (code, tables, settings, founding_places and the like).
- The price for the first 30 paying businesses ($19 and $39) stays. Its public name is now "launch price" (was "founding price"). A Founding 25 business that chooses a plan after its 90 days still gets that price whatever the count says (the facts line below says so; the public facts no longer do).
- "Founding Partners" (the first 20 approved Kabsi Partners, facts 08 and the partners page) is a different thing and did not change.
- What Nora says now is in `knowledge/facts/03-assistant-rules.md`: there is no public founding offer, an invited business opens the link in its invitation, Nora never gives an address for it and never describes its terms.

## The program, word for word as it stood in the public facts until 10 Nov 2026

### What it is, who can apply, the places, how applications work, the day 60, 85 and 90 messages (facts 06)

- **Founding 25 (applications open on kabsi.co/founding from 27 Oct 2026, Web 2):** three months of Kabsi Pro, free, for 25 businesses, by application, not first come. It is for a business in the UAE, the UK or the US with a verified Google profile with at least 10 reviews and a place customers visit, whose owner is on WhatsApp and says yes to a 2-minute check-in every two weeks and to a case study (named or anonymous; the owner approves the final text, and it uses only numbers the owner chooses to share). The places: 10 for beauty and hair, 5 for food and drink, 5 for health, 5 for other trades; a place still open 30 days after applications open goes to the next good applicant of any trade. The application asks for the business, the country, the kind of business, the WhatsApp number, why now, named or anonymous case study (anonymous unless the owner picks named; the words the owner agrees to say which), and the two yeses. Kabsi answers within 2 working days. The 90 days start when Kabsi's access to the Google profile works, with no card. Kabsi writes on WhatsApp on day 60 and day 85; on day 90 the owner chooses a plan at the founding price ($19 or $39 a month, kept while the subscription stays paid) or stops. Nothing is charged by itself, and the owner can leave any time. The number of places left shown anywhere is the real count. The application page is kabsi.co/founding; Home, Pricing and every industry page show the Founding 25 banner with the real number of places left. (Partners session, 26 Oct 2026, master plan 30.17 section 1; page by Web 2, 27 Oct 2026.)
