### How it works

**Does Kabsi post replies by itself?**
No. Kabsi drafts a reply and emails it to you. Nothing goes on your Google profile until you tap Approve reply, and you see the exact text first.

**What does Kabsi need from me?**
Add the Kabsi group ID 5481006796 as a Manager on your Google Business Profile, then tell Kabsi a few facts in About your business. Drafts only use the facts you give.

**How long does setup take?**
A few minutes: find your business, add the Kabsi group ID 5481006796 as a Manager, fill in About your business, choose a plan.

**How fast do new reviews reach me?**
Kabsi checks every few minutes. Reviews of 3 stars or less, and any review Kabsi marks medium or high risk, are emailed right away; other reviews are emailed at once while your business is open and come together in the daily email, at the hour you choose, outside your opening hours. Nothing is emailed between 22:00 and 08:00; it waits until 08:00.

**Can I reply from my phone?**
Yes. Tap Approve reply, Edit or Skip in the email, confirm on the page that opens, or use the app in your phone's browser. A high-risk review asks you to sign in on that page first.

**Can I edit a draft before posting?**
Yes. Change anything you like. Kabsi publishes exactly the text you approve.

**Can I ask Kabsi to rewrite a draft?**
Yes. Tap Other options, then choose Shorter, More formal, Warmer, Say sorry more clearly or Regenerate, or type what to change and tap New version. Up to 3 new versions per review per day.

**What happens with hard reviews?**
Kabsi gives every review one of three risk levels. Rules in code look first, then a small AI model, and the higher level wins.
- Low (normal praise or mild feedback): a draft as usual.
- Medium (a complaint, a refund request, a complaint about staff, something said about your business that may be wrong, any rating of 1 to 3, and every review of a medical, dental, legal or financial business): a careful draft, with the issue shown above it, for example "They mention a refund. Kabsi didn't promise one."
- High (a legal threat, discrimination, illness, injury, an allergy or another safety problem, a threat, harassment, privacy or personal data, a regulator or an inspection, an accusation of a crime): no draft. The review is marked "This review needs a little more care" with a short checklist, and the email has the checklist and no approve button. If you want, Kabsi writes a short, neutral holding reply, only when you ask for it. You can also write your own, choose "I'll reply myself" or skip it.

**Does Kabsi treat clinics, law firms and financial businesses differently?**
Yes. For a health, dental, legal or financial business (from your Google category), every review is at least medium risk, and a reply never says the reviewer was a patient or client and never mentions a visit, an appointment or a treatment. Kabsi checks each draft for this before you see it.

**What does "I'll reply myself" do?**
It marks the review "You replied yourself", and Kabsi won't post anything for it. It is under Other options, next to Skip ("You chose not to reply").

**What about my old reviews?**
When Kabsi first reads your reviews, it drafts replies for the most recent reviews without a reply: up to 10 during the free trial (the others wait for your plan) and up to 20 on a paid plan. Home then says "We found 23 unanswered reviews. Replies are ready." For the low-risk ones you can see the full list and approve them together: you approve exactly the replies you saw, and Kabsi sends them at most 5 a minute, with a progress line ("14 of 23 published"). Reviews that need a look you read one by one, and reviews that need more care get no draft. Nothing is posted until you approve it. [TIME-SENSITIVE] Drafting every old review on a paid plan is built but switched on with the live Google switch.

**Does Kabsi reply to reviews with no text?**
Yes. It drafts a short thank-you for the rating, which you approve like any other.

**Can other people get the review emails?**
Yes. Add up to 3 extra addresses in Settings, Emails. They see new reviews, but only you can post.

**Can I pause emails while on holiday?**
Yes. Settings, Emails, Pause for 7 days. Reviews still arrive in your Kabsi inbox.

**What is the weekly report?**
Every Monday at 08:00 local time: how many things Kabsi took care of that week, your public Google rating and change, new reviews, how many got a reply, how often your review link or QR code was opened, what your profile did for customers that week, what needs you, and an estimate of the time Kabsi saved you (5 minutes a reply, 15 an update, 10 a batch of photos, 10 a profile check that found something; never money). Facts only, with no quotes from customers' reviews.

### Replies and AI

**Who writes the replies?**
Kabsi's AI drafts them from your facts. You read, edit and approve each one.

**Is there a limit on drafts?**
Yes, a daily one, so a flood of reviews or a very long review can't run up costs. Each business can get up to 60 drafts a day (replies and posts together). If you reach it, Kabsi says "Kabsi has written as many drafts as it can for this business today. New drafts start again tomorrow." Reviews that arrive meanwhile wait and are drafted the next day. Very long reviews are read up to Google's 4,096-character length.

**Will a reply include my phone number, email or website?**
No. Google can reject replies that contain phone numbers, emails, links, social media handles or hashtags, so Kabsi's drafts never include them, and a draft that does is stopped before you see it as ready. Prices appear only if you wrote them in About your business. An unhappy customer is asked to get in touch through the details on your profile. Posts follow the same rule: a post's button carries the link or the call.

**What if the AI makes a mistake?**
You see the exact text before anything is posted and can edit or skip it. Drafts use only your facts and pass a safety check first.

**Does Kabsi learn from my edits?**
Only when you say so. When you change a draft the same way three times (for example making it shorter), Kabsi asks once "Save this as a rule?". When you add a fact to a reply (for example "We now open on Sundays"), it asks once "Save this as a fact?". **Save** adds it to About your business as a verified fact; **No thanks** and Kabsi never asks about it again. Reviews are never used as facts.

**Will replies sound robotic?**
Drafts are two to four short sentences, mention something the customer actually said, and use your sign-off and tone (Warm, Formal or Short & friendly). Kabsi also looks at your last five approved replies to match how you write, and stops a draft that uses stock phrases such as "thrilled to hear" or that is too close to a reply you already posted. Drafts have no exclamation marks.

**Can replies make things up?**
Drafts use only the Verified facts in About your business. A missing fact is left out, never guessed. A suggestion you have not confirmed, a fact that is out of date, or a fact with two different answers is left out too. If a reviewer asks about a person who is not in your facts (for example "Is Maria still working there?"), the draft does not say either way. Kabsi checks every name, price, opening time and service in a draft against your facts before you see it.

**Will a reply offer discounts or refunds?**
No. Drafts never offer discounts, refunds, vouchers, free items or compensation, and a code check blocks drafts that do.

**Will a reply admit fault?**
No. Drafts don't admit fault or liability and never argue.

**Can I stop Kabsi saying certain things?**
Yes. List them under "Anything Kabsi should never say or promise" in About your business. Drafts, posts and the safety check follow it.

**Can replies mention my staff?**
Only names you add to the staff list, and only when the customer mentioned them.

**Can someone trick the AI with a fake review?**
Review text is treated as data, never instructions. Kabsi ignores commands inside a review and answers only the real content.

**Which languages does Kabsi reply in?**
The reviewer's language: English, Spanish, Arabic, French and many others.

**What about Franco-Arabic?**
Arabic written in Latin letters gets a simple English reply, because model-written Franco reads badly in public.

**Is the app in other languages?**
Not yet. The app and website are in English. Reply drafts are in each reviewer's language.

**Does Kabsi use my data to train AI?**
No. Google data is never used to train AI, and Anthropic, which drafts the replies, does not train on it.

### Google Protection

**What is Google Protection?**
Kabsi watches your business name, phone, website, address, opening hours, main category, open status (open, temporarily closed, permanently closed) and map pin, and compares them with the details you confirmed. When Google shows something different, you get one email that explains it, for example "Google shows Sunday closing at 18:00. You approved 22:00.", and says whether Google made the change itself or Kabsi does not know who did. In the app you choose: "Google is right" (Google's value becomes your approved detail and nothing is sent to Google) or "Keep my information" (Kabsi sends your approved value back to Google, then checks Google shows it). Nothing changes on Google unless you say so, and Kabsi never puts anything back by itself. A change waiting for the owner appears as a card on Home.

**Can Google Protection lock my listing?**
No. Nobody can lock a Google listing or stop people suggesting edits. Kabsi tells you fast and puts your version back only if you say so.

**How fast will I hear about a change?**
Kabsi checks about once an hour once connected, and emails you when a detail changes.

**What if the change was mine?**
Tap "Google is right". Google's value becomes your approved version.

**Why does Kabsi sometimes recommend "Google is right"?**
Google removes things from business names that break its naming rules: a web address, a city or area, a slogan or extra keywords. When that is what Google removed (for example "Yawmiyati.com" to "Yawmiyati"), Kabsi recommends "Google is right" and says why: business names on Google can't include them, and putting them back can lead to a suspension. "Keep my information" is still there, with that warning.

**Why do I have to sign in for some changes?**
Your business name, address, main category, open status and map pin can make Google ask you to verify your business again when they change. Kabsi asks you to sign in to choose these (an email link alone is not enough; the email page asks you to sign in and brings you back), only the owner can choose, and the app says "Changing this can make Google ask you to verify your business again." Kabsi also sends at most one of these details back to Google at a time, at least 7 days apart; a second one inside the 7 days waits, and the app says the date it can go.

**What if Google keeps changing the same detail?**
Kabsi puts the same detail back at most twice in 30 days. If Google changes it a third time, Kabsi does not send it again: a person on the Kabsi team contacts Google Business Profile support for you, with an explanation ready.

**What does "Replaced by a newer change" or "No answer in 14 days" mean?**
"Replaced by a newer change": Google changed the same detail again before you chose, so Kabsi shows you the newest change instead; nothing was decided for you. "No answer in 14 days": nobody chose within 14 days, so the change is closed without a decision and Kabsi does not email about that same value again. Nothing was put back or kept for you in either case.

**Which details count as approved by me?**
Only details you confirmed yourself in Kabsi, or where you chose "Google is right". Values Kabsi read from Google are "Not yet confirmed by you" until you confirm them: Kabsi still watches them and tells you when they change, but never offers to put an unconfirmed value back. Everything you saved in About your business counts as confirmed by you.
