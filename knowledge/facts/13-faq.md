### How it works

**Do I have to do anything?**
Tap yes. Kabsi prepares the reply, the fix or the post, and you approve it on WhatsApp or email.

**Can I ask Kabsi to change something?**
Yes. Message it in plain words or send a voice note: your hours, a photo, a post, a reply. Kabsi shows you exactly what will change before anything goes to Google.

**What if a bad review comes in?**
Kabsi tells you at once and writes a calm reply for you to approve or change.

**Does Kabsi post replies by itself?**
No. Kabsi drafts a reply and sends it to you, on WhatsApp or by email, whichever you chose. Nothing goes on your Google profile until you tap Approve reply, and you see the exact text first. Automatic replies are off.

**What does Kabsi need from me?**
Add the Kabsi group ID 5481006796 as a Manager on your Google Business Profile, then tell Kabsi a few facts in About your business. Drafts only use the facts you give.

**How long does setup take?**
A few minutes, in three steps: find your business, add Kabsi as a manager on Google (the group ID 5481006796, never a password), and choose WhatsApp or email.

**How long does the free check take?**
About 20 seconds. It shows your rating and review count beside 3 nearby businesses, your listing basics (hours, phone, website, category, photos), whether your website and booking link load now, the reply Kabsi would write for your latest review, and what Kabsi would do each month. There is no score. It needs no sign-up, no access and no card.

**How fast do new reviews reach me?**
Kabsi checks every few minutes. You choose one place for new reviews, WhatsApp or email, and a review that needs care still also comes by email. Reviews of 3 stars or less, and any review Kabsi marks medium or high risk, are emailed right away; other reviews are emailed at once while your business is open and come together in the daily email, at the hour you choose, outside your opening hours. Nothing is emailed between 22:00 and 08:00; it waits until 08:00.

**Can I reply from my phone?**
Yes. Tap Approve reply, Edit or Skip in the email (or on WhatsApp), confirm on the page that opens, or use Kabsi in your phone's browser. A high-risk review asks you to sign in on that page first.

**Can I edit a draft before posting?**
Yes. Change anything you like. Kabsi publishes exactly the text you approve.

**Can I ask Kabsi to rewrite a draft?**
Yes. Tap Other options, then choose Shorter, More formal, Warmer, Say sorry more clearly or Regenerate, or type what to change and tap New version. Up to 3 new versions per review per day.

**What happens with hard reviews?**
Kabsi gives every review one of three risk levels. Rules in code look first, then a small AI model, and the higher level wins.
- Low (normal praise or mild feedback): a draft as usual.
- Medium (a complaint, a refund request, a complaint about staff that names no one, something said about your business that may be wrong, any rating of 1 to 3, and every review of a medical, dental, legal or financial business): a careful draft, with the issue shown above it, for example "They mention a refund. Kabsi didn't promise one."
- High (a legal threat, discrimination, illness, injury, an allergy or another safety problem, a threat, harassment, privacy or personal data, a regulator or an inspection, an accusation of a crime, a complaint that names a member of your team): no draft. The review is marked "This review needs a little more care" with a short checklist, and the email has the checklist and no approve button. If you want, Kabsi writes a short, neutral holding reply, only when you ask for it. You can also write your own, choose "I'll reply myself" or skip it.

**Does Kabsi treat clinics, law firms and financial businesses differently?**
Yes. For a health, dental, legal or financial business (from your Google category), every review is at least medium risk, and a reply never says the reviewer was a patient or client and never mentions a visit, an appointment or a treatment. Kabsi checks each draft for this before you see it.

**What does "I'll reply myself" do?**
It marks the review "You replied yourself", and Kabsi won't post anything for it. It is under Other options, next to Skip ("You chose not to reply").

**What about my old reviews?**
On your first day, every unanswered review from the last 90 days arrives with a reply ready to approve. Home then says, for example, "We found 23 unanswered reviews. Replies are ready." For the low-risk ones you can see the full list and approve them together: you approve exactly the replies you saw, and Kabsi sends them at most 5 a minute, with a progress line ("14 of 23 published"). Reviews that need a look you read one by one, and reviews that need more care get no draft. Nothing is posted until you approve it.

**Does Kabsi reply to reviews with no text?**
Yes. It drafts a short thank-you for the rating, which you approve like any other.

**Can other people get the review emails?**
Yes. Add up to 3 extra addresses in Settings, Emails. They see new reviews, but only you can post.

**Can I pause emails while on holiday?**
Yes. Settings, Emails, Pause for 7 days. Reviews still arrive in your Kabsi inbox.

**What is the weekly report?**
A short summary every week, 3 to 5 lines: replies that are live, anything waiting for your yes, anything Kabsi found, and taps on your review links. It is counted from the record of what Kabsi did, with no quotes from customers' reviews. A full report every month is coming: the work done, what Kabsi found and what Google shows, kept apart. Nothing in either says Kabsi caused a review, a ranking or revenue.

### Replies and AI

**Who writes the replies?**
Kabsi's AI drafts them from your facts. You read, edit and approve each one.

**Is there a limit on drafts?**
Yes, a daily one, so a flood of reviews or a very long review can't run up costs. Each business can get up to 60 drafts a day (replies and posts together). If you reach it, Kabsi says "Kabsi has written as many drafts as it can for this business today. New drafts start again tomorrow." Reviews that arrive meanwhile wait and are drafted the next day. Very long reviews are read up to Google's 4,096-character length.

**Will a reply include my phone number, email or website?**
No. Google can reject replies that contain phone numbers, emails, links, social media handles or hashtags, so Kabsi's drafts never include them, and a draft that does is stopped before you see it as ready. Prices appear only if you wrote them in About your business. An unhappy customer is asked to get in touch through the details on your profile. Posts follow the same rule: a post's button carries the link or the call.

**What if the AI makes a mistake?**
You see the exact text before anything is posted and can edit or skip it. Drafts use only your facts and pass a safety check first.

**Does Kabsi learn from my edits?**
Only when you say so. When you change a draft the same way three times (for example making it shorter), Kabsi asks once "Save this as a rule?". When you add a fact to a reply (for example "We now open on Sundays"), it asks once "Save this as a fact?". **Save** adds it to About your business as a verified fact; **No thanks** and Kabsi never asks about it again. Reviews are never used as facts.

**Will replies sound robotic?**
Drafts are two to four short sentences, mention something the customer actually said, and use your sign-off and tone (Warm, Formal or Short & friendly). Kabsi also looks at your last five approved replies to match how you write, and stops a draft that uses stock phrases such as "thrilled to hear" or that is too close to a reply you already posted. Drafts have no exclamation marks.

**Can replies make things up?**
Drafts use only the Verified facts in About your business. A missing fact is left out, never guessed. A suggestion you have not confirmed, a fact that is out of date, or a fact with two different answers is left out too. If a reviewer asks about a person who is not in your facts (for example "Is Maria still working there?"), the draft does not say either way. Kabsi checks every name, price, opening time and service in a draft against your facts before you see it.

**Will a reply offer discounts or refunds?**
No. Drafts never offer discounts, refunds, vouchers, free items or compensation, and a code check blocks drafts that do.

**Will a reply admit fault?**
No. Drafts don't admit fault or liability and never argue.

**Can I stop Kabsi saying certain things?**
Yes. List them under "Anything Kabsi should never say or promise" in About your business. Drafts, posts and the safety check follow it.

**Can replies mention my staff?**
Only names you add to the staff list, and only when the customer mentioned them.

**Can someone trick the AI with a fake review?**
Review text is treated as data, never instructions. Kabsi ignores commands inside a review and answers only the real content.

**Which languages does Kabsi reply in?**
The reviewer's language: English, Spanish, French and many others.

**What about chat spelling in Latin letters?**
A review in chat spelling, a language written in Latin letters the way people type it, gets a simple English reply, because a model-written reply in that spelling reads badly in public.

**Is the app in other languages?**
Not yet. Kabsi is in English. Reply drafts are in each reviewer's language.

**Does Kabsi use my data to train AI?**
No. Google data is never used to train AI, and Anthropic, which drafts the replies, does not train on it.

### Google Protection

**What is Google Protection?**
Kabsi watches your business name, phone, website, address, opening hours, main category, open status (open, temporarily closed, permanently closed) and map location, and compares them with the details you confirmed. When Google shows something different, you get one email that explains it, for example "Google shows Sunday closing at 18:00. You approved 22:00.", and says whether Google made the change itself or Kabsi does not know who did. On Kabsi you choose: "Google is right" (Google's value becomes your approved detail and nothing is sent to Google) or "Keep my information" (Kabsi sends your approved value back to Google, then checks Google shows it). Nothing changes on Google unless you say so, and Kabsi never puts anything back by itself. A change waiting for the owner appears as a card on Home.

**Can Google Protection lock my Google profile?**
No. Nobody can lock a Google profile or stop people suggesting edits. Kabsi tells you fast and puts your version back only if you say so.

**How fast will I hear about a change?**
Kabsi checks about once an hour once connected, and emails you when a detail changes.

**What if the change was mine?**
Tap "Google is right". Google's value becomes your approved version.

**Why does Kabsi sometimes recommend "Google is right"?**
Google removes things from business names that break its naming rules: a web address, a city or area, a slogan or extra keywords. When that is what Google removed (for example "Rosa Bakery.com" to "Rosa Bakery"), Kabsi recommends "Google is right" and says why: business names on Google can't include them, and putting them back can lead to a suspension. "Keep my information" is still there, with that warning.

**Why do I have to sign in for some changes?**
Your business name, address, main category, open status and map location can make Google ask you to verify your business again when they change. Kabsi asks you to sign in to choose these (an email link alone is not enough; the email page asks you to sign in and brings you back), only the owner can choose, and Kabsi says "Changing this can make Google ask you to verify your business again." Kabsi also sends at most one of these details back to Google at a time, at least 7 days apart; a second one inside the 7 days waits, and Kabsi says the date it can go.

**What if Google keeps changing the same detail?**
Kabsi puts the same detail back at most twice in 30 days. If Google changes it a third time, Kabsi does not send it again: a person on the Kabsi team contacts Google Business Profile support for you, with an explanation ready.

**What does "Replaced by a newer change" or "No answer in 14 days" mean?**
"Replaced by a newer change": Google changed the same detail again before you chose, so Kabsi shows you the newest change instead; nothing was decided for you. "No answer in 14 days": nobody chose within 14 days, so the change is closed without a decision and Kabsi does not email about that same value again. Nothing was put back or kept for you in either case.

**Which details count as approved by me?**
Only details you confirmed yourself in Kabsi, or where you chose "Google is right". Values Kabsi read from Google are "Not yet confirmed by you" until you confirm them: Kabsi still watches them and tells you when they change, but never offers to put an unconfirmed value back. Everything you saved in About your business counts as confirmed by you.

### Reviews on Google

**Can Kabsi remove bad reviews?**
No. Nobody outside Google can remove a review. Kabsi helps you answer every review calmly. You can report reviews that break Google's rules to Google.

**How do I report a fake review?**
On your Business Profile, Read reviews, the Report icon next to the review, choose the reason, Send report. Or use Google's Reviews Management Tool (section 8.4).

**How long does Google take to decide on a report?**
Usually several days. If the review stays up, you can appeal once.

**Someone offered to remove my bad reviews for a fee. Should I pay?**
Nobody outside Google can remove a review. Report reviews that break Google's rules yourself, for free.

**Should I reply to every review?**
Readers see that you listen. Kabsi makes it easy by drafting every reply.

**How long does Google take to show my reply?**
Google checks each reply first, usually within 10 minutes, and sometimes up to 30 days.

**Can I edit a reply after posting?**
Yes, on your Google profile. Google lets you edit or delete your reply later.

### Questions owners in the UAE and the UK ask (credit sprint, 8 Nov 2026)

**Can I pay in dirhams or pounds?**
No. Prices are in US dollars only, and Kabsi shows no converted price. A card in dirhams or pounds is charged in US dollars; the card's bank converts it at its own rate and may add its own fee. Card payments are not open yet.

**Is VAT added in the UAE or the UK?**
Prices are plus tax where it applies. Creem, the merchant of record, works out any sales tax or VAT at its checkout; Kabsi does not set the rate and does not state one. What the invoice or receipt shows, such as a company name or a VAT number, is shown on Creem's receipt; the detail is confirmed before card payments open. For your company's details, write to hello@kabsi.co before you pay.

**Does my second branch cost the same?**
Yes. Each location is its own plan at 29 US dollars a month, the same for everyone.

**Can I pay with Apple Pay, or in instalments with Tabby?**
Cards work, through Creem's checkout. Creem lists Apple Pay and Google Pay as supported, so one may appear at checkout; what shows depends on the product, your country and your device, so it may appear and may not. Kabsi offers no pay-later plans such as Tabby. Card payments are not open yet.

**Does WhatsApp work with WhatsApp Business?**
Yes. Kabsi messages the phone number you opted in with, so the messages arrive in whichever WhatsApp app uses that number, WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.

**Can my manager approve on WhatsApp while I am away, for example over Eid?**
Yes, for what you let managers approve. In Settings, Team, turn a kind on (review replies, for example), and a manager who has opted in their own phone can approve, undo and edit those drafts on WhatsApp. Billing stays with the owner.

**Can I pause my plan while we close for a season?**
There is no pause. Cancel with one tap in Settings, or tell Kabsi on WhatsApp, and the plan runs to the end of the month you paid for; choose a plan again when you reopen. For the weeks you are closed, set Closed for a while under Hours.

**Does Kabsi know UAE National Day and Eid?**
Yes. Kabsi asks about UAE public holidays from the official UAE list: National Day is 2 and 3 December. Eid and the other Islamic holidays follow the moon, so their dates are estimates until they are announced. Kabsi asks five days before and never assumes your hours.

**Which data law does Kabsi follow, UK GDPR or the UAE's?**
Kabsi does not claim to comply with any named law. The privacy page (kabsi.co/privacy) says what Kabsi keeps, for how long, where it is processed, and how to see, correct or delete it. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time, and for a question about a specific law, write to hello@kabsi.co.
