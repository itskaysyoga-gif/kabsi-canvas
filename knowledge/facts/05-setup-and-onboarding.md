## 3. Setup and onboarding

### 3.1 Before you start

- A **verified** Google Business Profile on Google Maps that you **own or manage**, so you can add a Manager. Kabsi cannot check verification itself, so setup asks you. If "People and access" is missing, the listing may need to be verified or claimed first.
- A Google account or an email address you check (sign-in is with Google or an emailed code, no password).
- A few minutes.

### 3.2 Signing in

Signing up and logging in are the same steps. After the free check on /get-started, tap **Save your snapshot and continue**; the business you checked is kept, so setup starts with it already picked.

- **Continue with Google** (first choice): pick your Google account. Kabsi receives only your name and email address (openid, email and profile). It gives Kabsi no access to your Business Profile; that comes later through the Manager invitation.
- **Continue with email**: enter your email, wait a moment for the quick bot check (usually there is nothing to click), tap **Send me a code**, then type the 6-digit code from the email (there is no sign-in link). The code works for 10 minutes. Not there? Check spam, wait for the timer and tap **Send a new code**, or **Use a different email**.
- Limits that stop inbox flooding: at most 3 codes per email address in 15 minutes and 10 code requests per device or network in an hour. Throwaway email addresses (for example mailinator.com) cannot sign up. There are no passwords.

### 3.3 The setup steps (/start)

**Four stages (UX 1, 27 Oct 2026; this replaces the six steps described after it, whose situation screens and Need a hand panel still apply).** A bar at the top names them: **Your free check**, **Sign in**, **Connect WhatsApp**, **Add Kabsi on Google**. The free check comes first with no sign-up ("Here is your free check. Nothing has changed on Google, and Kabsi has no access yet."), and the trial is explained apart from it: free for 14 days once Kabsi is connected, no card. After signing in, the owner confirms "Is this your business?" and who looks after its Google profile (I do; someone else does it for me; I am not sure, and Kabsi helps find out). Then WhatsApp: the owner sends Kabsi one message with the code shown on the page, and the page updates by itself when it arrives. Then Manager access, with three states shown ("Your invite on Google", "Accepted by Kabsi", "Access checked"), the same access steps as everywhere (ID 5481006796, "Copy the ID"), help from Rashid, the founder, on request, and the trial starting only when access is checked ("Your free trial starts today and ends on [date]."). Then the first task, and only after it the rest of setup (competitors, extra searches, tone, AI questions, team and photos), one thing at a time. A setup link (kabsi.co/start/resume/CODE, for the signed-in owner only) opens the next unfinished step on any phone or computer.

Before UX 1, setup had six steps, with a bar of six at the top (on a phone it reads "Step 3 of 6: Connect"): **1 Check** (the free Google Profile Check), **2 Sign up**, **3 Connect** (add Kabsi as a Manager, with a live status), **4 About you** (five quick questions), **5 Confirm** (confirm your details once access works), **6 Trial** (the Pro trial runs). Steps 1 and 2 come before there is an account. The target is your first approved reply within 10 minutes of access working.

**Need a hand?** Every setup screen, from the free check to confirming your details, has a **Need a hand?** button under its main button, in the same place each time. It opens a panel (from the bottom on a phone, from the side on a computer) with, in this order: **Ask Nora**, with three suggested questions for that exact step and a box for your own (Nora is Kabsi's AI assistant, marked AI; she knows which of the six steps you are on, whether Google access works and which situation you reported, never asks for your Google password or a verification code, and says so if asked); **Watch it**, a 20 second clip of the step, only when there is one; and, from the Google access step on, **Book a free 20-minute setup call**. Before sign-up it offers Nora only. The panel never opens by itself. If you look stuck, one line appears above the button, once per step: "Stuck? I can walk you through it, or book a free setup call." It shows after 3 minutes on the Manager step without the invitation, after a failed access check, or when one of the situation screens opens. When Nora cannot solve something she offers the setup call or to pass it to the team, who reply by email; the team's note says which step you were on.

**Step 1: Find your business**

Search the name and city as on Google Maps (or name plus street or area), pick the result and tap **Yes, this is {business}**.

Then **One quick check**: "Kabsi works with a verified Google Business Profile that you own or manage. Is {business} yours on Google?"

- **Yes, I manage it and it's verified:** setup continues.
- **I'm not sure:** a 30-second check at business.google.com shows how to tell.
- **No, it isn't verified yet, or someone else manages it:** setup shows how to verify or ask for access on Google, and waits until then. Nothing is saved before you answer yes.

A computer shows a real screenshot on each card (taken 4 October 2026) and a phone shows plain steps; **Show me the phone version** or **Show me the computer version** switches. In the Google Maps app: tap your profile picture, then **Your business profiles**; choose the business, then **⋮** or **Profile settings**, then **People and access**; tap **Add**, paste the group ID, choose **Manager**, then **Invite**. On a computer: open your Business Profile, the menu next to the business name, **Business Profile settings**, **People and access**, **Add**, paste the group ID, choose **Manager**, **Invite**. The same steps everywhere on the website (kabsi.co/manager-steps, the free check, 27 Oct 2026, master plan 30.18 section 6): "On Google, open your profile (search your business name while signed in), then Menu, Business Profile settings, People and access, Add. Paste 5481006796, choose Manager, then Add (on some screens, Invite)." The group then shows as Pending until Kabsi accepts, usually within minutes. The page has a "Copy the ID" button, a short recording of the steps on a real profile (a phone recording on a phone, a computer recording on a larger screen), the three states (invite pending, accepted by Kabsi, access checked) and "Send these steps to whoever looks after your Google profile" (WhatsApp or email). 3. The status under the guide reads **Waiting for your invitation. We check every 30 seconds.** and turns green, **Invitation received. You're connected**, and the screen moves on by itself. While Kabsi is still in early access (see 2.2) a person on the Kabsi team accepts the invitation by hand, so it reads **We'll email you when access is ready** and you can close the page. When access works you get Kabsi's own access notice, "Kabsi is now a Manager of {business} on Google": what changed, what Kabsi can do, and how to remove Kabsi (Settings, **Disconnect Kabsi from Google**, or People and access). Kabsi sends this notice for every change it makes to its access to your profile, within 48 hours and separate from Google's own emails: by email, and on WhatsApp too when you connected WhatsApp. The Manager step and the access email also say: "Google Business Profile is free from Google. 4. Three ways out sit under the status. **Send these steps to my other device**: **Email them to me** sends one email to your own address with a link back to the step (at most 3 times a day), or **Share on WhatsApp** opens a share message with the link. **Someone else manages my Google listing**: a ready message to copy or send by WhatsApp or email to whoever manages the profile; it asks them to add Kabsi as a Manager and never asks for a password or code. **Book a free 20-minute setup call.** The page kabsi.co/manager-steps shows the same guide for anyone you send it to.
5. Tap **I've sent the invitation** (or **Continue** once connected), or **Do this later** and finish the other steps first.

**Something not working?** Under the guide, three questions help when Kabsi cannot see your profile: "Can you sign in to your Business Profile?", "Do you see a Get verified button?" and "Do you see a warning that your profile is suspended or limited?" (Yes or No). A list under them has the other situations. Each opens one screen with the same layout: what is happening in one sentence, at most four numbered steps, a button to Google's exact help page, **I've done it** and the free setup call link. The situations: **you're a manager, not an owner** (a ready message for the owner to add Kabsi); **your profile needs verifying** (what Google may ask, how to film a video: the street and sign outside, the inside, your tools, you opening the door or the till; Kabsi never asks for your verification code and neither does Google); **Google is reviewing your profile** (up to 5 business days, with the date it started; we email you when it is done); **your profile is suspended** (common causes, the evidence to gather, Google's reinstatement help; no promise about the outcome; no charge while it is suspended); **someone else holds the profile** (three cases: I lost my Google login, an old employee or agency has it with a ready message asking them to make you an owner, or I don't know who has it and you ask Google for ownership; Google gives the current owner 3 days to reply and in practice it can take up to 7, and Kabsi reminds you on day 3 and day 7); **no profile in this Google account** (try the right account first); **Kabsi can't look after this business** (Google only allows businesses that meet customers in person or travel to them; no trial is taken and nothing is charged); **several locations** (set up the first, then add each other for $15 a month or $150 a year, plus tax where it applies); **your organisation blocks adding people** (a ready message for the IT administrator). While you wait, Home says it in one line.

A Manager can do everything Kabsi needs; only owners can add or remove people. You stay the owner and can remove Kabsi any time, without asking us: in Kabsi (Settings, **Disconnect Kabsi from Google**) or on Google (People and access).

**Step 4: Five quick questions** (while Kabsi waits for access)

"While you wait for access: these make replies sound like you. Skip any of them and Kabsi uses safe defaults." Five questions, each saved as a fact you gave (Verified, from you), in Settings, About your business:

| Question | Saved as | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| How do you sign off a reply? (for example "Luca, Trattoria Verde") | How you sign replies | Every reply ends with it |
| How should replies sound? Three tappable examples: Warm, Formal, Short and friendly, each with a sample reply line | Tone | Sets the voice of drafts |
| How can an unhappy customer reach you? A phone or WhatsApp number | Business phone | Never written in a public reply. Unhappy customers are asked to contact you through the details on your Google profile, because Google can reject replies that contain phone numbers |
| One thing customers love about you | Things you'd like mentioned | Used only when a reviewer raises that topic |
| One thing Kabsi should never say or promise (for example "Never promise same-day delivery") | Never say | Drafts, posts and the safety check all follow it |

Beside them is a card, **Get your reviews on WhatsApp**. Until owners are switched on at launch it says **WhatsApp: coming soon** and offers nothing to tap (the number exists and is being tested); then it shows **Connect WhatsApp** (a one-time code you send from your phone, which is also your opt-in). It never blocks setup, and neither does any question: **Skip for now** moves on, and the answers can be added or changed later in Settings, About your business. **If you skip a question**, replies are signed with your business name, sound warm, ask unhappy customers to use the details on your Google profile, and nothing extra is added or avoided. Staff names, customer questions and everything else are added later in Settings.

**Settings, About your business** (each fact on its own line, with its status):

- **Verified**: you gave it or confirmed it. Only Verified facts are used in replies and posts.
- **Needs your OK**: a fact Kabsi did not get from you (a suggestion, or something read from your website once that is possible). Replies and posts leave it out until you tap **Confirm**. **Not right** turns it down and Kabsi does not suggest that value again.
- **Out of date**: Kabsi asks you to check it again. Prices are asked again after 90 days, staff names after 180 days, opening hours notes after 60 days. Until you tap **Confirm**, replies and posts leave it out.
- **Edit** saves a new version (the earlier one is kept as history). **Delete** removes the fact and every earlier version for good, and Kabsi stops using it.
- When two sources give different answers about something customers see (hours, phone, services, prices), Kabsi asks "Kabsi found two different answers. Which is right?" and leaves that fact out of replies and posts until you choose. It never picks one for you.

Never-say examples: "Don't confirm the reviewer is a patient or mention any treatment." "Don't promise same-day delivery." "Don't mention the old location."

**Step 5: Confirm your details** (once Kabsi has access)

Until access works this step says **Waiting for your invitation** with the live status, that waiting costs you no trial days, and **Go to Home**; nothing waits on a spinner. When access works, **Confirm your details** shows the six details Google shows customers: business name, phone, opening hours, website, main category and open status. Each has **Correct** (it is right) or **Change** (type the right value; for open status choose open, closed temporarily or closed permanently). Details you confirm show **Approved by you**, become the facts you approved and the baseline Google Protection keeps (see Google Protection); if Google later changes one, you decide what happens, and Kabsi writes nothing to Google here. A detail you leave alone stays as it is and shows **Needs your OK** in Google Profile, where **Approved by you** also shows next to each detail you approved. If Kabsi has not read the profile yet (it takes a few minutes), the step says so and **Skip for now** moves on. Then Home opens with the day-one backlog card ("We found 23 unanswered reviews. Replies are ready."), and the first reply you approve within 10 minutes of access is the goal.

**Step 6: The Pro trial runs**

Home shows a banner for the first days: "Your 14-day Pro trial has started. No card needed." (30 days when the owner came through a partner link). The trial starts only when Kabsi has working access to a verified profile, so waiting for access or for Google to verify a profile costs no days. The setup emails (welcome, day 2, day 7 your week, day 11 three days left) run as described in 4.3; from day 11 you choose Essentials or Pro, monthly or yearly, in Settings and in the email, and choosing a plan never interrupts approving a reply. From day 11 Home shows a card, "Your Pro trial ends in 3 days" (or "ends today"), that links to the Plan page; in the 7 days of grace it says "Your trial has ended", that Kabsi keeps watching and preparing replies but publishes nothing until a plan is chosen, and that whatever is waiting stays waiting; after an ended plan it says drafts, Google Protection and the Weekly Care Report are paused. Until card payments open each of these says "Card payments in US dollars are coming. Until they open there is nothing to pay." and offers no payment button and no price. Until card payments open (section 4.2) the plan page says "Card payments in US dollars are coming. Until they open there is nothing to pay." Your review link and QR code are free in every country.

### 3.4 After setup

Statuses: Setup not finished, Waiting for Google access, Free, Active. A business goes active when Google access works and a free trial or paid plan is running. It is Free when access works and no trial or plan is running. Kabsi then drafts the backlog replies (10 on a trial, 20 on a paid plan), takes the Google Protection snapshot and starts the weekly cycle.

### 3.5 Recommended first actions

1. Home: read "things need your attention" and start with the first card.
2. Get reviews: copy your review link and print the QR code (three steps).
3. Posts: under **Write a post**, write a first post and save it.
4. Settings, About your business: add a few customer questions and never-say items.
5. Settings, Emails: choose your daily email time and time zone, and which notices reach you by email and in the bell.
