## 5. Review links and cards

A review link, QR code or NFC card opens the business's own Google review page. No rating screen, no filtering, no reward: every customer sees the same page. The public wording is: "Ask every customer the same way, as they visit. Never offer a reward."

### 5.1 Free for everyone: review links and QR codes

**Inside Kabsi (Get reviews, three steps)**

1. **Copy your review link.** Tap **Create my review link**: a short link (go.kabsi.co/{CODE}) and a QR code. Tap **Copy review link**.
2. **Print your QR code.** **Download QR** (SVG) or **Print** (a page saying "Leave us a Google review").
3. **Optional: use your own NFC card or tag.** If you own an NFC card, tag or sticker, write the same link on it with a free NFC writer app (section 5.4). Kabsi generates the link and the QR code; the tag is yours.
You can have up to 5 links per business (**Create another link**), for example one per spot, to compare opens. Each shows its opens for 7 days and can be switched off or on. Under each link, **When someone opens this link** lets you choose **Go straight to Google** (the default) or **Show the page** (the Google review button first, plus "Need help now? Message the manager" when you added a WhatsApp number in About your business). Everyone sees the same thing.

Share it in your own messages, Instagram bio, receipts, menus and follow-up emails.

**Without signing in: the free tool (/google-review-link)**

Type the business name and city: the box suggests businesses as you type, the same suggestions as the free check (Scan 3, 5 Nov 2026, master plan 30.34 item 3). After choosing, a card shows the business's name and address with **Change business**, so the owner always confirms or changes it; a search without a suggestion lists the matches and never chooses for the owner. Then a review link for the business's Google profile (search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=...), a message to copy, and a review card: the business name, "Scan to review us on Google", a charcoal QR code with rounded dots in a yellow frame and kabsi.co small at the foot, with no stars, no Google logo and nothing that asks for a rating. The card downloads as a PNG and as a printable A6 PDF (105 by 148 mm); on a phone the link can be shared. Free, no sign-up, up to 20 searches per visitor per hour. Kabsi doesn't count opens of this Google link. Not found? Try the exact Maps name plus city. Kabsi limits how many business searches it runs a day, so Google's search costs stay small. If the day's limit is 0 or used up, the tool, like the free Google Profile Check, says "The free check is busy today. Try again tomorrow, or start your free trial." with **Start your free trial** as the button; the business search in setup says "Back soon". They work again the next day (UTC); nothing the owner has set up is affected.

**The free tool tells the truth (Launch 2, 6 Nov 2026, master plan 30.36 item 7).** **Copy link** says "Copied" only when the copy worked; when the browser refuses it, the link is selected and one line says "Press and hold the link to copy it." on a phone or "Press Ctrl+C or Cmd+C to copy it." on a computer. **Test the link**, beside Copy, opens the review link in a new tab, with the line "Check it opens your business. Google may ask customers to sign in before they write a review." When Google's name for the business has letters the card cannot print (the card prints Latin letters only, and Kabsi shows no Arabic on its site), one field, **Name on the card**, asks for the name to print ("Type the business name in English letters as you want it printed."); the card, the PNG, the PDF and the message use that name, and the downloads wait until it has letters. A download is counted only when the file was made; when a phone or browser cannot draw the card, the page says "The card could not be made on this device. Try the PDF, or another browser." When the day's searches are used up the tool says "The review link tool is busy right now." and "Try again in a few minutes.", with the trial button.

**Without signing in: the free Google Profile Check (/google-profile-check)**

Search a business by name and city and see what customers see on Google, from public information only: rating, number of reviews, opening hours and whether it is open now, phone, website, category and whether the profile has photos. It does not use the owner's Google profile and changes nothing. The page headline is "See what customers see on Google, and what needs fixing." The result is a list of status flags, each a plain fact and what Kabsi would do about it: **Needs fixing** (red: the business shown as closed, or no opening hours), **Worth fixing** (amber: no phone number, website, category or photos shown, or fewer than 10 reviews) and **After you connect** (grey). There is no score, no ranking, no statistic and no claim about lost customers, and no comparison with other businesses. Public information does not include which reviews have a reply, whether holiday hours are set, when photos and posts were added, the description or the owner's posts, so the check never reports a count of unanswered reviews or a gap in posts: the grey flags say Google does not show it and that Kabsi reads it once the owner connects, and the owner approves every change. **Email me this check** sends the result to the address given, at most twice a day to one address (after that: "We already sent checks to this email today. Try again tomorrow."). **Send my check to WhatsApp** appears while Kabsi has a WhatsApp number (it has had one since 23 Oct 2026; with none the page shows only the email option): the visitor types their number with the country code and ticks a box that says "Send my Google Profile Check to this WhatsApp number as one message. I understand Kabsi will not message me again unless I ask."; Kabsi then sends one message with the result and nothing after it, and keeps a one-way hash of the number (never the number), the Place ID, those words, the page and the time as proof (a number can get one check a day, a visitor three an hour; the message is a Meta-approved template). a box, unticked, says "Send me tips on managing my Google profile" and tips are sent only if it is ticked (Kabsi keeps the address, the business's Google Place ID, the choice, those words, the page and the time as proof). Free, no sign-up, 3 checks a day per visitor (the fourth is refused: "You have used your 3 free checks for today. Please come back tomorrow."), up to 10 requests a minute, and a bot check. Kabsi does not keep Google's public details for a business: each check, and the email or WhatsApp copy of it, reads them from Google again, and only the business's Google Place ID is kept. When the daily limit on Google lookups is 0 or used up, or the check is paused, the page says "The free check is busy today. Try again tomorrow, or start your free trial." and the button is **Start your free trial**. The old setup screens at /get-started are gone (Launch 2, 6 Nov 2026): that address opens the free check. Business details come from Google Maps. Kabsi is not partnered with Google.

### 5.2 Your own NFC cards and tags

Kabsi sells no NFC cards, anywhere. The review link and QR code work with your own QR codes and NFC cards: buy any blank NFC card or sticker online (chip type NTAG213 or NTAG215) and write your Kabsi review link onto it yourself (section 5.4), or simply print the QR code. Kabsi works 100% digitally.

**A Kabsi card you already have**

Kabsi cards that were sold before 21 Oct 2026 keep working. Kabsi itself issues no new ones. Since Cards 2 (11 Nov 2026), Kabsi Partners make Kabsi card links on their partner home and write them on the review cards they sell (section 5.3).

- Tap a card that isn't linked ("This Kabsi card isn't set up yet"), sign in and link it; or in Get reviews, **Add a Kabsi card**, type the 6-character code under the QR, tap **Add**.
- Name cards ("Counter", "Table 4") with **Save name**. **Switch off** stops a card within about a minute; it then shows "This review link isn't active. Please ask the staff for help."
- **On WhatsApp (Cards 2, 11 Nov 2026):** the page of a card that isn't linked has **Set up on WhatsApp**, which opens a chat with "Link card ABC234" written in. Kabsi answers "Link card ABC234 to [business]?" with one button, **Yes, link it**; an owner or manager with several businesses picks the business instead. Then Kabsi asks where the card is: **Front desk**, **Till** or **Table**, or type a place, like Bar. A name is a place, never a person: a person's name or job ("Sam's till", "Waiter") is refused, and a thank-you is never taken as a place. A number Kabsi doesn't know gets a sign-in link that keeps the code, and the card links as soon as the business is added. A card linked to another business reads as not found, and its business is never named. Staff of a business can't link cards; Kabsi asks them to ask the owner or a manager. 10 wrong codes in an hour stop card codes from that number for an hour.
- **"cards" on WhatsApp** lists each card with its place and its taps this week (from Monday) and last week. Tap a card to change its place. A tap is not a review: Kabsi counts taps, never reviews from a card, and never ranks people or sets anyone a target.
- **The weekly report** (page and email) has a block "From your Kabsi cards" when the business has a card: each card's place and its taps that week, with "A tap is not a review." Taps from bots are left out.

### 5.3 Kabsi card links from Kabsi Partners (Cards 2, 11 Nov 2026)

- Kabsi sells no cards. A partner who sells review cards makes Kabsi card links on kabsi.co/partner, in batches of 10, 25 or 50 (200 to start, until Kabsi raises the number), and writes one link (go.kabsi.co/ABC234) on each card.
- The partner sets each card's business with the same business search as the free review link tool. Kabsi keeps only the business's Google Place ID. A partner can set 50 businesses a day.
- Until the owner claims it, a tap on the card goes straight to that business's Google review form: no page in between, no rating question, no filter. Kabsi counts the tap (no IP address is stored), and the partner sees taps until the claim.
- The owner claims the card by sending "Link card" and its code to Kabsi on WhatsApp, or on the card's setup page. A card set for one business links only to that business on Google. A number Kabsi doesn't know gets a sign-in link through the partner: the business gets the 30 day Pro trial and the partner earns under the partner terms (the last link before sign-up counts; a partner's own business earns nothing). After the claim, the card's taps are the business's, in "cards" and the weekly report.

### 5.4 How to write your review link onto an NFC tag

You need: a blank NTAG213 or NTAG215 card or sticker, a phone that can write NFC, and a free NFC writer app such as NFC Tools.

1. In Kabsi, open Get reviews and tap **Create my review link** (or use an existing one). Tap **Copy review link**. It looks like https://go.kabsi.co/ABC234.
2. Use the link exactly as copied. Don't add "?s=q": that ending is for the printed QR code, so Kabsi can tell taps from scans.
3. Open NFC Tools and go to **Write**.
4. Tap **Add a record**, choose **URL / URI**, paste the link and confirm.
5. Tap **Write** and hold the phone against the tag until the app confirms.
6. Test it: tap the tag with another phone, ideally one iPhone and one Android. Your Google review page should open.
7. Optional: lock the tag in NFC Tools (the **Other** tab, **Lock tag**) so nobody can rewrite it. Locking is permanent. After locking, the tag can never be changed.

Good to know:

- Opens from your tag count under that review link and as taps in the weekly report. You can switch it off in Kabsi.
- Writing Google's own review link instead works, but Kabsi can't count opens or switch it off.
- **Add a Kabsi card** is for Kabsi card codes only, not tags you wrote yourself.
- NFC doesn't work through metal.
- A review link answers about 30 opens a minute from one phone or one Wi-Fi network. Above that it asks the person to try again in a minute, so nobody can guess link codes quickly. A real customer opens it once.

### 5.5 Where to put cards and QR codes

Where people pay or wait (counter, till, reception), on tables, on receipts, invoices, bags and menus (printed QR), and in follow-up messages. Trade-specific ideas are in section 7.

### 5.6 Rules for cards and links

- Ask every customer the same way. Never offer a discount, gift, prize or upgrade for a review; Google can remove those reviews. Cards say "Leave us a Google review", never "5 stars".
- Taps and scans are opens, not reviews. Kabsi never records who tapped.
- Deleting a business stops its review links and QR codes, and any NFC card that carries them.

### 5.7 Ask a customer and the printed cards (V3, 26 Oct 2026)

**Ask a customer (WhatsApp, both plans).** Send "ask a customer" to Kabsi, or share a contact from your phone, and Kabsi sends back one ready message with your review link, as its own message so you can forward it: "Thank you for visiting {business} today. If you have a minute, tell others how your visit went: {link}" (the same words on Get reviews on the Kabsi website, under "Send your review link", UX 1). Forward it from your own phone after a real visit. Kabsi never receives, keeps or uses a customer's number or name (a shared contact is dropped before anything is stored) and never messages a customer. The message is the same for every customer: no rating question first, no reward, no filtering by how someone felt, no per-person or per-staff counts. The owner, a manager and staff can all use it.

**What Kabsi counts.** Opens of your review link, by where they came from: a forwarded message (the link in the message ends ?s=m), a printed QR code (?s=q) or a tag or the typed short link. Bots are left out and nobody is recorded. Opens are shown beside new reviews and never as a share of them: "An open is not a review."
