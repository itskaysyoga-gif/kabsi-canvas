## 10. Troubleshooting

**I have a duplicate listing.**
Do not delete a profile you think is a duplicate: deleting it can lose its reviews, and any managers linked to it. Kabsi is a Manager, so it cannot delete a profile either. Instead ask Google to mark the duplicate or merge the profiles from your Business Profile; ask Google to merge, and Google decides. Google's page "Resolve duplicate profiles & ownership issues" (support.google.com/business/answer/12756178, read on 22 Oct 2026) says merging combines the reviews, replies may be lost, and a profile flagged as a duplicate does not show on Search or Maps. For help, offer the free 20-minute call or hello@kabsi.co (section 1.3).

**I didn't get my login code.**
Check spam, check the address, tap **Resend code**. Still stuck: hand off.

**I can't find "People and access" on my Google profile.**
Kabsi works with a verified profile that you own or manage. The listing may need to be verified or claimed on Google first, and only owners can add people. You can finish the other setup steps now.

**I sent the invitation but it still says "Waiting for your invitation" (or "We'll email you when access is ready").**
**[TIME-SENSITIVE]** While Kabsi is in test mode (2.2), real businesses stay at "Waiting for Google access" until the team accepts the invitation (early access, within one working day) or Kabsi connects to Google. Check that you invited group ID 5481006796 exactly, with the Manager role, on the right business, and that you did not invite the email address hello@kabsi.co.

**I got "Kabsi can't reach {business} on Google".**
Google stopped letting Kabsi read the reviews, usually because the Kabsi group was removed as a Manager. Add it again as a Manager under People and access. Kabsi reconnects by itself within a few minutes. Until then no replies are drafted and nothing is posted. If you removed Kabsi on purpose, do nothing.

**My business isn't found in the search.**
Use the exact Google Maps name plus city, or name plus street. It must have a Google Business Profile on Maps.

**It says "Not ready: Kabsi couldn't safely verify this draft".**
The draft failed a check twice, for example it named someone who is not in your facts, gave a price or opening time you never gave, or read like a reply you already posted. Kabsi shows the draft with the problems listed, never as ready. Tap **Fix it myself** to start from it, write your own reply, ask for changes, or tap Skip.

**The email link says it has expired or was already used.**
Links work once and last 72 hours (3 days). Open Kabsi and use Reviews, Needs reply. Tapping Approve a second time, or opening the link again, does nothing new: the page shows what already happened.

**The email page asks me to sign in.**
For a high-risk review, a change to your opening hours and any change to your profile details, the page asks the person the email was sent to to sign in with their email code first, then brings them back to the same page. Normal review replies and skips work from the link alone. For every link, if someone else is signed in on that phone or computer, the page says the link was sent to someone else and does nothing: sign out, then use the link again or sign in with the address the email went to.

**Can I undo an approval from the email page?**
Yes, on the email page and in the app. After you tap Approve, the screen shows "Publishing in 10 seconds" with Undo. Undo inside the 10 seconds and nothing is sent to Google; the reply goes back to Needs reply. After the 10 seconds it says "Approved. On its way to Google." and it is too late to undo. An approval is good for 7 days: if something it waits on (Google asking for an action on your profile, or Kabsi pausing changes to Google) lasts longer, nothing is sent and the item comes back for you to approve again.

**I posted but the reply isn't on Google.**
After you approve, Kabsi waits 10 seconds, then sends the reply to Google, usually within a minute or two. Google checks every reply before it appears, usually within 10 minutes, sometimes longer. Reviews shows "Google is checking your reply" while it waits. Kabsi checks again after 10 minutes and then less often, and never posts the same reply twice. If Google does not accept the reply, the review comes back to Needs reply with the reason, so you can edit it and approve again. If Google still does not show it after 7 days, Kabsi treats it the same way. Approving the same reply twice (for example in the app and from the email) sends it once. **[TIME-SENSITIVE]** In test mode nothing is sent to Google at all.

**I approved a post, photo, holiday hours or "Keep my information" and it isn't on Google yet.**
Posts, photos, special hours and putting a detail back with Google Protection go to Google the same way as replies: Kabsi waits 10 seconds after you approve, then sends it, usually within a minute or two. Until then the post or photo shows "Sending to Google", the hours show "Sending" and the change shows "Your information is on its way to Google". Kabsi then reads your profile again and marks it "Your information is back on Google" once Google shows it and no longer lists it as its own change. Approving twice (two taps, two tabs, the app and the email) sends it once. If Google does not answer, Kabsi checks your profile instead of sending again. A post Google is still checking shows once Google allows it. If Google refuses it or still does not show it after 7 days, it shows "Not posted", "Couldn't post", "Not saved" or "Google did not take your information", and nothing is sent twice. **[TIME-SENSITIVE]** In test mode nothing is sent to Google at all.

**I'm not getting review emails.**
Check spam, and Settings, Emails: emails may be paused (**Turn emails back on**), or the daily time and time zone may be off. Reviews that need extra care arrive at once; other reviews arrive at once while you are open and in the daily email after hours, and nothing is emailed between 22:00 and 08:00. Reviews always appear in the app too, and in the bell on Home.

**My staff member gets emails but can't post.**
That's by design. Extra addresses (up to 3) see new reviews only. To let someone help in the app, invite them in Settings, Team: as a manager they can approve what you allow; as staff they can add photos and share the review link.

**Kabsi didn't prepare a post this month.**
Kabsi prepares a post only from something true: a fact you confirmed in About your business in the last 90 days, or a photo you approved in the last 30. Without one it asks "Anything new this month? A dish, a service, an event?" instead. A draft that fails a check never reaches you. Kabsi's posts also need the switch on and an active business.

**There is no Suggest phrases button.** Search phrases come back with Kabsi writing posts for you, once Kabsi's AI credit is on. Write your post in your own words meanwhile.

**My photo won't upload.** Use JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC up to 20 MB, up to 20 at a time. A file that is not really a photo, or a moving image, is refused with the reason on the Photos screen.

**Home says "Google check is late" in amber.** Kabsi has not managed to read the Google profile for more than 6 hours. The team is alerted when checks keep failing. If you removed the Kabsi group from People and access, add it again as a Manager.

**My card opens "This Kabsi card isn't set up yet".**
The card isn't linked yet. Tap through and sign in to link it, or add its code under Get reviews, **Add a Kabsi card**.

**My card opens "This review link isn't active".**
It was switched off. Switch it on under Get reviews. If the business was deleted, the card was unlinked.

**My card doesn't react when tapped.**
NFC doesn't work through metal, so don't mount it on metal. The QR code works as a backup. Otherwise hand off.

**My self-written NFC tag doesn't work.**
Rewrite it with the exact link from **Copy review link**, as a URL / URI record. Check the link is switched on in Get reviews. If the tag was locked with a wrong link, it can't be fixed: use a new tag. Tags you wrote yourself can't be added with **Add a Kabsi card**; their opens show under the review link.

**My plan shows "Paid" but hasn't started.**
Correct: a plan starts when Google access works (section 4.3).

**Google Protection says "Google did not take your information".**
Google didn't accept the change, or still did not show your version 7 days later. Update the detail on your Google profile directly, or hand off.

**Google Protection says "Kabsi's team is asking Google support".**
Google changed the same detail again after Kabsi put your approved value back twice in 30 days. Kabsi does not send it a third time; a person on the Kabsi team contacts Google Business Profile support for you.
