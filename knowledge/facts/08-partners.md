## 6. Partners

**[COMING]** Kabsi Partners, an affiliate program, pays from the day card payments open. People can join now: at kabsi.co/partners/join they sign in (WhatsApp, Google or an email code), answer five short questions (what kind of partner, country, how they will share Kabsi, the partner terms, and in the UAE an advertiser permit number or "I will only share privately") and get their partner link and QR code at once. There is no application. Kabsi reviews each partner before their first payout, and the first 20 partners Kabsi reviews are Founding Partners. The partner home is kabsi.co/partner. The rules (decided 26 Oct 2026, master plan 30.15 section 5, and 10 Nov 2026, 30.50; the partner terms at kabsi.co/partners/terms are the full text):

- Six kinds of partner: review card sellers, freelancers or small agencies, creators, consultants or accountants, POS or booking resellers, and Kabsi customers.
- A partner earns 25 percent of the subscription revenue Kabsi collects from a business they referred, not counting tax, refunds or chargebacks, for as long as that subscription stays paid, extra locations included: an extra location is paid as its own payment and earns the same 25 percent of what was collected for it. Trials earn nothing.
- Each commission waits 30 days from the payment. A refund or chargeback inside the 30 days cancels it; one after a payout comes off the next payout.
- Payouts are on the 5th of each month for every partner with 25 US dollars or more cleared; below that it rolls over. Each payout comes with a statement. USDT, USDC or PayPal.
- The partner home (kabsi.co/partner) shows the link and QR code, visits, trials, paying businesses, what was earned, what is pending in the 30 days, and the next payout date, all from Kabsi's own records. A referred business shows as its trade and area only.
- Kabsi asks how a partner wants to be paid once 50 US dollars has cleared (or when a payout is on the way). The details are kept encrypted and shown as the last 4 characters. A change of payout method is confirmed on the partner's own WhatsApp with "Yes, it's me", or, for a partner without WhatsApp, with a 6-digit code sent to their email.
- Kabsi customers have their own referral link: an owner sends "my link" to Kabsi on WhatsApp. The reward is one free month for each referred business that pays. A customer who joins Kabsi Partners earns 25 percent instead. A business never carries both an owner reward and a partner commission.
- A referral counts for 60 days from the first real action (a scan, a sign-up or a WhatsApp message with the partner's code). If a person follows more than one partner, the last one before sign-up counts; at sign-up the partner is saved on the business and never changed.
- No commission when the business is the partner's own: the owner's email, WhatsApp number or Google business matches the partner's.
- A business that comes through a partner gets a 30-day Pro trial instead of 14 days. No discount codes and no cash to customers.
- Do not quote earnings for a person and do not promise dates.
- The partner link is kabsi.co/?via=CODE, where the code is P- and 6 letters and numbers. There is no partner page per person at kabsi.co/p yet.

- Removed on 21 Oct 2026: the partner reselling model, with its wholesale prices, partner invoices, partner-covered plans, partner-set-up businesses, partner workspaces, partner approval modes, partner cards and partner emails. Never offer any of them.
- An agency or consultant that looks after a client's Google profile is invited by the owner as a Manager in Team (section 8). They see and do only what that role and the owner's approval rules allow. Kabsi has no partner who can see a business's reviews, drafts, replies, photos or reports.
- Questions about becoming a partner: kabsi.co/partners/join or hello@kabsi.co. Do not promise terms, dates or earnings.

### Partners 1: review card sellers first, and Google's review rules (10 Nov 2026, R-148)

- Kabsi Partners speaks first to sellers of NFC and QR Google review cards, then to local web and social media freelancers. The line: "The card gets the reviews. Kabsi answers them." With a partner link, every card a partner sells comes with a 30-day Kabsi trial (the same 30-day Pro trial as above), and the partner earns 25 percent of every payment for as long as the business stays. Anyone can join now at kabsi.co/partners/join; partner links pay from the day card payments open. The first 20 partners Kabsi reviews are Founding Partners.
- Kabsi works with any review card: it reads every new Google review, however it came in, and prepares a reply for the owner's yes.
- **Card links (Cards 2, 11 Nov 2026):** the partner home has "Card links": a partner makes Kabsi card links in batches (200 to start, until Kabsi raises the number), sets each card's business with the review link search (50 a day), and writes the links on the cards they sell. Kabsi sells no cards. Until the owner claims a card, a tap goes straight to the business's Google review form; the owner claims it by sending "Link card" and the code on WhatsApp, and a new business signs up with the 30 day Pro trial through the partner. The partner terms (version 2026-11-11.1) add the card rule: every customer goes straight to the review form, no page, rating question or filter in front of it, each card set only for the business it is sold to, taps never reviews, never a ranking of staff.
- A partner follows Google's review rules: no review gating or filtering (asking only happy customers), no rewards for reviews, no fake reviews. Breaking this rule ends the partnership and its payments. It is in the partner terms (section 5, updated 10 Nov 2026).
