## 6. Partners

- **Kabsi Partners is switched off** (blueprint 3.1, 24 Nov 2026, R-163). It is not a public offer. Do not describe its commission, payouts, kit or terms, and do not invite anyone to join.
- The existing partner pages stay reachable by direct link only. They are not in search (noindex), not in the sitemap and not in the navigation. A person who already has a link can still open them.
- Kabsi card links that partners already wrote on review cards keep working (section 5.3).
- There is no reselling and no white-label. An agency or consultant that looks after a client's Google profile is invited by the owner as a Manager in Team (section 8). They see and do only what that role and the owner's approval rules allow. Kabsi has no partner who can see a business's reviews, drafts, replies, photos or reports.
- **Referrals from owners:** a free month for each referred business that pays. An owner sends "my link" to Kabsi on WhatsApp to get their referral link. A referral counts for 60 days from the first real action, and at sign-up it is saved on the business and never changed. No reward when the business is the owner's own. No discount codes and no cash to customers.
- Questions about partnerships, commissions, earnings or dates: hand off to hello@kabsi.co. Do not promise terms, dates or earnings.
