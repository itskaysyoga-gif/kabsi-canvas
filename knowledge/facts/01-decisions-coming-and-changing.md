## Decisions of 21 Oct 2026: what is coming and what is changing

Final product decisions were made on 21 Oct 2026. Most are not built yet. This block says which, so the assistant never sells something that does not exist and never hides something that is ending.

**How to read the markers (assistant rule).**

- **[COMING]** means decided but not built. Say it is **not available yet**, give **no date**, and never offer it as something the person can use or buy now. If someone asks for it, say what exists today first, then that it is planned.
- **[CHANGING]** means true today and being removed or replaced. Describe what is true today. If the person is deciding whether to buy or sign up, say it is changing and that the team will confirm the current offer; never promise it will last.
- When a card is merged, the marker is removed from the line in the same change. A line with no marker is true today.
- The claims rule: never quote an industry statistic (for example a percentage of "more calls" or "ranking") as fact, and never say what Google's algorithm rewards. Say what Kabsi does.

**Review link page (live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.8-05).** Each review link either goes straight to Google (every existing link, so printed QR codes never change) or, when the owner switches that link to "Show the page" on Get reviews, opens a short Kabsi page at kabsi.co/review/{CODE}. The page shows the Google review button first and large, the same for every visitor, then one line, "Need help now? Message the manager", that opens the business's own WhatsApp (the number is a fact the owner adds in About your business, with the country code; with no number the line is hidden). It asks no question about the visit, shows no rating screen and sends nobody elsewhere. Opens are counted per link the same way as before. A business with no plan keeps its review link and QR code.

**Staff "Send review link" (live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.8-06).** On Get reviews, and under the upload on the Photos screen for the staff role, a **Send review link** button opens the staff member's own WhatsApp with this message and the business's review link: "Thanks for visiting {business}. If you would like to leave a review, here is our link: {link}". The staff member picks the customer and presses send. It is the same message for every customer (never only the happy ones), it uses the place link, and Kabsi records nothing: not who sent it, not how many were sent, no targets, no leaderboards. The message never uses the words reward, free, discount, "if you enjoyed", happy or 5 stars; a test fails if it does. The button is open to every business, with or without a plan.

**Review graphics (Pro, live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.8-07).** On the Reviews screen, a 4 or 5 star review that still has its words has **Make a graphic**. The owner picks a square (for posts and a website) or a story (for stories), sees the image, and downloads it or shares it from the phone's share sheet. It shows the stars, the review text cut to about 200 characters at a word with an ellipsis, the reviewer's first name and initial, and the business name, in Kabsi's colours and fonts. Links, phone numbers, email addresses, hashtags, handles and emoji are removed from the text; dashes and exclamation marks are softened to match Kabsi's style. A 3 star or lower review gets no graphic. Reviews in Arabic or another non-Latin script are not supported yet and the owner is told so. Kabsi posts nothing and keeps nothing: the image is made when the owner asks and is not stored, and Kabsi keeps review text for 30 days only. It is part of Pro: a business on Essentials, or with no plan, is told it is a Pro feature and nothing is made.

**Report this review (Pro, live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.8-11).** On a review in the Reviews screen, **Report this review** opens a guide for a review the owner believes breaks Google's rules. It lists Google's content categories in plain words (fake and misleading content, fake engagement, rating manipulation, impersonation, misrepresentation, harassment, hate speech, offensive content, personal information, obscenity and profanity, sexually explicit or adult content, violence and gore, off-topic, advertising, unclear or repetitive content, restricted, dangerous, child safety and terrorist content), read from Google's content policy page on 21 Oct 2026 and linked; an evidence checklist (why, the dates, what shows the person was not a customer, screenshots, related reviews; the owner keeps the evidence, Kabsi remembers only which boxes were ticked); the steps and a link to Google's Reviews Management Tool; and a place to record what happened: reported, Google answered, still showing or removed, with one appeal. Kabsi reminds the owner in the Reviews screen a week after the last update. Google decides: Kabsi cannot remove a review, does not report it for the owner, and never promises that a review will be removed or gives legal advice. Google says a report typically takes several days to look at, and that it does not step into disputes between a business and a customer, so a review the owner simply disagrees with is not a reason. No AI is used. It is part of Pro; a business on Essentials or with no plan is told so.

**Holiday hours (live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.5-03).** Five days before a public holiday Kabsi asks the owner by email, and in Kabsi under Hours, what the business will do: **normal hours**, **closed**, or **custom hours**. Kabsi never assumes holiday hours. The email has one button, "Choose your hours", and three plain links, one for each answer. The page shows what Google will show, in words, and **Confirm and save to Google** is the owner's approval: closed and custom hours become special hours for those days and go out through the normal approval, with a 10 second undo, and Kabsi checks that Google shows them. Normal hours records the answer and changes nothing on Google. Custom hours are read back before they are saved. Confirming closed or custom hours needs the owner (or a manager the owner has let approve hours) signed in; normal hours needs only the link. A moon-dependent holiday (Eid, Arafah Day, the Islamic New Year, the Prophet's birthday) says "expected" and the owner can move the days up to three either way. Ramadan is asked once, before the month starts, with two answers: normal hours or different hours for the whole month. The holidays come from each country's own official source, checked on 21 Oct 2026, for England and Wales (GOV.UK bank holidays), the United States (the federal holidays), Canada (the general holidays of the Canada Labour Code), New Zealand, Singapore and the United Arab Emirates. **Australia and Ireland** are different: their official pages could not be read, so Kabsi uses the national public days from a software package (date-holidays), checked on 22 Oct 2026, and says so in its own records; state and county holidays are not covered, and the team checks these two against the official pages when it can. WhatsApp quick replies for the same question are coming with the WhatsApp channel; until then it is email and Hours.

**Photos (live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.5-01 part A).** On Photos the owner, a manager or staff adds up to 20 photos at a time from the phone's camera or gallery: JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC (iPhone), up to 20 MB each. Kabsi checks each file in the background, usually within a minute, never while you wait: it reads what the file really is (a PDF renamed to .jpg is refused), refuses moving images, turns the photo upright, resizes it to Google's limits and saves a new copy with no hidden details at all, so the location (GPS), the phone model and the date taken never reach Google. Kabsi never draws on, generates or edits a photo beyond turning and resizing it. It then checks, in its own code, whether the photo is dark ("This photo is dark."), blurry, smaller than Google's 720 by 720 pixels, or looks like a photo already added. The owner picks Google's category (outside, inside, team, at work, product, food and drink, menu, other); Kabsi does not suggest one or look for faces, screens, flyers or other brands' logos yet, because that needs the AI model, which is off until Kabsi's AI credit is on. When photos are ready, Home and Photos show one card, for example "3 photos are ready", with **Approve all** (the photos Kabsi has no note on) or one by one below it; each approved photo goes to Google once, after the 10 second undo, through the same approval as replies. Kabsi uploads the photo itself to Google; it never gives Google a link to its storage. Videos (MP4 or MOV) are accepted and kept for 30 days; sending videos to Google comes later. Kabsi also suggests photos when something is missing: "Add photos of your business" when there are none, "Add a photo of the inside, your team and what you sell" (the kinds still missing among outside, inside, team, at work and what you sell), and "Add a new photo" when the newest one through Kabsi is 30 days old. The Profile Coach counts the photos added through Kabsi by kind. **Cover and logo (live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.5-01 part B):** on a ready photo the owner can choose "Preferred cover photo" or "Logo" instead of a category; the Cover and logo strip shows the current ones. Kabsi says "preferred" because Google decides which photo it shows first, and it gives advice, not a block: a cover photo works best wide (about 16 by 9, at least 1080 pixels across), a logo square (at least 250 by 250). **Staff upload link (live from 21 Oct 2026):** the owner or a manager makes a link on Photos (shown once, to copy); anyone with it opens kabsi.co/u/..., takes or chooses up to 20 photos and sends them, with no account; the page shows only the business name and can only upload; the photos wait for the owner's approval like any other; up to 60 a day per link; "Turn the link off" stops it at once, and making a new link turns the old one off. The WhatsApp photo drop is on both plans (30.15 section 3.4; built in V3, 26 Oct 2026).

**Post checks (live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.5-02 part A).** Before you see a post, and again when you approve it, Kabsi checks it in its own code: no phone numbers, emails, links or social handles in the text; a button opens only your own website (Call uses the phone on your profile); no prices or offers that are not in your facts or what you typed; no "best", "number one" or similar; your category or town at most twice; no health, safety or results promises; not more than 70 percent like a post of the last 90 days; no medical results for clinics; no Offer posts for hotels and other places to stay; no alcohol promotion in countries where it is not allowed. An Offer needs its dates and terms, an Event its title and dates. A post that fails says why, in plain words. Kabsi's own drafts are 150 to 300 characters and lead with one concrete thing; you may write up to 1,500. A post's photo goes to Google as a private link that works for one hour. Kabsi does not sell posts as scheduling: Google lets owners schedule posts themselves; Kabsi's part is preparing true posts each month.

**The Posts month view (live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.5-02 part B).** Posts shows this month and next. Each post shows its state (Prepared, Waiting, Published, Skipped, Not published, Being removed, or Removed from Google), its kind (Update, Offer, Event), its day and time, and who made it. When posts are prepared, one card says, for example, "2 posts for November 2026 are ready" with **Approve the month** (each post its own approval, each sent on its day; one that fails a check stays for you) or one by one: change the text, Approve, or Skip. When the month has fewer posts than you chose and none waiting, the page asks "Anything new this month? A dish, a service, an event?". **Write a post**: choose Update, Offer or Event; an Offer has a title, start and end dates, terms, and an optional code and redeem link; an Event has a title, dates and optional times; pick one of your own checked photos, a button (a link only to your own website) and, if you like, the day and time to send it (empty means right after you approve). Kabsi checks it when you save it. **Kabsi's posts**: on or off. **Undo and removing a post (live from 22 Oct 2026, P0.7-05):** after you approve a post it waits 10 seconds with **Undo**, like a reply; Undo stops it before Google has it. A post that is on Google has **Remove from Google**: you confirm once, it waits 10 seconds with Undo, then Kabsi asks Google to remove it and checks until Google no longer shows it. Removing works only on the dashboard, never by email or WhatsApp, and only for posts Kabsi published. By default Kabsi prepares one post a week (4 a month); the owner can lower it to 2 a month. Posts are prepared only from something true; otherwise Kabsi asks its one question.

**Search terms report (Pro, live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.5-05).** In Insights, Pro shows the words people searched on Google to find the business, for the last full month, as Google's Performance API reports them, with "fewer than 15" where Google gives only a threshold. It is part of Pro, built, and not marked Coming soon anywhere. Labelled "Example" figures appear only for a demo business or while Google runs in test mode. On Essentials the screen says the report is part of Pro and links to the Plan page.
