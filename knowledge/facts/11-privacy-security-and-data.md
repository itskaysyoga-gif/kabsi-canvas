## 9. Privacy, security and data

### 9.1 Who is responsible

Kabsi is operated by Rashid Abou Hamzy, who is also the person responsible for the personal data. The footer, the privacy page, the terms and every email read the operator line from one constant and say: "Kabsi is operated by Rashid Abou Hamzy. Contact: hello@kabsi.co." The privacy page and the terms say Kabsi is not partnered with Google. Card payments are processed by Creem, our merchant of record, and the privacy page lists Creem as a processor. The site gives no city and never a street address. The footer of every public page says: "Google and Google Business Profile are trademarks of Google LLC. Kabsi is independent and not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Google."

### 9.2 What Kabsi collects

- **Owners:** email and extra alert addresses; business name, address, country, time zone and Google identifiers; About your business facts; Google reviews (public name, rating, text, dates), drafts and replies; posts, photos, special hours; listing details for Google Protection; plan and payments (plan, amount and date); the authorisation you gave; a log of emails sent; a record of approvals and changes (who did what and when, from the dashboard or an email link, the country of the connection and the browser, never the IP address and never review text).
- **People who tap a card or open a link:** code, time, country, Android or iPhone, chip or QR, and a crawler flag. Never who they are or their IP address.
- **Free tool users:** the search text goes to Google's Places API; a one-way salted hash of the IP is kept one hour to stop abuse.
- **Free Google Profile Check users:** the search text goes to Google's Places API; what Google shows publicly about the business they pick is shown and not kept (only the business's Google Place ID is kept with an email or WhatsApp request); a one-way salted hash of the IP is kept one day to enforce 3 checks a day. If they ask for the check by email: the address, the business's Google Place ID, whether they ticked the tips box, the words next to it, the page and the time (proof of their choice). If they ask for it on WhatsApp (only while Kabsi has a number): a one-way hash of the number, the Place ID, the words next to the tick, the page, the time and whether the message was sent; the number itself is used once to send and is not kept.
- **[COMING] Visibility checks (from launch):** the business name, category, address and the keywords the owner confirms go to DataForSEO (Google Maps results and AI answers) and to Google's Gemini API (AI answer checks); Geoapify gets the map's centre point to draw the street map under the pins. Kept: rank at each map point, the public name, rating, review count, photo count and categories of the businesses in the results, the AI answers (shown as samples), and the competitors the owner picks. No review text, no addresses or phone numbers of other businesses and no customer personal details are kept or sent for this. Kept while the business uses Kabsi and deleted 30 days after it leaves.
- **[COMING] People who apply to Kabsi Partners (from launch):** name, email, country and city, what they do, where they will share Kabsi and how many local owners that reaches, one line on how they will recommend it, the UAE advertiser permit number if given, WhatsApp number, the time they accepted the partner terms. Payout details are kept encrypted in the database vault and shown as the last 4 characters. A partner sees the trade and area of a business that joined through them and counts of visits, scans, trials and payments, never its name, reviews or data unless the owner says yes once.
- **People who chat with Nora (the assistant):** their messages, the page they asked from, browser time zone and language, device type, the site that sent them, the country from the network (never the IP address), and any contact details they choose to give. Each chat is labelled (topic, intent, country) and a short report goes to the Kabsi team so a person can follow up. News emails only with a yes.
- **People who wrote to Kabsi's earlier WhatsApp number (before 22 Oct 2026):** they can ask for that chat to be deleted by emailing hello@kabsi.co (see section 9.5b). Kabsi does not copy chats into its own database.
- **Forms:** what you enter. **Visitors:** anonymous statistics and error reports.

### 9.3 How it's used

To provide Kabsi, to process public reviews so you can answer them, to keep Kabsi secure, and to keep payment records as the law requires. Kabsi doesn't sell or rent personal data, doesn't use it for advertising, doesn't send marketing emails without asking first, and never uses Google data to train AI models.

### 9.4 AI

Review text and business facts go to Anthropic's API for drafts and checks (photos are checked by Kabsi's own code and are not sent to an AI model); Anthropic doesn't train on this data. The owner approves every reply, post, photo and listing change. AI drafts can be wrong, so read each one; you're responsible for what you approve.

### 9.5 Processors and regions

Supabase (database, sign-in, storage; Frankfurt, Germany), Cloudflare (go.kabsi.co links and the bot check on forms), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email; EU, Ireland), Anthropic (drafts), Google (Business Profile and Places APIs), Sentry (error reports; EU, without IPs or review content), PostHog (anonymous statistics; United States), Zoho (the hello@kabsi.co mailbox), GitHub (keeps the code and runs the weekly backup, which is encrypted before it leaves the job) and Cloudflare R2 (where the encrypted backups are kept). **[COMING], from launch:** DataForSEO (Google Maps results and AI answers), Google's Gemini API (AI answer checks), Geoapify (map images), Meta for the Meta Pixel and the Conversions API (ad measurement), a speech to text provider for WhatsApp voice notes (ElevenLabs or OpenAI, named on the privacy page once chosen), and PayPal only for a partner who chooses it to be paid. The privacy page marks each of these "From launch" until the feature is switched on. Kapso and Meta are listed again when Kabsi has a WhatsApp number. Calendly and Creem are added to the list on the privacy page when they go live.

Some providers are outside the United Arab Emirates and the EU. They process data only on Kabsi's instructions and under their data protection terms, including standard contractual clauses where required.

### 9.5b WhatsApp: privacy (privacy page section 14, terms section 16)

How it works today: Kabsi has no WhatsApp number at the moment (the earlier number was retired on 22 Oct 2026). People write to hello@kabsi.co, and a person at Kabsi reads and answers it; it is not an automatic bot. Nothing is approved, published or changed over WhatsApp. Approvals happen only on kabsi.co. Kabsi never asks for a Google password or a code Google sends, by email, on WhatsApp or anywhere else. People should not send review text, passwords or codes.

What is sent: nothing goes over WhatsApp today. Kabsi puts no Google data and no review text into WhatsApp, and the assistant (Nora) reads no WhatsApp messages.

Who handles it: when Kabsi has a WhatsApp number again, Kapso (the inbox and the messaging service) and Meta (runs WhatsApp and carries the messages) are its processors, and the privacy page and terms name them before it starts. Kabsi does not use WhatsApp for marketing messages.

How long it is kept: if someone wrote to Kabsi's earlier WhatsApp number before 22 Oct 2026, no automatic deletion date was set for that chat; they can ask for it to be deleted by emailing hello@kabsi.co from their account address, and Kabsi tells them when it is done. The chat stays on the person's own phone until they delete it there.

Not live yet (P0.8-03; say "not available yet", never give a date): owner alerts and approvals on WhatsApp. When they come: alerts only after the owner opts in (the time, the phone number and the wording shown are recorded); a review alert carries the business, the star rating, the reviewer's first name, the review text up to 300 characters and the draft, after that opt-in; the owner can reply STOP (or a similar word such as UNSUBSCRIBE) at any time and Kabsi stops at once and records it. The privacy page and terms are updated, and account holders emailed, before any of it is switched on.

Likely questions and the answers: "Do you have a WhatsApp number?" Not at the moment; write to hello@kabsi.co and a person answers, not a bot. "Do you ask for my Google password or code on WhatsApp?" Never, on WhatsApp or anywhere else. "Can I approve a reply or post on WhatsApp?" Not yet; approve on kabsi.co. "Will Kabsi send me alerts on WhatsApp?" Not yet; email is the only alert channel today, and WhatsApp alerts will need the owner's opt-in and can be stopped with STOP. "How do I delete a chat I had with your old WhatsApp number?" Email hello@kabsi.co; there is no automatic deletion date, so Kabsi deletes it when asked.

### 9.6 Cookies

Sign-in storage, the last business you opened (in your browser), and the visitor's privacy choice. Kabsi uses its own small privacy bar (no outside consent tool), by country (master plan 20.4): in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and when the country is unknown) one small bar at the bottom says "Kabsi uses cookies to measure visits and improve ads. You choose." with "Accept", "Only necessary" and a "Details" link to /privacy; nothing that needs consent loads before Accept, and "Only necessary" also stops the visit statistics. Everywhere else there is no pop-up. "Your privacy choices" at the foot of every page opens one switch, "Let Kabsi measure its ads", on or off, saved the moment it moves. The answer is remembered for 12 months. PostHog counts visits without cookies or any identifier until the visitor accepts; after Accept (or the switch turned on) it keeps an anonymous identifier and may record screens on public pages (never inside the app). Each choice is stored as proof (anonymous id, the signed-in user if any, country and rule, the exact wording and its version, bar or switch, page, time; no IP, email or name). The Meta Pixel is built and runs on public pages only (never in the app, sign-up screens, staff pages, the billing return page, a business's review page or email links), and only where the choice allows ads; it sees page views, pricing, a started and a finished free scan. Sign-up, trial and plan bought go from Kabsi's servers through the Conversions API, with email and phone hashed, only for an owner who allowed ad measurement; a Click to WhatsApp ad's click id is sent with them so ads learn from paying owners. **[COMING]:** the Pixel and the Conversions API stay off until Kabsi's Meta dataset is set up (Rashid's step); until then nothing goes to Meta. Kabsi does not sell anyone's data. Vercel Speed Insights measures page speed for real visitors without cookies or identifiers. Sentry and PostHog drop email addresses, phone numbers and review text before anything is sent. The bar is in English only at launch. It opens after the page has painted (about 1.5 seconds after the page loads, or at the first touch; "Your privacy choices" opens at once), so a first visit is the page itself; the country rule and any stored choice are read as the page loads, and PostHog starts at the same moment as the banner, so nothing is counted before the visitor could have seen the banner and a visit shorter than that without a touch is not counted. Nora's launcher on public pages appears about 3 seconds after load or at the first touch. A public page's first load does not carry the sign-in client, the banner or Nora (docs/reports/lighthouse-home-2026-10-22.md). Where a sign-up came from: for the visit only (no cookie), the browser keeps the first and last campaign (utm fields), the first page and the referring site, and Kabsi saves them with the account at sign-up, on the server; an ad click id (gclid, fbclid, fbc) only where the privacy choice allows advertising. The list of everything Kabsi measures is docs/EVENTS.md. A referral link (via=CODE) sets a first-party cookie, kabsi_via, for 60 days, the newest referral wins, and the code is saved with the account and the business at sign-up (disclosed on /privacy); Kabsi Partners itself is [COMING] after launch. Usage events go to PostHog through kabsi.co (kabsi.co/ingest) with ids, counts and channels only; access, drafts, approvals, publishing, decisions, payments and new accounts are sent by Kabsi's servers from its own records, never from the browser; the demo workspace and kabsi.co test accounts send nothing. No autocapture; no screen recording inside the app.

### 9.6b Website protection

kabsi.co sends HSTS, nosniff, a strict referrer policy and a permissions policy that turns off camera, microphone and location for the pages (choosing a photo from the phone still works); a content security policy runs in report-only mode, with reports to Kabsi's error tracker, and is enforced only after 14 clean days. App pages, email action pages and API answers are never cached. A Cloudflare Turnstile check, verified on the server, protects sign-up and login by email code, both free tools (the Profile Check and the review link and QR code) and the start of a chat with Nora for a visitor who is not signed in; the free tools also allow at most 10 requests a minute from one network. Vercel's bot protection watches kabsi.co. The check is invisible unless Cloudflare needs a click, and then a box with a check box appears in the form. If the check cannot finish within about 10 seconds, the form says "We could not check your browser. Try again." with a Try again link that runs it again. Likely question: "Why do I see a check box?" It is Cloudflare's check that you are a person; it does not track you.

### 9.8 Your rights and deleting data

- You can ask to see, correct, delete, get a copy of, or object to the use of your data. Most of it works in the app; otherwise email hello@kabsi.co from your account address, and Kabsi replies within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
- **Delete a business:** Settings, Emails page, **Delete this business from Kabsi**, type the business name, **Delete in 7 days**. You get an email notice ("<Business>: leaves Kabsi on <date> unless you cancel"). Until the date, everything keeps working and you can tap **Cancel deletion**. Then Kabsi deletes reviews, drafts, posts, photos, reports and settings, deletes review links, unlinks NFC cards (they stop opening your page), and emails a confirmation. Payment records are kept where the law requires.
- Also remove the Kabsi Clients group (ID 5481006796) from People and access. To delete your login too, or if you can't sign in, email hello@kabsi.co.
- A reviewer asking about their review: hand off.
