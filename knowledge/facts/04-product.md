## 2. Product

### 2.1 Kabsi in one paragraph

Kabsi keeps a local business's Google Business Profile complete and current by following Google's own published guidance, and the owner approves every change. The owner invites the Kabsi group ID 5481006796 as a Manager on their Google Business Profile. Kabsi then shows a short list called **What needs your attention**, each item with a ready draft where Kabsi can write one. Every new Google review arrives by email with a reply already drafted in the reviewer's language, and the owner taps Review reply in the email, then Approve reply, Edit or Skip. Kabsi also drafts Google posts (one a week if the owner wants), checks photos before they go up, sets special hours for holidays, watches the listing for changes (Google Protection) and sends a factual report every Monday. Nothing is ever written to Google without the owner's explicit approval. Every business also gets a free review link and a QR code that open its Google review page. They work with the owner's own QR codes and NFC cards. Replies are one of five modules: Kabsi is not only a reply writer.

Main line (brand line, from 27 Oct 2026 on the website, master plan 30.15 section 1): "Your business on Google. Taken care of." What Kabsi is, in one line: a Google Business Profile assistant on WhatsApp. The homepage headline is "Your business on Google. Taken care of through WhatsApp." with the line "Kabsi prepares your review replies, posts and updates to your business details. You approve each one on WhatsApp before it goes live." Google's name appears only as plain words, never in a logo or graphic, and Kabsi never implies Google approves or partners with it. The older lines "Your reviews and listing. Taken care of." and "Your Google Business Profile, taken care of." are retired.

Names in the app and on the website (K-02): Reviews, Google Profile, Google Protection, Get Reviews, Weekly Care Report, Settings. The app and the website no longer show a Profile Score, a Do now list or a points value, and the email button for a new review reads "Approve reply"; Edit, Skip and Open Kabsi sit under it as plain links. Every Kabsi email has exactly one yellow button. An owner who still says "Replies", "Profile Care", "Listing Shield", "Review Link and Card" or "Monday Report" means Reviews, Google Profile, Google Protection, Get Reviews and the Weekly Care Report.

### 2.1a The five modules

Kabsi is presented as five modules. Use these exact names.

- **Reviews:** every new review arrives by email with a reply drafted in the reviewer's language. The owner approves it with the button "Approve reply".
- **Google Profile:** one screen with a row for every detail Kabsi watches (business name, main category, phone, website, address, service area, opening hours, special hours, description, open status, attributes, verification): what Google shows, what you approved, a status (Matches what you approved, Differs on Google, Needs your OK, Not read yet) and when it was last checked. A detail you have not confirmed says "Not yet confirmed by you" and Kabsi never offers to put it back. At the top, "What needs attention": each change Google made with its two choices ("Google is right", "Keep my information"; before and after stacked on a phone) and what is missing or unconfirmed. A manager decides only what the owner allows and never a name, address, main category or open status change. Editors for the description, services and attributes come later. Posts, photo checks and special hours are all approved by the owner first. Home is where the owner starts: it says "You're all caught up" or "3 things need your attention", with the first items as cards.
- **Hours (inside Google Profile):** one screen for three things. **Opening hours**: the owner sets each day of the week as open or closed, with one to three periods a day, and closing after midnight is allowed. Review shows the week in plain words, and "Confirm and save to Google" is the approval of exactly that week; Kabsi sends it after a 10 second undo window and checks that Google shows it. From then on Google Protection treats those hours as the ones the owner approved, and the history says the owner changed them. **Special days**: a holiday, a late night or a closure for chosen dates, with the same confirmation. **Closed for a while**: the first day closed and the day the business opens again; Google has no reopening date, so Kabsi sets the days in between as closed special hours. A calendar shows the special days that are set. A manager can change hours only when the owner has let managers approve profile changes.
- **Google Protection:** compares the Google profile with the details the owner confirmed and alerts the owner when they differ. The email explains the difference ("Google shows Sunday closing at 18:00. You approved 22:00.") and links to the app, where the owner chooses "Google is right" or "Keep my information". A change waiting for the owner is a card on Home and at the top of the Google Profile screen, which also shows the status line "Google Protection: on, last check 09:14". Home's "What Kabsi did this week" shows each check.
- **When Google needs action on the profile:** Kabsi reads Google's own state for the profile once a day, and at once when Google tells Kabsi something changed. When Google suspends the profile, asks the owner to verify it, sees someone else claiming it or stops letting it be managed, Kabsi stops sending anything to Google for that business, shows an urgent task "Google needs action" that says what Google is asking, and tells the Kabsi team. Approved replies and changes wait. When Google clears it, Kabsi starts sending again by itself. Only the owner can act with Google: Kabsi never files an appeal and never promises that Google reinstates a profile. A WhatsApp message for this comes with the WhatsApp channel.
- **Get Reviews:** a review link and QR code for everyone; they work with your own QR codes and NFC cards. The page shows "link activity", which is not the same as reviews: Google decides which reviews appear.
- **Weekly Care Report (live from 21 Oct 2026, P0.5-04):** an email every Monday at 08:00 local time, also kept under Insights. It starts with how many things Kabsi took care of that week, counted from the record of what Kabsi did (replies prepared, updates and photos published, changes to your profile found), then: the reviews (new, replies published, average rating), the Google Profile (checked how many times, changes found and how each was resolved, or "No important changes"), content (updates and photos published), customer activity for the week against the week before (views, calls, website clicks, direction requests; labelled as examples until Kabsi's Google connection is live), what needs you (nothing, or at most three lines), the Profile Coach's plan for the week (at most three lines), and the time saved. The time saved is an estimate and is never turned into money: 5 minutes for each reply published, 15 for each update (a post, a change to your hours or a profile detail), 10 for each batch of photos and 10 for each profile check that found something, with the working one tap away. On Pro it also shows how people found you. No review text and no customer names appear in it. Google's figures are kept with a report for 30 days and then removed from the report; Kabsi's own counts stay.

Plans are Essentials and Pro (section 4.1). Get Reviews (the review link and QR code) is free for everyone and needs no plan.

### 2.1b What needs your attention

- Everything an owner can act on is one list of tasks, made by Kabsi's own checks (never typed in by hand): a reply ready for a new review, a review that needs more care, a reply Google did not show, a change Google Protection found, two different answers for one fact in About your business, a Google post or photos ready to approve, something that did not reach Google, Kabsi losing its Manager access, the plan ending within 7 days, and things missing from the profile (Business Knowledge, phone, website, main category, opening hours, the review link, photos, a recent post).
- Each task has a priority: **Urgent** (customer-facing information is wrong, a high-risk review, a reply Google refused, something that did not reach Google, lost access, the plan ending within 2 days), **Recommended** or **Nice to have**. There is no score and no points value. Each task has a short reason and opens the page where the owner does it. A task closes by itself when the thing is done (the reply is published, the question answered, the photos posted), usually within seconds.
- Home is the Action Center. It answers one question, "Do I need to do anything?". With nothing waiting it says **You're all caught up. Kabsi is taking care of the rest.** Otherwise it says how many things need the owner ("2 things need your attention") and shows at most 3 cards, urgent first and then oldest first; **See all** shows the rest. Each card says what happened, why it matters, what Kabsi suggests, the evidence (for a changed detail: "Google shows 18:00. You approved 22:00."), a risk label where there is one, what Kabsi prepared, one main button, **Later** (3 days), the time and a **History** link with what happened to that item. A high-risk review card says Kabsi did not draft a reply. The first time Kabsi reads your reviews, all the old unanswered ones are one card ("We found 23 unanswered reviews. Replies are ready.") with the list of low-risk replies to approve together and a link to read the rest one by one. Suggestions to make the profile more complete are listed lower on Home and never turn it from "all caught up" to "needs you". Home has no score, no chart and no quick-action tiles. Below the cards: **What Kabsi did this week** (read from Kabsi's activity record, routine checks as one line a day), one line on how many people opened the review link in the last 30 days ("That is activity, not reviews"), and a link to the Weekly Care Report.
- A strip under the business name always shows the Google connection, the last successful check and the plan. Green: "Google connected, Checked 14 minutes ago". If the last successful check is more than 6 hours old the strip turns amber and reads "Google check is late, Last checked 7 hours ago", so Home never looks healthy when checks are failing. If Kabsi lost access it is red. Businesses in early access (a person posts what you approve) show "Our team looks after your profile" with no check time, because Kabsi does not read their profile from Google yet. The plan part of the strip ("Free trial, 9 days left") is shown to the owner only; a manager does not see billing.
- Kabsi keeps to Google's limits: at most 5 changes a minute to one profile (Google allows 10). If Google stops answering, Kabsi pauses its Google calls for 5 minutes and then carries on; the checks that were due wait their turn, and nothing is sent twice.
- The owner (or a manager) can **do it**, put it off with **Later** (3 days) or **Skip** it (30 days); it comes back after that if it still applies. Nothing is sent to Google until the owner approves it. Never present the list as a ranking or as a promise of more customers or reviews.
- Nora, Kabsi's assistant in the dashboard, can read the same list (most important first) and say what the top one to three are in plain words, with a link to the page. She never mentions a score. She saves a fact into About your business only when the owner has just told her that fact.

### 2.1d Team, roles and who approves

- A business on Kabsi has three roles. **Owner**: sees everything, approves everything, manages the team, the plan and billing, and can disconnect Kabsi or delete the business. **Manager**: sees everything except billing and the plan, and approves only what the owner allows. **Staff**: adds photos and shares the review link; sees nothing else and approves nothing.
- The owner manages the team in **Settings, Team**: invite someone by email as a manager or staff member (they get an email and join when they sign in with that address; the invitation lasts 14 days), change a role, remove someone, or cancel an invitation. A business always keeps at least one owner. Every role change is recorded.
- **Who can approve**, per kind (review replies, Google posts, photos, hours, profile changes): by default only the owner. The owner can turn a kind on to let managers approve it too. Changes to the business name, address, main category or open status, and reviews that need more care, always need the owner, whatever the setting.
- Approval emails (Approve reply links) go to owners, plus any extra addresses the owner added in Settings, Emails. Managers approve in the app.
- **What Kabsi tells you, and when (Settings, "What Kabsi tells you"):** every email and every notice in the app goes through one notification engine. Owners and managers choose, for each kind of notice, when the email comes (the default, at once, in the daily email, or off) and whether it shows in the **bell** on Home (the bell lists what Kabsi told you, newest first, with Mark all as read; it approves nothing). The defaults: reviews that need extra care and urgent profile changes at once; a new review at once while the business is open (by the opening hours the owner confirmed; if there are none, it counts as open) and in the daily email after hours; everything else in the daily email, sent at the daily email time set in Settings. **Quiet hours** are 22:00 to 08:00 in the business's time zone: no emails are sent then, except an urgent change to the name, address, phone, main category, hours or open status on Google; a held email goes out at 08:00. The daily email groups the day's notices by kind, with the business name first ("Yawmiyati: 2 new reviews and 1 profile change"). These always stay on and cannot be switched off: urgent profile changes, Google access notices, billing and plan notices, account notices and replies from the team. Pausing emails for 7 days (Settings, Emails) stops the ones that can be switched off. The same event is never sent twice to the same address. Staff are told nothing about reviews or profile changes.
- **What every Kabsi email looks like:** one design for all of them: a sand background, one white card with the Kabsi mark, a heading, the content, one yellow button with black text, then a footer. The subject starts with the business name and then says what happened, for example "Yawmiyati: new 5-star review, reply ready" or "Yawmiyati: a review needs a little more care"; the line shown after the subject in the inbox repeats what matters. The footer says "Nothing is published until you approve it", "Why you got this" in one line, links to **Email settings** (and to **Unsubscribe from these emails** on every email you can switch off), how to reach the team (hello@kabsi.co), the operator line ("Kabsi is operated by Rashid Abou Hamzy. Contact: hello@kabsi.co.") and that Kabsi is independent of Google. Every email also has a plain-text version. Emails you can switch off carry a one-click unsubscribe that Gmail and Apple Mail show as their own Unsubscribe button; it stops only that kind of notice, and you can turn it back on in Settings, Email settings. Kabsi never emails an address that bounced for good or marked an email as spam. Sign-in codes come from the sign-in system with the same look and carry no unsubscribe, because they cannot be switched off.
- An agency that looks after a client's profile is invited by the owner as Manager in Team, and approves only what the owner allows. There are no partner approval modes.
- Nora acts with the rights of the person signed in, never more.

### 2.1c Who can use Kabsi (eligibility)

Kabsi follows Google's Business Profile guidelines (support.google.com/business/answer/3038177), so it works only where Google does.

- **Works for:** a business that customers visit during stated hours (a shop, café, clinic, salon, garage or hotel); a service-area business that travels to customers (a plumber, cleaner or photographer) with a service area; a profile that is verified on Google and that the person owns or manages.
- **Not eligible:** online-only businesses with no in-person contact; rental or for-sale properties; PO boxes and virtual offices used as an address; other cases Google lists as not eligible.
- Kabsi cannot check verification itself, so setup asks. If the profile is not verified, the person verifies it on Google first (free, at business.google.com) and comes back. The terms let Kabsi pause the service for a profile that Google's guidelines do not allow.
- If Google's list changes, the site's `src/lib/eligibility.ts` changes first and this section follows.

### 2.1e Where things are in the app

On a computer the left menu has eight places: Home, Reviews, Get Reviews, Google Profile, Content (Posts and Photos), Insights (an overview and the Weekly Care Report), Business Knowledge and Settings. Google Profile has three tabs: Google Profile, Hours and Profile Coach. On a phone the bottom bar has five: Home, Reviews, Profile, Content and More; More opens Get Reviews, Insights, Business Knowledge and Settings, with help below. The plan is inside Settings, next to Notifications and Team, and the Weekly Care Report is inside Insights. The Insights overview says in one sentence how many customers interacted with your Google profile this month, compared with last month, with tiles for calls, website clicks, direction requests and times the profile was seen, daily charts under "See details" and, on Pro, how people found you (the search terms of the last full month). It says what changed and never why. Google's figures are read when you open the screen and are not stored. Until Kabsi's Google connection is live every figure is an example and is labelled Example. Every older address of an app page still opens the right screen. "Need a hand?" offers hello@kabsi.co (a person answers) and the guides. What a person sees follows their role: the owner sees everything; a manager sees everything except Plan and Team (billing and the team are the owner's); staff see only Photos and Get Reviews. A page that is not part of someone's role says so and offers Home.

- **Early access:** a business can start before the approval. A person on the Kabsi team accepts your Google invitation and posts what you approve, within one working day. Drafting, the safety checks and your approval work as normal, and you still approve every word. Google Protection stays off until Kabsi connects to Google. Early access is limited to 20 businesses.
- Real businesses can sign up and finish setup now. A free trial or a paid plan starts when Google access works (section 4).
- The website and the app live at kabsi.co.
- The assistant must not give a date for Google's approval. If asked: "We're waiting on Google's review and can't give a date. Your trial or plan starts when access works."
