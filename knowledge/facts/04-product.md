## 2. Product

### 2.1 Kabsi in one paragraph

Kabsi. Your business on Google. Taken care of. Your Google business profile, managed for you. Approve work and make changes straight from WhatsApp or email. Kabsi answers every review, protects the listing and its links, keeps the profile fresh, and proves the work every month. (Blueprint 3.1, 24 Nov 2026, R-163, which wins over every earlier version.) It works only through the Google Business Profile the owner shares with it: the owner adds the Kabsi group ID 5481006796 as a Manager on Google, never a password. Kabsi speaks only to the owner and the owner's staff. Kabsi never communicates with a business's customers, on any channel, and stores no customer contact details. Nothing is ever written to Google without the owner's explicit approval. Automatic replies are off, and are not offered.

**The three benefits.**

1. **Every review answered.** A reply written in your voice for every review, and an alert the moment a bad one arrives.
2. **Your listing protected.** Hours, phone, website and booking link checked every day. One tap to fix what changed.
3. **Proof of the work.** A short summary every week and a full report every month.

"Also included: holiday hours, up to 2 posts a month, photos from your phone, and tools to get more reviews." The tools are the review link, the QR code and the counter card. The owner uses them; Kabsi never asks a customer for a review.

**Who it is for.** Owner-run local businesses in English-speaking countries, the United States and the United Kingdom first. Any English-speaking business with a Google Business Profile is eligible, for example in Canada, Australia, Ireland, New Zealand or the UAE.

**How the owner works with Kabsi.** WhatsApp is the owner's working interface, not only a notice channel: the owner can ask for work and make changes in plain words or with a voice note, as far as each task is built today (section 9.5b). Email and the web are complete alternatives: an owner who chooses email can do the same work there. Spoken words and forwarded messages are never a yes: a publication needs a tap on Approve or a typed yes from the owner or a manager. Staff can send photos and words; only owners and managers approve. When something is missing Kabsi asks one question. A message that is not about the owner's Google profile or Kabsi account gets one fixed line, never a model answer.

**What an owner can send on WhatsApp (OFFER-2, 24 Nov 2026).** "help" lists these. Each change is shown back exactly before the yes, and nothing goes to Google without a tap on Approve.

- **"waiting":** a numbered list of what needs the owner: reviews to answer, items waiting for a yes, and changes Kabsi found on Google. Sending a number opens that item; "make 2 shorter" changes the draft of item 2.
- **"done":** the latest weekly summary in a few lines (new reviews and how many were answered through Kabsi, what waits for a yes, taps on the review link and cards) with a link to the full report.
- **"my hours":** the hours on the Google profile, one line a day.
- **A new usual time for one day** (for example "Saturdays 10am to 4pm" or "closed on Sundays"): one day at a time. Kabsi asks at most one question back, shows the whole week as it will be on Google, and sets it after the owner taps Approve. A date (tomorrow, 24 December) stays a change for that day only.
- **A new phone number or website:** shown back exactly as the owner typed it, set on Google after the owner taps Approve; a typed yes is not enough. While Google itself has changed the phone and that change is still open, a phone change from WhatsApp is refused and the owner is sent to kabsi.co.
- **A post in plain words:** the owner's own message becomes the post (a "please post" in front is left out), shown back for the yes.
- **"Post this" under a photo:** Kabsi drafts the post's words when its AI drafting is on; otherwise the photo goes the plain photo way (Yes, Edit or Skip). The words "Post this" are never posted.
- **"QR":** the review QR code itself, as an image in the chat, with "Print it or show it at the counter."
- **Refused from a chat:** the business name, address, category, description and services. Kabsi says these are changed on Google itself, because a wrong change can make Google check the business again, and sends the guide on kabsi.co (kabsi.co/guides/keep-your-hours-and-details-the-same-everywhere).
- **Anything else:** one fixed line, "Kabsi cannot help with that", which ends with how to reach a person: send "talk to a person" or email hello@kabsi.co.

**The five kinds of work.**

1. **Kabsi does it without being asked, no approval needed:** watches for new reviews, checks the listing, the links and the profile status, counts link and card taps, writes the weekly summary and the monthly report.
2. **Kabsi prepares it without being asked, the owner approves:** a reply to each review, a fix when the listing changed, holiday hours, up to 2 posts a month.
3. **The owner starts it on WhatsApp:** for example a reply made shorter, hours for one day, a photo for Google or a post.
4. **Kabsi explains or guides only:** business name, address and category changes from chat, services, attributes and description, offers with dates and codes, card details, and anything Google must do itself (verification, reinstatement, review removal).
5. **Never:** a message to a customer on any channel, a customer contact list, a cold WhatsApp message, a publication without the owner's yes, or general questions that are not about the owner's Google profile or Kabsi account.

**Posts.** At most 2 prepared posts a month. One comes from the calendar (an upcoming holiday, season or local date) with only facts the owner confirmed; one comes from the owner, a photo they sent or their answer to the monthly "anything new?" question. If there is no confirmed fact worth saying, Kabsi prepares nothing: no generic tips, no invented offers. Every post waits for one tap.

Name, address, map location and open or closed status are alert only, with a link to Google. ### 2.1a The five modules

Kabsi is presented as five modules. Use these exact names.

- **Reviews:** every new review reaches the owner with a reply drafted in the reviewer's language, on WhatsApp or by email, whichever the owner chose for routine messages. The owner approves it with the button "Approve reply". The whole reply, the greeting and the sign-off included, is in the language of the review: an Arabic review gets a reply in Arabic, a French review a reply in French, and the sign-off keeps the business name with the words around it in that language. A reply is the owner's own answer on their own Google profile, not Kabsi's content, so the rule that Kabsi's own website and marketing show no Arabic does not apply to it.
- **Google Profile:** one screen with a row for every detail Kabsi watches (business name, main category, phone, website, address, service area, opening hours, special hours, description, open status, attributes, verification): what Google shows, what you approved, a status (Matches what you approved, Differs on Google, Needs your OK, Not read yet) and when it was last checked. A detail you have not confirmed says "Not yet confirmed by you" and Kabsi never offers to put it back. At the top, "What needs attention": each change Google made with its two choices ("Google is right", "Keep my information"; before and after stacked on a phone) and what is missing or unconfirmed. A manager decides only what the owner allows and never a name, address, main category or open status change. Editors for the description, services and attributes come later. Posts, photo checks and special hours are all approved by the owner first. Home is where the owner starts: it says "You're all caught up" or "3 things need your attention", with the first items as cards.
- **Hours (inside Google Profile):** one screen for three things. **Opening hours**: the owner sets each day of the week as open or closed, with one to three periods a day, and closing after midnight is allowed. Review shows the week in plain words, and "Confirm and save to Google" is the approval of exactly that week; Kabsi sends it after a 10 second undo window and checks that Google shows it. From then on Google Protection treats those hours as the ones the owner approved, and the history says the owner changed them. **Special days**: a holiday, a late night or a closure for chosen dates, with the same confirmation. **Closed for a while**: the first day closed and the day the business opens again; Google has no reopening date, so Kabsi sets the days in between as closed special hours. A calendar shows the special days that are set. A manager can change hours only when the owner has let managers approve profile changes.
- **Google Protection:** compares the Google profile with the details the owner confirmed and alerts the owner when they differ. The email explains the difference ("Google shows Sunday closing at 18:00. You approved 22:00.") and links to Kabsi, where the owner chooses "Google is right" or "Keep my information". A change waiting for the owner is a card on Home and at the top of the Google Profile screen, which also shows the status line "Google Protection: on, last check 09:14". Home's "What Kabsi did this week" shows each check.
- **When Google needs action on the profile:** Kabsi reads Google's own state for the profile once a day, and at once when Google tells Kabsi something changed. When Google suspends the profile, asks the owner to verify it, sees someone else claiming it or stops letting it be managed, Kabsi stops sending anything to Google for that business, shows an urgent task "Google needs action" that says what Google is asking, and tells the Kabsi team. Approved replies and changes wait. When Google clears it, Kabsi starts sending again by itself. Only the owner can act with Google: Kabsi never files an appeal and never promises that Google reinstates a profile. A WhatsApp message for this comes with the WhatsApp channel.
- **Get Reviews:** a review link and QR code for everyone; they work with your own QR codes and NFC cards. The page shows "link activity", which is not the same as reviews: Google decides which reviews appear. **Where to put your link** (built 24 Nov 2026, PROOF-2): one link each for four places the owner already sends to every customer: Booking confirmation, Receipt, Email signature and Social bio. "Make this link" makes it, "Copy link" copies it, and each shows its taps in the last 30 days. Each is an ordinary review link, the same for everyone, with no reward and no question about the visit. Kabsi sends nothing to customers and never sees who taps.
- **Your weekly report (every Monday at 08:00 local time; it replaced the Weekly Care Report on 27 Oct 2026):** every Monday, kept under Reports, and sent on WhatsApp or by email, the same place as new reviews: 3 to 5 lines: what Kabsi did that week, changes Kabsi found on the profile, review link taps, then what waits for your yes and one next step. It is counted from the record of what Kabsi did, never guessed. No review text and no customer names appear in it. A real owner's report never shows example figures, and a block without real numbers is left out. From 24 Nov 2026 the weekly summary asks Google for no figures; Google's figures are in the monthly report (section 2.1, The monthly report). Google's figures in an older weekly report are removed after 30 days; Kabsi's own counts stay.

### 2.1d Team, roles and who approves

- A business on Kabsi has three roles. **Owner**: sees everything, approves everything, manages the team, the plan and billing, and can disconnect Kabsi or delete the business. **Manager**: sees everything except billing and the plan, and approves only what the owner allows. **Staff**: adds photos and shares the review link; sees nothing else and approves nothing.
- The owner manages the team in **Settings, Team**: invite someone by email as a manager or staff member (they get an email and join when they sign in with that address; the invitation lasts 14 days), change a role, remove someone, or cancel an invitation. A business always keeps at least one owner. Every role change is recorded.
- **Who can approve**, per kind (review replies, Google posts, photos, hours, profile changes): by default only the owner. The owner can turn a kind on to let managers approve it too. Changes to the business name, address, main category or open status, and reviews that need more care, always need the owner, whatever the setting.
- **A yes is for what was shown (Fix 2, 4 Nov 2026).** A post's Approve, on WhatsApp and on kabsi.co, is for its exact words and its exact photo. If the words or the photo change after the post was shown, that Approve no longer works: WhatsApp answers "This one is already handled. Nothing changed." and kabsi.co shows the new version to read and approve. Nothing goes to Google from the old yes. On Home, a post with a photo opens on Posts, where the photo is shown, instead of one tap.
- Approval emails (Approve reply links) go to owners, plus any extra addresses the owner added in Settings, Emails. Managers approve in the app.
- **What Kabsi tells you, and when (Settings, "What Kabsi tells you"):** every email and every notice in Kabsi goes through one notification engine. Owners and managers choose, for each kind of notice, when the email comes (the default, at once, in the daily email, or off). The defaults: reviews that need extra care and urgent profile changes at once; a new review at once while the business is open (by the opening hours the owner confirmed; if there are none, it counts as open) and in the daily email after hours; everything else in the daily email, sent at the daily email time set in Settings. **Quiet hours** are 22:00 to 08:00 in the business's time zone: no emails are sent then, except an urgent change to the name, address, phone, main category, hours or open status on Google; a held email goes out at 08:00. The daily email groups the day's notices by kind, with the business name first ("Maple Street Salon: 2 new reviews and 1 profile change"). ### 2.1c Who can use Kabsi (eligibility)

Kabsi follows Google's Business Profile guidelines (support.google.com/business/answer/3038177), so it works only where Google does.

- **Works for:** a business that customers visit during stated hours (a shop, café, clinic, salon, garage or hotel); a service-area business that travels to customers (a plumber, cleaner or photographer) with a service area; a profile that is verified on Google and that the person owns or manages.
- **Countries:** any English-speaking business with a Google Business Profile; the United States and the United Kingdom first (blueprint 3.1).
- **Not eligible:** online-only businesses with no in-person contact; rental or for-sale properties; PO boxes and virtual offices used as an address; other cases Google lists as not eligible.
- Kabsi cannot check verification itself, so setup asks. If the profile is not verified, the person verifies it on Google first (free, at business.google.com) and comes back. The terms let Kabsi pause the service for a profile that Google's guidelines do not allow.
- If Google's list changes, the site's `src/lib/eligibility.ts` changes first and this section follows.
